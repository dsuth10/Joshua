# Embedded research workflow

Use this procedure for an AFK Research ticket. Research answers a decision; it is not a general reading list.

1. State the exact decision the research must inform and the claims that need evidence.
2. Prefer primary and authoritative sources: official curriculum or standards documents, government or school policies, original research, and first-party subject guidance.
3. Search and inspect the source directly. Record the title, publisher, date or version, URL or local path, and the relevant finding in your working notes.
4. Separate source-backed facts from interpretation and recommendations. Mark uncertainty, outdated material, and jurisdictional limits.
5. Return a concise finding, the decision it supports, and any new questions it exposes. Link or attach the source to the ticket.

Never invent curriculum codes, policy requirements, research findings, or learner data. If authoritative evidence is unavailable, say what remains unknown and preserve it as a decision or assumption.
