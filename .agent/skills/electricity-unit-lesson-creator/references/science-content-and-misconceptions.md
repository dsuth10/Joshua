# Science Content and Misconceptions

## 1. Scientific Accuracy (P1 Validation)
- **Source vs Form**: Energy source and energy form must be distinguished correctly.
- **Circuit Path**: A complete circuit is represented as a continuous conducting path.
- **Diagrams**: Circuit diagrams communicate connections, not physical scale drawings.
- **Generation**: Turbine and generator roles are distinguished. Solar PV is represented as direct light-to-electrical transformation.
- **Process Chains**: Coal, hydro and nuclear process chains must be correct at the intended Year 6 level.
- **Evidence**: Source comparisons use balanced, relevant evidence and acknowledge trade-offs.
- **Simulation Limits**: Tinkercad or classroom circuits must not be described as complete power-station simulations.

## 2. Common Misconceptions
- **Unipolar Misconception**: Believing that only one wire is needed from a battery to a bulb.
- **Clashing Current**: Believing current flows from both terminals and meets at the bulb.
- **Current Consumption**: Believing current is "used up" by components so less returns to the battery.
- **Source/Form Confusion**: Referring to "coal energy" instead of coal as an energy source containing chemical potential energy.
