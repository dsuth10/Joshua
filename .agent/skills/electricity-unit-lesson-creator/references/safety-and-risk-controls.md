# Safety and Risk Controls

## 1. Safety Gates (P1 Validation)
- **Approved Circuits Only**: Only approved low-voltage classroom circuits are instructed.
- **Disconnection**: Power is disconnected before physical circuit changes.
- **No Mains**: No student is instructed to touch, test, unplug, open or repair mains equipment.
- **Checkpoints**: Component-specific risks, teacher checkpoints and pack-up instructions are present.
- **Communication**: Safety communication must tell children to keep clear and notify a responsible adult rather than investigating hazards themselves.

## 2. Risk Controls in Tasks
All practical task cards and teacher guides must explicitly state:
- When to pause for a teacher checkpoint before connecting power.
- How to safely handle components (e.g. LEDs with resistors, avoiding short circuits that cause battery heating).
