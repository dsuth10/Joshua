# Tinkercad and Practical Progression

## 1. Progression Logic
- Predict -> Build in Tinkercad -> Simulate and gather evidence -> Build or test physically -> Explain, troubleshoot and improve -> Communicate in the journal.
- Tinkercad is used as a safe place to predict and diagnose before transferring understanding to low-voltage physical components.

## 2. Practical Task Resource Family
Every practical resource must define:
- Why the task is being completed.
- Mode: Tinkercad, physical, or combined.
- Components/equipment and prerequisite setup.
- Safety rules and a teacher checkpoint.
- A prediction before simulation or power.
- Numbered steps (that do not do the scientific thinking for the student).
- Success tests and evidence to capture.
- Model boundaries (particularly for generation-system representations).
- A systematic troubleshooting routine.
- Extension by deeper reasoning rather than component quantity.
- Pack-up and inventory.
- Matching teacher solution and observation prompts.
