| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |

| **Q2** | Author's tone | **C** | Uses persuasive, urgent, and critical evaluative language ("reckless", "foolish", "irresponsible"). |
| **Q3** | Why Mars spending is a serious mistake | **B** | Funding and attention are urgently needed to solve pressing ecological and social challenges on Earth. |
| **Q4** | Alternative funding uses | **C** | Paragraph 3 cites restoring native forests, protecting wildlife, cleaning oceans, and developing affordable clean energy. |

| **Q8** | Meaning of "vital" | **B** | In context, "vital funds" means indispensable, crucial, and essential financial resources. |
| **Q9** | Why moving to Mars is foolish | **A** | Earth provides irreplaceable living systems (clean air, water, fertile soil) that cannot be replicated. |
| **Q10** | Persuasive technique in phrase | **B** | Uses emotive and evaluative wording ("unrealistic dreams") to critique colonisation advocates. |
| **Q11** | Ethical duty emphasised | **B** | Argues we have an important duty to care for and protect the living planet that supports us. |
| **Q12** | Inference about technology | **B** | Machines and sealed habitats are fragile and artificial compared to Earth's natural biosphere. |
| **Q13** | Text structure | **B** | Opens with contention, progresses through financial, environmental, and ethical arguments, closes with a call to action. |
| **Q14** | Author's viewpoint alignment | **B** | Strongly advocates investing intelligence and resources into Earth rather than space escapism. |
| **Q15** | Central overarching message | **A** | Humanity must urgently prioritise Earth's ecological health over colonising Mars. |

### Year 5 Mathematics: Division Word Problems (Red & Blue Groups, Q16–30)
| Question # | Problem Context | Calculation & Steps | Correct Answer |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Q16** | 864 seedlings shared equally across 8 reserves | $864 \div 8 = 108$ | **B (108 seedlings)** |
| **Q17** | $4,350 grant shared equally among 6 scientists | $\$4,350 \div 6 = \$725$ | **C ($725)** |
| **Q18** | 2,736 kWh solar power over 9 days | $2,736 \div 9 = 304\text{ kWh/day}$ | **B (304 kWh)** |
| **Q19** | 1,575 kg apples packed in 15 kg crates | $1,575 \div 15 = 105\text{ crates}$ | **B (105 crates)** |
| **Q20** | 348 birds housed in aviaries of 12 | $348 \div 12 = 29\text{ aviaries}$ | **C (29 aviaries)** |
| **Q21** | 1,250 saplings in trucks holding 45 (rounding up) | $1,250 \div 45 = 27\text{ rem } 35 \rightarrow 28\text{ trips}$ | **B (28 trips)** |
| **Q22** | 3,890 bottles in cartons of 24 (leftover remainder) | $3,890 \div 24 = 162\text{ rem } 2\text{ bottles}$ | **A (2 bottles)** |
| **Q23** | $6,120 shared between 18 river projects | $\$6,120 \div 18 = \$340$ | **B ($340)** |
| **Q24** | 1,848 g flour across 7 sourdough loaves | $1,848 \div 7 = 264\text{ g/loaf}$ | **B (264 g)** |
| **Q25** | 437 students on buses of 52 (rounding up) | $437 \div 52 = 8\text{ rem } 21 \rightarrow 9\text{ buses}$ | **C (9 buses)** |
| **Q26** | 1,022 plastic items collected by 14 volunteers | $1,022 \div 14 = 73\text{ items/person}$ | **C (73 pieces)** |
| **Q27** | $1,568 spent on 16 solar kits | $\$1,568 \div 16 = \$98\text{ per kit}$ | **C ($98)** |
| **Q28** | 5,000 L tank using 125 L per day | $5,000 \div 125 = 40\text{ days}$ | **B (40 days)** |
| **Q29** | 2,475 books in boxes of 12 (full boxes made) | $2,475 \div 12 = 206\text{ rem } 3 \rightarrow 206\text{ full boxes}$ | **B (206 boxes)** |
| **Q30** | 4,752 kg cardboard recycled over 11 days | $4,752 \div 11 = 432\text{ kg/day}$ | **B (432 kg)** |
