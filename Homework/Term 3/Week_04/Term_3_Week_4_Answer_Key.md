## 🔴 Red Group Detailed Solutions & Text References

### Science Reading Comprehension (Questions 1–15)
* **Q1:** *Why is electricity described as an energy carrier?*
  * **Answer:** **B. It carries energy that has been transformed from another source.**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 1 ("It carries energy that has changed from another form.").
* **Q2:** *What energy change occurs in most large generators?*
  * **Answer:** **A. Mechanical energy becomes electrical energy.**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 1 ("changes movement energy into electrical energy.").
* **Q3:** *What produces current inside the generator described?*
  * **Answer:** **C. Magnets and coils moving past one another.**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 1 ("coils of wire and magnets move past one another. This movement produces an electric current.").
* **Q4:** *What common step links coal, nuclear and geothermal stations in the text?*
  * **Answer:** **B. They all use heat to help spin a turbine.**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 2 ("heat turns water into high-pressure steam. The steam spins a turbine...").
* **Q5:** *Which trade-off is stated for nuclear generation?*
  * **Answer:** **C. It releases little carbon dioxide while operating, but its waste needs safe management.**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 2 ("Nuclear generation releases very little carbon dioxide while operating, yet radioactive waste must be managed safely.").
* **Q6:** *Why can geothermal power not be built equally well everywhere?*
  * **Answer:** **A. Suitable underground heat locations are limited.**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 2 ("suitable hot-rock locations are limited.").
* **Q7:** *How do wind and hydroelectric systems differ from many thermal stations?*
  * **Answer:** **A. They use movement to spin turbines without first making steam.**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 3 ("Other systems use movement without first making steam.").
* **Q8:** *What advantage of hydroelectric generation is mentioned?*
  * **Answer:** **B. It can respond quickly when demand rises.**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 3 ("Hydroelectric stations can respond quickly when demand rises...").
* **Q9:** *Why might tidal generation be easier to plan than wind generation?*
  * **Answer:** **A. Tides are predictable.**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 3 ("Tides are predictable...").
* **Q10:** *What makes solar photovoltaic generation different from the other methods described?*
  * **Answer:** **B. It transforms sunlight directly without a turbine or generator.**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 4 ("Solar photovoltaic panels are different because they have no turbine or generator.").
* **Q11:** *What limitation affects solar generation?*
  * **Answer:** **C. Its output falls at night and during heavy cloud.**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 4 ("generation falls at night and during heavy cloud.").
* **Q12:** *Why does the text support using a mixture of electricity sources?*
  * **Answer:** **B. One source can cover the limits of another.**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 5 ("Steady or controllable sources help when wind and sunlight are low... balance benefits and drawbacks.").
* **Q13:** *What role can batteries and pumped hydro play?*
  * **Answer:** **B. They store energy for later use.**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 5 ("Batteries and pumped hydro can store energy for later use.").
* **Q14:** *Which factor is NOT listed for engineers to compare?*
  * **Answer:** **D. The colour of turbine blades.**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 5 lists cost, reliability, location, building effects, waste, and greenhouse gases. Colour of blades is not listed.
* **Q15:** *What is the main conclusion of the text?*
  * **Answer:** **C. An electricity mix balances the benefits and drawbacks of different methods.**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 5 ("choose an electricity mix that balances benefits and drawbacks.").

## 🔢 Mathematics Workings & Solutions (Questions 16–30)

### Red & Blue Groups (Year 5 Curriculum)
* **Q16:** *Which fraction is equivalent to $3/5$?*
  * **Answer: C. $12/20$**
  * *Workings:* Multiply numerator and denominator by 4: $\frac{3 \times 4}{5 \times 4} = \frac{12}{20}$.
* **Q17:** *Simplify $14/21$ to its lowest terms.*
  * **Answer: A. $2/3$**
  * *Workings:* $\text{GCD}(14, 21) = 7$. $\frac{14 \div 7}{21 \div 7} = \frac{2}{3}$.
* **Q18:** *Complete the equivalent fraction: $5/8 = ?/24$.*
  * **Answer: C. $15/24$**
  * *Workings:* $24 \div 8 = 3$. $5 \times 3 = 15$.
* **Q19:** *Which fraction is $9/12$ written in simplest form?*
  * **Answer: B. $3/4$**
  * *Workings:* $\text{GCD}(9, 12) = 3$. $\frac{9 \div 3}{12 \div 3} = \frac{3}{4}$.
* **Q20:** *Complete the equivalent fraction: $7/10 = ?/100$.*
  * **Answer: C. $70/100$**
  * *Workings:* $100 \div 10 = 10$. $7 \times 10 = 70$.
* **Q21:** *Which fraction is NOT equivalent to $4/6$?*
  * **Answer: D. $16/20$**
  * *Workings:* $\frac{4}{6} = \frac{2}{3}$. Options A ($2/3$), B ($8/12 = 2/3$), and C ($12/18 = 2/3$) are equivalent. D ($\frac{16}{20} = \frac{4}{5}$) is not.
* **Q22:** *A recipe uses $6/8$ of a cup of oats. What is this amount in simplest form?*
  * **Answer: B. $3/4$ cup**
  * *Workings:* $\frac{6 \div 2}{8 \div 2} = \frac{3}{4}$.
* **Q23:** *What is $3/10 + 4/10$?*
  * **Answer: A. $7/10$**
  * *Workings:* $\frac{3+4}{10} = \frac{7}{10}$.
* **Q24:** *Calculate $11/12 - 5/12$ and simplify the answer.*
  * **Answer: C. $1/2$**
  * *Workings:* $\frac{11-5}{12} = \frac{6}{12}$. Simplify: $\frac{6 \div 6}{12 \div 6} = \frac{1}{2}$.
* **Q25:** *A class reads $2/7$ of a book on Monday, $3/7$ on Tuesday and $1/7$ on Wednesday. How much is read altogether?*
  * **Answer: B. $6/7$**
  * *Workings:* $\frac{2+3+1}{7} = \frac{6}{7}$.
* **Q26:** *A tank is $9/10$ full. Then $3/10$ of the tank is used. What fraction remains, in simplest form?*
  * **Answer: C. $3/5$**
  * *Workings:* $\frac{9-3}{10} = \frac{6}{10}$. Simplify: $\frac{6 \div 2}{10 \div 2} = \frac{3}{5}$.
* **Q27:** *Mia walks $5/8$ of a trail in the morning and $2/8$ in the afternoon. How much of the trail does she walk?*
  * **Answer: A. $7/8$**
  * *Workings:* $\frac{5+2}{8} = \frac{7}{8}$.
* **Q28:** *A ribbon is $13/15\text{ m}$ long. If $4/15\text{ m}$ is cut off, how much remains in simplest form?*
  * **Answer: B. $3/5\text{ m}$**
  * *Workings:* $\frac{13-4}{15} = \frac{9}{15}$. Simplify: $\frac{9 \div 3}{15 \div 3} = \frac{3}{5}\text{ m}$.
* **Q29:** *A garden uses $4/12$ of its area for flowers and $5/12$ for vegetables. What fraction is used altogether, in simplest form?*
  * **Answer: B. $3/4$**
  * *Workings:* $\frac{4+5}{12} = \frac{9}{12}$. Simplify: $\frac{9 \div 3}{12 \div 3} = \frac{3}{4}$.
* **Q30:** *A baker has $17/20$ of a tray of slices and sells $9/20$. What fraction remains, in simplest form?*
  * **Answer: B. $2/5$**
  * *Workings:* $\frac{17-9}{20} = \frac{8}{20}$. Simplify: $\frac{8 \div 4}{20 \div 4} = \frac{2}{5}$.
