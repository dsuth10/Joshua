## 🔴 Red Group Detailed Solutions & Text References

### Reading Comprehension (Questions 1–15)
* **Q1:** *Why are the Dayak referred to as the native peoples of Borneo?*
  * **Answer:** **B. They have lived on Borneo for thousands of years and developed cultures tied to its land.**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 1 ("For thousands of years, many Dayak groups have lived here... Each group has rich cultural traditions.").
* **Q2:** *Which three nations share territory on the island of Borneo?*
  * **Answer:** **A. Indonesia, Malaysia and Brunei**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 1 ("Borneo is shared by Indonesia, Malaysia and Brunei.").
* **Q3:** *Why did traditional Dayak communities build their villages along rivers?*
  * **Answer:** **B. Rivers served as the main highways for travel and trade.**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 2 ("rivers served as the main highways for travel and trade.").
* **Q4:** *What was the practical purpose of raising longhouses on tall stilts made of ironwood?*
  * **Answer:** **C. To protect inhabitants from floods and forest animals.**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 2 ("The stilts protected people from floods and wild animals.").
* **Q5:** *What communal benefit did the longhouse design provide to Dayak villages?*
  * **Answer:** **B. It allowed dozens of families to share a wide porch for meetings, daily tasks and storytelling.**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 2 ("This large porch was used for village meetings, daily tasks, music, and telling old stories.").
* **Q6:** *How does the rotational planting method (ladang) support the environment?*
  * **Answer:** **C. It leaves plots fallow after a few harvests so the soil can recover naturally.**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 3 ("After a few harvests, the plot is left alone. This allows the forest soil to rest and grow back naturally.").
* **Q7:** *Which of the following is NOT mentioned as a crop grown by Dayak farmers?*
  * **Answer:** **C. Wheat**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 3 mentions "hill rice, vegetables, fruits and herbs".
* **Q8:** *What natural forest material is gathered to weave traditional baskets?*
  * **Answer:** **A. Rattan vines**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 3 ("Skilled makers weave strong baskets from rattan vines.").
* **Q9:** *What is the sape, and what is its role in Dayak culture?*
  * **Answer:** **B. A wooden boat-shaped lute played during harvest feasts to produce gentle melodies.**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 3 ("The sape is a wooden boat-shaped lute that makes gentle, plucked notes.").
* **Q10:** *What festival is specifically mentioned as a harvest celebration?*
  * **Answer:** **C. Gawai Dayak**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 3 ("During harvest feasts like Gawai Dayak...").
* **Q11:** *According to the text, which modern commercial activities challenge Borneo's ecosystems?*
  * **Answer:** **B. Logging, mining and palm oil farms**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 4 ("Today, Dayak communities face changes from logging, mining and palm oil farms.").
* **Q12:** *How are contemporary Dayak leaders and youth responding to environmental challenges?*
  * **Answer:** **B. By combining customary community rules with modern digital map tools to record borders.**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 4 ("They combine customary community rules with modern digital map tools to record forest borders.").
* **Q13:** *What can be inferred about the relationship between Dayak culture and the rainforest?*
  * **Answer:** **B. Protecting ancestral lands is essential for preserving Dayak cultural heritage and biodiversity.**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 4 ("By protecting their homelands, the Dayak people preserve their ancient culture. They also help save one of the most important rainforests on Earth.").
* **Q14:** *In the context of the longhouse, what was the function of the private family rooms?*
  * **Answer:** **B. They housed individual families while opening onto the shared communal porch.**
  * *Reference:* Paragraph 2 ("Private family rooms opened onto a wide shared porch.").
* **Q15:** *What is the primary central idea of the text?*
  * **Answer:** **B. The Dayak peoples have deep cultural ties to Borneo's rainforests and are actively working to protect their heritage.**
  * *Reference:* Whole text synthesis.

## 🧮 Year 5 Mathematics Detailed Worked Solutions (Questions 16–30)

* **Q16: Excursion Costs & Discount**

  * Step 3 (Deduct catering order): 54 − 4 = **50 boxes (B)**
