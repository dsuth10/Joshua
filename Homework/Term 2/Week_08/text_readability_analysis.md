## 💡 Key Readability Metrics Explained

* **Flesch Reading Ease**: Scores range from 0 to 100. Higher scores indicate texts that are easier to read (e.g., 90-100 is easily understood by an average 11-year-old, whereas 0-30 is best understood by university graduates).
* **Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level**: Translates the reading ease score into an official school grade level in the United States school system (e.g., a score of 6.5 means a 6th to 7th-grade reading standard, which aligns with Australian Year 5/6).
* **Coleman-Liau & ARI**: Formulas based on characters per word and words per sentence rather than syllables. These are highly accurate for mechanical word-length calculations.
* **SMOG Index & Gunning Fog**: Calculate readability based on sentence length and the percentage of polysyllabic (three or more syllable) words.
