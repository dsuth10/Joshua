## Mandatory integrity review

For every candidate and passage:

- [ ] No omitted clause
- [ ] No repeated clause
- [ ] No invented words
- [ ] No clipping or corrupted audio
- [ ] Stable voice identity across all three passages
- [ ] No comic or caricatured “ape voice”

Any unchecked item is disqualifying.
