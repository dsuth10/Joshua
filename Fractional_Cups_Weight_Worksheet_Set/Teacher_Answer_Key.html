Finding How Many More Grams

Worked method: subtract the 1 cup already on the scales, then multiply the remaining cups by the mass of 1 cup.

Answers in order: 350 g, 240 g, 250 g, 270 g, 280 g, 450 g, 100 g, 280 g, 450 g, 330 g.
