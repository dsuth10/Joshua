SCOPE_CONTROL

The MVP & Feature Slices

A common failure in software engineering is trying to build too much at once. We avoid this by designing a Minimum Viable Product (MVP)—the simplest playable version that we can test with users.

The Dream Version (Bloated Scope)

Trying to build everything at the start often results in half-finished code and bugs:

  • 10 complex level layouts with custom boss fights
  • 6 character classes with complex skill shops
  • Multiplayer matchmaking support
  • Cinema cutscenes and narrative story systems

The MVP Version (Smart Scope)

A clean, focused slice that plays reliably, allowing quick testing and iteration:

  • 1 polished, fully playable test level
  • 1 character controller with simple movement code
  • 1 obstacle, 1 collectible item, and a scoring system
  • Clear win/lose conditions and a simple restart button

MoSCoW Prioritisation Strategy

Must Have
Core play loop. Game won't run without it.
Should Have
Important features, but not dealbreakers.
Could Have
Nice polish additions if time permits.
Won't Have Yet
Saved for Future Version 2.0 updates.

QUALITY_CONTROL

Testing & Feedback Loop

In software engineering, feedback is simply product information. We test early and often to locate critical bugs and check if players understand the game rules without instructions.

MVP playability Checklist

Quality Leads use this to audit the MVP version:

  • Does the game launch to the title screen correctly?
  • Can the player control movement smoothly?
  • Is the main objective obvious to a new tester?
  • Do scoring and state variables calculate accurately?
  • Can you hit both win and lose outcomes?

Bug Audit Template

When a crash or issue is detected, log details:

BUG_ID: #012 // COLLISION_FAIL

SYMPTOM: Player falls through level boundaries

STEPS TO REPEAT: Jump while hugging the far-left corner block

SEVERITY: HIGH // CRITICAL BLOCKER

FIXED_STATUS: UNRESOLVED (Developer assigned)

DEPLOYMENT_SHIPPING

The Studio Launch Pack

At the end of the project cycle, your team will present your complete product during the launch expo. Make sure your deliverables showcase the professionalism of your studio.

Required Deliverables checklist

  • Playable Video Game: Polished mechanics, working variables, free from blocker bugs.
  • Landing Page or Poster: Clear pitch, target audience breakdown, player controls.
  • Player Instruction Guide: Comprehensive scoring keys, tips, win rules.
  • User Testing Report: Logged bug reports, user feedback records, improvements evidence.
  • 10-Slide Pitch Deck: Concept origins, MVP breakdown, sprint board history.
  • Individual Reflection: Role logs, learning analysis, future scope decisions.

Teacher Checkpoint

Before entering the Launch Expo, you must obtain final launch clearance:

"Does your launch pack help a new player understand, run, enjoy, and successfully navigate your game without team assistance?"
