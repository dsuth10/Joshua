Purpose of assessment: To identify how legal and environmental issues in Australian communities can be managed.

Implementation: This task is designed to be completed in parts identified and as suggested in the Unit plan and Teaching and learning overview.

Conducting the assessment 

- Students are to complete assessment tasks in class time.

- Teachers or helpers can:

- read questions aloud to students

- scribe for the students

- prompt students’ responses by posing additional questions.

- Students are able to seek assistance from their teacher regarding comprehension and interpretation of sources.

Prerequisite knowledge and skills

Exposure to the following elements prior to implementation of the assessment will support student engagement with the task.

Assessment package

Materials provided as part of the assessment suite include the following:

- Assessment task (includes suggested sources to support implementation of task)

- Model response 

- *Guide to making judg**ment**s* (GTMJ)

- Teaching notes 

Part A: Describing roles

Students to complete as per instructions on the assessment task.

Part B: Sorting, recording and representing

Students to complete as per instructions on the assessment task.

Part C: Proposing action

Provide prompt questions to support the students’ selection of the most effective preparation strategy. Ask:

- *What is involved in this strategy?*

- *How will this strategy support people in an **extreme natural** event?*

- *Which strategy will be the easiest to implement across the community?*
