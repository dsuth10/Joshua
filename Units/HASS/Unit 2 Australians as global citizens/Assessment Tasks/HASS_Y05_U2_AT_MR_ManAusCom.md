**Part C: Proposing action**

- Which strategy would be most effective in preparation for an extreme natural event?

- Make it compulsory for all households to register an emergency plan with the local emergency services (police/fire and rescue). 

- Explain why this is an effective preparation strategy. 

The compulsory emergency plan is the better strategy because it means that people will know what to do if an event occurs. The early warning may reach a lot of people and give them some time to decide what to do, but an emergency plan will help people to find each other, contact each other or to have access to essential supplies after the event. It is also a low-cost strategy.

- What would you include in an emergency kit? 

An emergency kit should contain a radio, first aid kit, torch, medicines, water and some food.

- How would these items be useful in an emergency? 

A radio provides updates about the management of the extreme natural event such as road closures, plus details such as where evacuation centres are located. A radio can be used if the power is cut due to the extreme event and a torch will also be necessary if this occurs. The medicines, water and food will be needed for the people during and after the extreme event.
