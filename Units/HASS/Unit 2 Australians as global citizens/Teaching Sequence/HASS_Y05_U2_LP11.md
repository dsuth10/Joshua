## Lesson concepts

## Example learning sequence

Examine the causes, effects and experiences of people involved in the 2011 Queensland floods
- Investigate a major flood occurrence in recent Australian history, in particular the Queensland floods of 2011 (slideshow *Floods*).
- Explain why this was a significant event.
- Describe how people responded to this event (building dams, reforestation, building on high ground, changing farming methods).
Evaluate evidence about the social, economic and environmental impacts of the event
- Describe the impact of the major flood on communities.
- Classify impacts of the major flood as social, economic or environmental.
Draw conclusions about the role of the law in responding to the event
- Investigate responses to the flood event and determine how the environment and people and communities were affected.
- Examine the role of the law and government in responding to flood events.
- Draw conclusions about how government responses will help communities to be better prepared for a natural hazard.
- Consider the role of citizens in responding to a natural hazard.
