01) SYSTEM OVERVIEW

Australia is both a representative democracy and a constitutional monarchy. This means we vote for people to represent us in Parliament, and we also have a King or Queen as our formal Head of State.

The system is designed to balance power. The laws are passed by the Parliament (which has two houses: the House of Representatives and the Senate), while the Monarch is represented by the Governor-General.

SYSTEM PARTS

HEAD OF STATE: The Monarch

REPRESENTATIVE: Governor-General

PARLIAMENT: Two Houses (Bicameral)
