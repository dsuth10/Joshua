## Lesson concepts

## Example learning sequence

Explore text features within historical arguments
- Revise historical terms relating to the development of Australia as a nation.
- Explore evaluative language features (sheet *Writing arguments*):
- language of judgment (adjectives/adverbs)
- key events (nouns)
- key actions (verbs)
- evidence (quotes).
Examine how to present findings about key figures and events that shaped Australia in the past
- Joint construction of an argument which:
- follows a logical structure
- incorporates source evidence
- uses historical terms and text conventions.
Locate and collect relevant information from primary sources to draft a short persuasive argument
- Write argument using paragraph conventions, source materials and historical terms.
- *How did individuals and events contribute to the development of Australia as a nation?*

## Lesson objectives

Students will:
Understand the structure and language features of an argument about individuals and events.

Locate and collect relevant information from sources about key figures and events that shaped Australia in the past?
