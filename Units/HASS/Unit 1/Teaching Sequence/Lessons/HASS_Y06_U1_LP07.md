## Example learning sequence

Consider the role of citizens on the path to Federation
- Revise prior knowledge about colonial life.
- Consider significant individuals and groups who would have an opinion about or be affected by Federation:
- Governor-General
- merchants (trade)
- other workers (transport, communication)
- politicians
- unemployed
- migrants
- children
- Henry Parkes
- Aboriginal peoples and Torres Strait Islander peoples.
Explore the role of groups in Australia's Federation
- Evaluate groups who would argue for or against Federation.
- Identify ways that change can be encouraged.
Explore protest scenarios to understand the power of individuals and groups in Australia's history
- Discuss how individuals and groups can make a change.
- Develop appropriate slogans to argue for or against Federation.
Evaluate the significance of democracy \[referendums\] in shaping Australian society, law and government
- View or simulate a protest scene.
- Summarise the outcome and significance of the referendums and the emotional effect on the relevant individuals and groups.

Categorise the points of view of different groups as either 'for' or 'against' Australia's Federation?
