## Lesson concepts

## Example learning sequence

Sequence information to identify cause and effect connections between events leading to Federation
- Revise how to construct historical timelines (slideshow *How to construct a History timeline*).
- Construct timeline to sequence events leading to Federation (sheet *Chronology of key events of the Federation movement*).
Examine the significance of the Corowa Conference in causing change
- Interpret sources to collect information about the cause and effect of the Corowa Conference (slideshow *Corowa Conference*).
- *When, where and why was the Corowa Conference held?*
- *What was the cause and effect of the conference?*
Develop arguments about the importance of developments or events in causing change
- Discuss interconnections between Henry Parkes, Tenterfield Oration, Corowa Conference and Federation.
- Write paragraphs arguing the significance of events and developments leading to Federation.
- *Which event was most significant in causing Federation?*
- *Were individual or group achievements more significant in the development of Australia as a nation?*
- *Was Henry Parkes' Tenterfield Oration directly responsible for Federation?*
