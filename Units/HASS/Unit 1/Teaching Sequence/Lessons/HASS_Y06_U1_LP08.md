## Example learning sequence

Explore democratic and non-democratic values
- Evaluate whether specific values are democratic or non-democratic (sheet *Identifying the values and features of democratic and non-democratic systems of government*).
- Elaborate on how these values apply to students' lives.
Collaborate to evaluate the influence of key institutions on Australia's democratic system of government
- Form small groups for inquiry (sheet *Shaping a democratic government*).
- Identify questions and key terms to help you locate and collect information using ICT.
- Find and take notes from sources (slideshow *The importance of the Magna Carta and the Westminster system*).
- Present arguments for the following focus question:
- *How did British institutions such as Westminster and Magna Carta influence Australia's democratic government?*
Reflect on learning to evaluate historical skills and knowledge
- Consider use of historical skills and knowledge in relation to inquiry task.

## Lesson objectives

Students will:
Understand the importance of democracy and the Australian Constitution in establishing Australia's systems of law and government.
Understand the values of the Magna Carta and Westminster system, which helped shape Australia's parliamentary government.

---

## Evidence of learning

Can the student:
Explain the role of democracy in Australia's Federation?
Work in groups to interpret information to identify the values and institutions that shaped Australia's parliamentary government?
