## Lesson concepts

## Example learning sequence

Examine information about Sir Henry Parkes' role in Federation
- Examine sources to collect information (slideshow *Henry Parkes*)*.*
- *Who was Sir Henry Parkes?*
- *What were the significant events in his life?*
- *How did his actions influence the development of Australia as a nation?*
Sequence information about the life of Sir Henry Parkes
- Develop a timeline on which important events and developments in the life of Henry Parkes are recorded.
Analyse a political cartoon to determine origin, purpose and perspective
- Review origin, purpose and perspective of sources (sheet *Father of Federation*).
- Analyse a visual primary source to identify:
- the origin and purpose of the source
- its perspective of Sir Henry Parkes and Federation
- the contribution of Sir Henry Parkes to the development of Australia as a nation.
Evaluate evidence to draw conclusions about the significance of Parkes' contributions to Federation
- Examine written extracts from Tenterfield Oration to identify Henry Parkes' key arguments for Federation.
- Analyse effects of Tenterfield Oration.
