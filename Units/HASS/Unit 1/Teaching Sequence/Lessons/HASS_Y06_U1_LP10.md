## Lesson concepts

## Example learning sequence

Examine the roles and responsibilities of the three levels of government
- Identify the purpose of the three levels of government (slideshow *Roles and responsibilities of the three levels of government*).
- Clarify the roles and responsibilities within each level of government.
- Describe connections between government roles and students' everyday activities (slideshow *Shared roles and responsibilities within Australia's federal system of government: An everyday example*).
Identify and evaluate characteristics of 'good' representatives for democratic leadership and government
- Explore leaders in many facets of life on a local, state and federal level.
- Participate in simulated voting activities based on leadership characteristics.
- Choose leadership quality.
- Identify people who possess this quality.
- Vote to identify possible future leaders.
Work in groups to investigate the roles and responsibilities within government institutions
- Investigate issue relevant to multiple levels of government.
- Explore shared responsibilities within governments.
- Identify everyday activities.
- Choose issue connected to local, state and federal governments.
- Explain role of each level of government.
- Present findings to peers.
- Play game to evaluate knowledge of government roles and responsibilities.
