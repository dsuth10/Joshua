## Lesson concepts

## Example learning sequence

Explore the unit focus
- Examine the unit inquiry question: *How have key figures, events and values shaped Australian society, its system of government and citizenship?*
- Activate prior knowledge of (slideshow *The significance of Federation today*):
- Australian colonial history
- politics and government.
**Review Australian geography**
- Revise states, territories and capital cities of Australia.
- Locate key Australian places.
- Identify icons of Australia, including icons of states and territories.
- Discuss student connections to state and national pride.
**Develop vocabulary and question bank to guide inquiry**
- Record ideas and questions related to the unit focus.
- *How was our system of government created?*
- *How have people changed Australia's history?*
- *How have Australian values developed?*
Collect relevant information from primary and secondary sources about the significance of Federation
- Investigate sources using key terms (sheet *Examining primary and secondary sources*).
- *What is the origin of the source?*
- *What is the purpose of the source?*
- *What information can be learned from the source?*
