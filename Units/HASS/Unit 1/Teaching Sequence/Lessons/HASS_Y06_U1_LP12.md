## Lesson concepts

## Example learning sequence

Explore roles within Parliament
- Explore roles in Parliament using images or games:
- Speaker
- Clerk
- Ministers
- Sergeant-at-arms
- Governor-General.
Examine the role of parliaments in making law
- Sequence stages of the passage of a Bill in state and federal parliament (slideshow *Parliament makes law*).
Collaborate with peers to analyse the significance and continuity of legal decisions
- Form groups and delegate roles (sheet *Law-making: Debating an issue (role play)*.
- Analyse an issue for parliament for its:
- significance
- continuity/sustainability
- ethics.
- Present arguments for or against a law, recognising multiple viewpoints.
Reflect on learning to evaluate the cause and effect of changing laws
- Examine possible laws and their effect on society, the economy and the environment.
- Evaluate multiple viewpoints and the difficulty of passing ethical and fair laws for everyone.
