## Lesson concepts

## Example learning sequence

Examine the problems that emerged in colonial Australia
- Classify problems as political, social, economic or environmental.
- *What are some problems Australia faces today?*
- *Are these problems political, social, economic, environmental or a combination?*
- Sort colonial problems as political, social or economic (sheet *Problem cards*).
Identify issues leading to Australia's Federation movement
- Explore problems that emerged in the Australian colonies during the 1800s (slideshow *Problems of colonial life*).
- The wealth and power of colonies and their governments.
- Colonial defence.
- Transport and trade between colonies.
- The presence of migrant workers from China and the South Sea Islands and the perceived impact on employment.
- Colonial/social rivalry.
- Slow and costly communication.
Infer cause and effect sequences leading to Federation
- Determine how cause and effect sequences of colonial problems led to Federation (sheet *Problems of colonial life in Australia).*
- Evaluate the political, social and economic factors that caused the colonies to fight for a federated Australia.
