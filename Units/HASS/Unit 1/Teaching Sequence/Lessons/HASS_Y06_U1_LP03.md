## Lesson concepts

## Example learning sequence

Examine primary sources (political cartoons) to determine their origin and purpose
- Identify political cartoons as a type of primary source (slideshow *Political cartoons).*
- Analyse a modern political cartoon to develop understanding.
Analyse different viewpoints about Federation in the past
- Interpret a historical political cartoon (slideshow *Identifying points of view in the past*).
- Examine a political cartoon from the time of Australia's Federation (sheet *Federation cartoon*).
- Describe the visual and written features of the political cartoons.
- Discuss origin, purpose and perspectives of political cartoons about Federation.
Infer relationships between groups with similar or different points of view about Federation
- Identify points of view expressed in political cartoons as for or against Federation.
- Compare historical perspectives of Federation using sources as evidence.
