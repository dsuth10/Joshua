## Lesson concepts

## Example learning sequence

Recognise roles, rights and responsibilities in parliamentary processes
- Revise roles and processes within parliament.
Identify the effect of sustainability and ethics on parliamentary decisions
- Brainstorm issues relevant to students.
- Evaluate possible effects of introducing these laws.
- Develop criteria for analysing ethics and sustainability and their influence on laws.
- How do we ensure laws are ethical?
- How can we make sure laws are sustainable?
Work in groups to understand the roles and processes within Parliament
- Delegate roles (sheet *Parliamentary processes*).
- Choose an issue to suit given criteria.
Reflect on learning to propose viewpoints using discipline-specific terms
- Develop arguments for and against the issue.
- Role-play parliamentary processes.
- Reflect on learning in preparation for assessment.
