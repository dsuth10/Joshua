## Lesson concepts

## Example learning sequence

Examine the influence of the community and government in initiating laws
- Identify types of laws and their importance in society.
- Discuss importance of upholding laws.
Locate and interpret information from sources about the influences in initiating a new law
- Brainstorm reasons for changing or creating new laws (slideshow *From ideas to laws*):
- social
- environmental
- economic.
- Evaluate the influence of communities in creating laws.
- Interpret newspapers to decide on significant ideas, issues, events and days that could be recognised in parliament.
Examine the different viewpoints about government's decision to initiate a new law
- Explore reasons for a new law (sheet *From ideas to laws: understanding and discussing how new ideas for laws are initiated and passed through parliament*).
- Evaluate arguments for and against the new law, showing empathy to others' opinions and situations.
- Explore different perspectives of a new law.
