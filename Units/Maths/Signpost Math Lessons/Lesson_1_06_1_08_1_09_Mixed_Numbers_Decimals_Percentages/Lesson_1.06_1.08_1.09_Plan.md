# Lesson Plan: Signpost Mathematics — Lessons 1:06, 1:08 & 1:09
## Mixed Numbers, Percentages & Percentage Conversions

---

### Pedagogical Contemplation

1. **Cognitive Goal**: Students understand the proportional unity between fractions over 100, decimals, and percentages by (a) decomposing improper hundredths into whole numbers and decimal hundredths (e.g. $\frac{237}{100} = 2\frac{37}{100} = 2.37$), (b) enforcing place value zero placeholders for single-digit hundredths (e.g. $6\frac{8}{100} = 6.08$), and (c) applying 3-way equivalence ($\frac{x}{100} \leftrightarrow 0.xx \leftrightarrow xx\%$) to solve visual grid and real-world percentage problems.
2. **Interactive Alignment**: Interactive 100-grid shading simulations and step-by-step cloze conversion tables allow students to manipulate visual bar/grid lengths and immediately witness how shading 25 squares updates $\frac{25}{100}$, $0.25$, $25\%$, and uncoloured $75\%$.
3. **Surfacing Student Thinking**: Mini-whiteboard response checks ("3, 2, 1... SHOW!") surface common misconceptions—such as writing $6\frac{8}{100}$ as $6.8$ or confusing $5\%$ with $0.50$—allowing immediate teacher corrective feedback before independent worksheet practice.
4. **Pedagogical vs. Engagement Goal**: The primary pedagogical aim is mastering place value representations to hundredths and 3-way fractional/decimal/percentage equivalence. The engagement design utilizes live visual grid shading and real-world battery indicators to make abstract numerical relationships concrete.

## 2. Learning Intentions & Success Criteria

### WALT (We Are Learning To)
- Represent mixed numbers with hundredths as decimals (e.g., $2\frac{37}{100} = 2.37$).
- Understand percentage as parts per hundred ($100\% = 1$ whole, $50\% = \frac{1}{2}$, $25\% = \frac{1}{4}$).
- Convert fluently between fractions over 100, decimals, and percentages.

### WILF (What I'm Looking For)
1. **Mixed Numbers to Decimals**: I can write improper fractions over 100 as mixed numbers and decimals.
2. **Place Value Zero Placeholder**: I can use a zero placeholder for single-digit hundredths (e.g., $6\frac{8}{100} = 6.08$).
3. **Grid Percentages**: I can state the coloured and uncoloured percentage of a 100-square grid.
4. **3-Way Equivalence**: I can convert any fraction over 100 to a decimal and percentage ($43/100 = 0.43 = 43\%$).

## 5. Detailed Lesson Sequence

### Phase 1: Hook & Warm-Up (10 Mins)
- **Focus**: Visualizing the 100-square grid and decomposing hundredths.
- **Teacher Action**: Project Slide 2. Display a 10 &times; 10 grid. Ask: *"How many small squares make up 1 whole grid? If we have 2 whole grids shaded and 37 squares on a 3rd grid, how many total hundredths is that?"*
- **Student Action**: Mini-whiteboard quick response:
  - $1 \text{ whole grid} = \frac{100}{100} = 1.00 = 100\%$
  - $2 \text{ wholes} + 37 \text{ hundredths} = \frac{237}{100} = 2.37$
- **Notes Drawer Logistics**:
  - `DO`: Direct students to write total hundredths on mini-whiteboards.
  - `WORK`: Whole class discussion and individual response.
  - `RECORD`: Confirm $237/100 = 2\frac{37}{100} = 2.37$.
  - `FINISH`: 3 minutes.
  - `CHECK`: Scan room for correct representation of 237/100.

### Phase 2: Explicit Teaching — Mixed Numbers & Decimals (15 Mins)
- **Focus**: Signpost Page 1:06 concepts.
- **Step 1: Improper Fractions to Mixed Numbers & Decimals**
  - Model: $\frac{237}{100} = 2 + \frac{37}{100} = 2.37$.
  - Model: $\frac{176}{100} = 1 + \frac{76}{100} = 1.76$.
- **Step 2: Place Value Zero Placeholder Rule**
  - Model: $6\frac{8}{100}$. Explain why $6\frac{8}{100} = 6.08$ (0 tenths, 8 hundredths).
  - Contrast with $6.8 = 6\frac{80}{100}$.
- **CFU Check**: Project Slide 3 (CFU Card). Mini-whiteboard check: $5\frac{3}{100} = ?$.
- **Notes Drawer Logistics**:
  - `DO`: Emphasize the place value zero in single-digit hundredths.
  - `WORK`: Students write $5\frac{3}{100}$ on mini-whiteboards.
  - `RECORD`: Call out correct response: $5.03$ (not 5.3!).
  - `FINISH`: 4 minutes.
  - `CHECK`: Ensure zero placeholder is used by all students.

### Phase 3: Explicit Teaching — Percentages & 3-Way Equivalence (15 Mins)
- **Focus**: Signpost Pages 1:08 & 1:09 concepts.
- **Step 1: Percentages as Parts Per Hundred**
  - Explain Latin root *per centum* ("out of 100").
  - Connect to battery charge (64%) and test scores.
  - Uncoloured percentage rule: $\text{Uncoloured \%} = 100\% - \text{Coloured \%}$.
- **Step 2: 3-Way Equivalence Triad**
  - Model: $\frac{43}{100} \leftrightarrow 0.43 \leftrightarrow 43\%$.
  - Calculator / Mental Division Rule: $\text{Numerator} \div \text{Denominator}$ (e.g., $35 \div 100 = 0.35$).
- **Step 3: Interactive Simulation (Slide 6)**
  - Use live 100-grid shader widget to demonstrate 10%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 64%.
- **Notes Drawer Logistics**:
  - `DO`: Model 3-way equivalence and run interactive grid simulation.
  - `WORK`: Students predict decimal and uncoloured % before clicking presets.
  - `RECORD`: Record 64% as 0.64 and 36% uncoloured.
  - `FINISH`: 5 minutes.
  - `CHECK`: Confirm fluency across fractions, decimals, and percentages.

### Phase 4: Guided & Independent Practice (20 Mins)
- **Focus**: Completion of `Lesson_1_06_1_08_1_09_Worksheet.docx`.
- **Tasks**:
  - Section 1 (1:06): Q1-Q6 Mixed numbers, decimals, zero placeholders, grid shading.
  - Section 2 (1:08): Q1-Q3 Coloured %, uncoloured %, 3-way equivalence table.
  - Section 3 (1:09): Q1-Q4 Grid shading, conversion tables, division calculations.
- **Notes Drawer Logistics**:
  - `DO`: Hand out student worksheets and monitor progress.
  - `WORK`: Individual student worksheet completion.
  - `RECORD`: Assist Tier 2 students with place value zero placeholders.
  - `FINISH`: 20 minutes.
  - `CHECK`: Circulate and check accuracy on conversion tables.

### Phase 5: Formative Assessment & Wrap-Up (10 Mins)
- **Focus**: Exit ticket check and review.
- **Teacher Action**: Project Slide 9 (Exit Ticket Prompt).
- **Exit Ticket Question**: *"A student says that $3\frac{4}{100}$ is equal to $3.4$. Are they correct or incorrect? Explain why on your whiteboard!"*
- **Student Action**: Write response on mini-whiteboards and display on "SHOW!".
- **Notes Drawer Logistics**:
  - `DO`: Collect exit ticket responses before dismissing class.
  - `WORK`: Individual exit ticket reflection.
  - `RECORD`: Correct answer: Incorrect! $3\frac{4}{100} = 3.04$. $3.4 = 3\frac{40}{100}$.
  - `FINISH`: 3 minutes.
  - `CHECK`: Record student mastery of place value zero placeholder rule.
