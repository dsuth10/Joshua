# Lesson Plan: Addition & Subtraction of Fractions (Year 4/5 Mathematics)

**Unit:** Number & Algebra — Fractions & Decimals  
**Lesson Title:** Addition & Subtraction of Fractions (Same Denominator)  
**Textbook Mapping:** Signpost Mathematics Lessons 1:12 & 1:13  
**Curriculum Alignment:** Australian Curriculum v9 (AC9M4N05 / AC9M5N04)  
**Target Duration:** 60 Minutes  

---

## 🎯 Learning Intentions & Success Criteria

### WALT (We Are Learning To)
- Add and subtract fractions with **like denominators** using visual area grids, equal-length number lines, fraction card strategies, and word problem scenarios.

### WILF (What I'm Looking For)
1. Explain the **Golden Rule**: When adding or subtracting fractions, the denominators MUST be the same and remain unchanged in the result.
2. Add numerators while keeping the denominator constant ($\frac{a}{c} + \frac{b}{c} = \frac{a+b}{c}$) using area bar models and stacked number lines.
3. Subtract numerators while keeping the denominator constant ($\frac{a}{c} - \frac{b}{c} = \frac{a-b}{c}$) using visual takeaway grids and number line jumps back.
4. Correctly evaluate inequality comparisons ($<$ and $>$) for fraction sums and differences.
5. Solve multi-step real-world fraction word problems (e.g., sharing chocolate blocks, paper usage, money remaining).

---

## 📚 Curriculum & Pedagogical Context

- **AC9M4N05**: Solve practical problems involving addition and subtraction of fractions with the same denominator.
- **AC9M5N04**: Add and subtract fractions with the same denominator, using concrete materials, visual models, and fractional notation.
- **Signpost 1:12 Focus**: Addition of fractions with like denominators ($\frac{3}{8} + \frac{2}{8} = \frac{5}{8}$), area grid shading, fraction cards, inequality statements ($<$ and $>$), and addition word problems.
- **Signpost 1:13 Focus**: Subtraction of fractions with like denominators ($\frac{4}{5} - \frac{1}{5} = \frac{3}{5}$), visual takeaway grids, fraction card subtractions, money/chocolate word problems, and true/false fraction comparisons.

### Step 1: Warm-Up & Learning Intentions (5 Mins)
- **Slide:** Slide 1 (Title, WALT & WILF)
- **Teacher Script:** 
  > *"Class, today we are taking our fraction knowledge to the next level by learning how to ADD and SUBTRACT fractions! Look at our Golden Rule on the board: 'When we add or subtract fractions, the denominators MUST be the same.' If you have 3 eighths of a pizza and you get 2 more eighths, how many eighths do you have altogether? 5 eighths! The size of the slices doesn't change—only the count of slices!"*
- **Logistics (Notes):**
  - **DO:** Project Slide 1. Read WALT and WILF aloud with the class.
  - **WORK:** Have students open math exercise books to today's date and heading: *Addition & Subtraction of Fractions*.
  - **RECORD:** Write the Golden Rule: *"Same denominator $\rightarrow$ Add or subtract numerators only!"*
  - **CHECK:** Quick thumbs up/down check on numerator vs denominator.

---

### Step 2: Model 1 — Addition of Fractions (Signpost 1:12) (10 Mins)
- **Slide:** Slide 2 (Interactive Addition Visualiser & Bar Models)
- **Teacher Script:**
  > *"Let's look at Slide 2. We have a grid split into 8 equal parts. First, we shade $\frac{3}{8}$ in blue. Then we add $\frac{2}{8}$ more in green. Count the shaded squares: $3 + 2 = 5$ squares shaded out of 8! So $\frac{3}{8} + \frac{2}{8} = \frac{5}{8}$. Notice the denominator stayed 8 the whole time!"*
- **Interactive Tool Action:** Toggle shaded blocks for $\frac{1}{8} + \frac{3}{8}$, $\frac{2}{8} + \frac{5}{8}$, $\frac{2}{5} + \frac{1}{5}$, and $\frac{1}{5} + \frac{3}{5}$. Watch the number line jump forward live!
- **Logistics (Notes):**
  - **DO:** Demonstrate bar model shading and number line jump on Slide 2.
  - **WORK:** Students draw a 5-block rectangle, shade 2 blocks red, 1 block blue, and write $\frac{2}{5} + \frac{1}{5} = \frac{3}{5}$.
  - **RECORD:** Copy $\frac{3}{8} + \frac{2}{8} = \frac{5}{8}$ into exercise books with labelled parts (Numerators added, Denominator unchanged).
  - **CHECK:** Ask: *"Why is $\frac{1}{5} + \frac{3}{5}$ equal to $\frac{4}{5}$ and NOT $\frac{4}{10}$?"* (Because slice size hasn't changed!).

---

### Step 3: Model 2 — Subtraction of Fractions (Signpost 1:13) (10 Mins)
- **Slide:** Slide 3 (Interactive Subtraction Visualiser & Takeaway Grids)
- **Teacher Script:**
  > *"Now let's switch to subtraction! Look at Slide 3. We start with $\frac{4}{5}$ of a block shaded green. If we take away $\frac{1}{5}$, we cross out 1 green block. How many green blocks are left? 3 blocks! So $\frac{4}{5} - \frac{1}{5} = \frac{3}{5}$. Just like addition, the denominator stays 5!"*
- **Interactive Tool Action:** Use interactive controls to demonstrate $\frac{5}{8} - \frac{1}{8} = \frac{4}{8}$, $\frac{7}{8} - \frac{2}{8} = \frac{5}{8}$, and $\frac{6}{10} - \frac{3}{10} = \frac{3}{10}$. Watch number line jump backwards!
- **Logistics (Notes):**
  - **DO:** Demonstrate visual cross-out method on grid and backward jump on number line.
  - **WORK:** Have students sketch an 8-grid, shade 7 blocks, cross out 2, and write $\frac{7}{8} - \frac{2}{8} = \frac{5}{8}$.
  - **RECORD:** Write $\frac{4}{5} - \frac{1}{5} = \frac{3}{5}$ in books.
  - **CHECK:** Cold-call student: *"What is $\frac{9}{10} - \frac{4}{10}$?"* ($\frac{5}{10}$).

---

### Step 4: Model 3 — Card Strategies & Inequality Comparisons (10 Mins)
- **Slide:** Slide 4 & 5 (Fraction Strip Cards & True/False Inequality Checker)
- **Teacher Script:**
  > *"When working with tenths and eighths, visual fraction cards help us quickly calculate and compare! Look at the inequality tip: the arrow points to the smaller number, like an open mouth wanting to eat the bigger number! $10 < 30$ means 10 is less than 30. Is $\frac{6}{8} > \frac{3}{8}$ true or false? True, because 6 eighths is greater than 3 eighths!"*
- **Interactive Tool Action:** Click fraction cards to fill tenths/eighths bars. Drag and drop $<$ or $>$ symbols into true/false statements.
- **Logistics (Notes):**
  - **DO:** Model fraction card alignment and inequality comparisons.
  - **WORK:** Have students evaluate $\frac{7}{8} > \frac{4}{8}$ (True) and $\frac{5}{10} < \frac{9}{10}$ (True).
  - **RECORD:** Write down inequality rule: *"Arrow points to smaller number ($<$ means less than, $>$ means greater than)."*
  - **CHECK:** Mini-whiteboard check: Show $\frac{8}{10}$ [?] $\frac{5}{10}$ ($>$).

---

### Step 5: Model 4 — Real-World Word Problems (8 Mins)
- **Slide:** Slide 6 (Word Problem Scenarios)
- **Teacher Script:**
  > *"Maths is all around us! Let's solve two real scenarios together:*
  > *1. Sharon ate $\frac{3}{12}$ of a chocolate block and Franco ate $\frac{5}{12}$. How much did they eat altogether? $\frac{3}{12} + \frac{5}{12} = \frac{8}{12}$!*
  > *2. Ron used $\frac{4}{10}$ of his paper and Eva used $\frac{5}{10}$. How much paper is left from 1 whole block ($\frac{10}{10}$)? Used = $\frac{9}{10}$, so Left = $\frac{10}{10} - \frac{9}{10} = \frac{1}{10}$!"*
- **Interactive Tool Action:** Reveal step-by-step problem breakdowns and visual chocolate block / paper bar models.
- **Logistics (Notes):**
  - **DO:** Work through chocolate block and paper usage word problems on screen.
  - **WORK:** Students write out full equation and sentence answer in exercise books.
  - **RECORD:** Solution: Sharon & Franco ate $\frac{8}{12}$; Paper left = $\frac{1}{10}$.
  - **CHECK:** Ask: *"If Tim ate $\frac{1}{8}$ of a chocolate bar, what fraction is left?"* ($\frac{7}{8}$).

---

### Step 6: Mini-Whiteboard CFU Check (5 Mins)
- **Slide:** Slide 7 (Rapid-Fire CFU Challenge)
- **Teacher Script:**
  > *"Hold up your mini-whiteboards! I'm going to display 4 quick questions—some addition, some subtraction, some comparisons. Write your answer large and clear, and hold it up when I say SHOW!"*
- **Interactive Tool Action:** Click "Reveal Answer" for each mini-whiteboard prompt after student responses.
- **Logistics (Notes):**
  - **DO:** Display CFU slide prompts one by one.
  - **WORK:** Students solve on mini-whiteboards and hold up on command.
  - **RECORD:** Teacher monitors class accuracy (target $\ge 85\%$).
  - **CHECK:** Identify any students needing targeted support during independent practice.

Section 1 is addition grids, Section 2 is subtraction grids, Section 3 is fraction card calculations, Section 4 is true/false comparisons, and Section 5 is word problems.   - **RECORD:** Teacher circulates for individual support and extension.
  - **FINISH:** Collect worksheets or self-correct using answer key on slide.
