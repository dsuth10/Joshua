Addition & Subtraction of Fractions

💡 The Golden Rule of Fraction Arithmetic

"When we add or subtract fractions, the denominators MUST be the same. We add or subtract the numerators only!"

🎯 WALT (We Are Learning To)

  • Add fractions with the same denominator using area grids and number lines.
  • Subtract fractions with the same denominator using visual takeaway models.
  • Compare fraction expressions using < and >.

🌟 WILF (What I'm Looking For)

  • Keeping the denominator unchanged in the answer.
  • Adding or subtracting numerators accurately.
  • Solving word problems about chocolate, paper, and money!

Teacher Presentation Notes — Slide 1

DO: Project Slide 1. Read WALT and WILF aloud with the class.

WORK: Direct students to open exercise books to today's date and write the heading: Addition & Subtraction of Fractions.

Model 4: Applied Fraction Problems

🍫 Chocolate Block Sharing

Sharon ate 312 of a block of chocolate and Franco ate 512 of the same block.
How much of the block did they eat altogether?

312 + 512 = 812 of the block eaten altogether!

📄 Paper Usage & Remainder

Ron used 410 of the paper. Eva used 510.
How much paper is left from 1 whole pack (1010)?

Ron (4/10)
Eva (5/10)
?
Used: 410 + 510 = 910  |  Left: 1010 - 910 = 110
