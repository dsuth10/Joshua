# Diagnostic Assessment Quiz — Lesson 3: Adapting a Recipe for the Class

**Unit:** Maths Unit 3: Number, Algebra & Measurement  
**Year Level:** Year 5 / Year 6  
**Format:** Microsoft Forms Import Standard  

---

1. A recipe for Anzac Biscuits makes 12 biscuits. You need to make enough for 24 students. What is the Recipe Multiplier?
A. 0.5
B. 2
C. 3
D. 12
Answer: B

2. If a recipe that serves 6 people requires 3 avocados, how many avocados are needed to make enough for 24 people?
A. 6 avocados
B. 9 avocados
C. 12 avocados
D. 18 avocados
Answer: C

3. A recipe requires 1/2 cup of sugar for 6 serves. How much sugar is needed for 18 serves?
A. 1 cup
B. 1 1/2 cups
C. 2 cups
D. 2 1/2 cups
Answer: B

4. You adapt a recipe by multiplying by 4. The original recipe calls for 400g of flour. What is the total mass of flour needed in kilograms?
A. 0.16 kg
B. 1.6 kg
C. 16 kg
D. 1600 kg
Answer: B

5. A smoothie recipe calls for 250mL of milk for 4 serves. How much milk is required for 20 serves?
A. 1.0 L
B. 1.25 L
C. 1.5 L
D. 2.5 L
Answer: B

6. Which formula should you use to find the Recipe Multiplier?
A. Recipe Multiplier = Recipe Makes + People Needed
B. Recipe Multiplier = People Needed ÷ Recipe Makes
C. Recipe Multiplier = Recipe Makes ÷ People Needed
D. Recipe Multiplier = People Needed × Recipe Makes
Answer: B

7. A recipe makes 8 mini pizzas and calls for 150g of diced capsicum. If you double the recipe, how many grams of capsicum will you use?
A. 75g
B. 200g
C. 300g
D. 450g
Answer: C

8. Why might a baker choose to make 3 full batches of biscuits instead of 2.3 batches?
A. 2.3 is impossible to multiply.
B. Making 3 whole batches avoids splitting eggs or half-packages of ingredients.
C. 3 batches uses less total flour.
D. 3 batches makes exactly 24 biscuits.
Answer: B

9. Which measurement change is correct when converting to supermarket units?
A. 1250 g = 12.5 kg
B. 1500 mL = 1.5 L
C. 2400 g = 0.24 kg
D. 3000 mL = 30 L
Answer: B

10. A party guacamole recipe calls for 1/4 tsp of salt for 6 serves. How much salt is needed for 24 serves?
A. 1/2 tsp
B. 3/4 tsp
C. 1 tsp
D. 2 tsp
Answer: C

11. You are adapting a recipe for a school assembly of 120 people. The original recipe makes 12 serves and calls for 100g of butter. How much butter do you need in total?
A. 500g
B. 1 kg
C. 1.2 kg
D. 10 kg
Answer: B

12. A recipe calls for 30mL of golden syrup for 12 biscuits. For 120 biscuits, how much golden syrup is needed?
A. 300mL (0.3L)
B. 3000mL (3.0L)
C. 30mL (0.03L)
D. 300mL (3.0L)
Answer: A
