## 📊 Summary of Student Performance & Assigned Work Packages

In accordance with responsive teaching principles, **every student has a tailored work package addressing their specific missed items**. 
- **Tier 3 (Intensive Support — 17 to 20 missed items):** Recovers fundamental fraction concepts, place value placeholders, 10-frame visuals, and step-by-step Golden Rule procedures. (Length: 4 Pages)
- **Tier 2 (Core Consolidation — 11 to 15 missed items):** Focuses on place value hundredths ($6.08$), renaming $\frac{n}{n} = 1$ in mixed sums ($1\frac{7}{10} + \frac{3}{10} = 2$), and Golden Rule denominator conversions. (Length: 3 Pages)
- **Tier 1 (Extension & Peer Mastery — 2 to 9 missed items):** Features a rapid 5-minute correction warm-up targeting their 2–9 specific errors, followed by advanced multi-step capacity word problems, 3-fraction expressions, and peer coaching roles. (Length: 2 Pages)
