Clinic 1: Decimal Place Value (Tenths vs Hundredths)

Why is 68100 NOT equal to 6.8?

6.8 = 8 Tenths (6810)

8 tenths is 80 hundredths (6.80)!

80100 Shaded

6.08 = 8 Hundredths (68100)

Needs a 0 placeholder in the tenths column!

8100 Shaded
Ones (1) . Tenths (110) Hundredths (1100) Fraction Form
6 . 0 8 68100
6 . 8 0 6810 or 680100

Clinic 2: Renaming & Simplifying Mixed Sums

Evaluating: 1710 + 310

Step 1: Add Numerators
1710 + 310 = 11010
➔
Step 2: Rename 1010
1010 = 1 Whole!
➔
Step 3: Combine
1 + 1 = 2
Golden Rule of Mixed Sums: Never leave nn hanging inside a mixed number! Always swap nn for 1 whole and add it to your whole numbers!

Clinic 3: Related Denominators & Golden Rule

Calculating: 14 + 38

❌ WRONG: Adding Across

1 + 34 + 8 = 412

Breaks the Golden Rule! You cannot add different slice sizes!

✅ CORRECT: Golden Rule

Convert 14 ➔ 1 × 24 × 2 = 28

28 + 38 = 58

Golden Rule Steps: 1. Find multiplier (× 2). 2. Multiply top & bottom. 3. Add numerators!
