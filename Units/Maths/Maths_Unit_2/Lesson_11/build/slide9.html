Model 4: Addition

Cyclone Evacuation (Combining Groups)

Read the Scenario:

"Team A rescued 23 survivors from the cyclone damage. Team B rescued 18 survivors. How many survivors were rescued altogether?"

Notice the Action:

  • We have two separate parts (23 and 18).
  • We want to find the total combined amount.
  • Action: Putting two unequal groups together = Addition!
