Model 2: Multiplication

Flood Supply Trucks (Equal Groups)

Read the Scenario:

"After the flood, 4 emergency supply trucks each carried 8 food packs to the disaster zone. How many food packs arrived in total?"

Notice the Action:

  • We have 4 trucks (4 groups).
  • Each truck has exactly 8 packs (equal items per group).
  • Action: Repeated addition of equal groups = Multiplication!
