How to Draw Subtraction

Steps to Draw on your Whiteboard:

  1. Draw the Whole (The Tank): Draw a big box/cylinder and label the side "120 L".
  2. Mark the Part Taken: Partition the tank. Shade/cross out the top part and label it "74 L used".
  3. Label the Unknown Part: Put a "?" in the unshaded bottom part.
  4. Write the Math sentence: Solve for the remaining part: 120 − 74 = 46.

Student Whiteboard Check:

  • Did you start with the full amount (120)?
  • Did you clearly separate the part taken away?
  • Is the remaining part labeled "? remains"?
  • Is your final answer labelled (46 litres)?
