How to Draw Addition

Steps to Draw on your Whiteboard:

  1. Draw the Parts: Draw two separate boxes or circles side-by-side.
  2. Label the Groups: Label one "Team A: 23" and the other "Team B: 18".
  3. Draw the Action (Combine): Draw arrows from both circles leading down into a larger "Altogether" container.
  4. Write the Math sentence: Calculate the sum: 23 + 18 = 41.

Student Whiteboard Check:

  • Did you keep the two starter groups separate at first?
  • Did you draw arrows showing them combining?
  • Did you label both numbers?
  • Is your final answer labelled (41 survivors)?
