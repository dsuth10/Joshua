Converting Time & Elapsed Time

Year 5 Mathematics — Australian Curriculum v9 [AC9M5T03]

Today we will learn 12-hour and 24-hour time, and use timeline jumps to work out how long things take.

Pedagogical Context

This lesson aligns directly with Content Descriptor AC9M5T03 from the Australian Curriculum v9.

  • Goal: Understand time notation conversion and solve elapsed duration problems.
  • Hook: Ask students where they have seen 24-hour time before (e.g., flight itineraries, train timetables, phone clocks, military). Why do they think it exists?

12-Hour vs. 24-Hour Time

There are two ways to write time. Here is how they are different:

12-Hour Time

  • Uses numbers 1 to 12
  • Always needs a.m. or p.m.
  • Uses a colon — e.g. 4:30 p.m.

Midnight = 12:00 a.m.  |  Noon = 12:00 p.m.

24-Hour Time

  • Uses 4 digits (0000 to 2359)
  • No a.m. or p.m.
  • No colon — e.g. 1630

Midnight = 0000  |  Noon = 1200

Remember: p.m. → add 12 to the hour (5:15 p.m. → 1715). a.m. → keep the numbers, add a zero if needed (6:30 a.m. → 0630).

How do we write time?

12-Hour Time

  • a.m. = morning
  • p.m. = afternoon and evening

Example: 8:30 a.m. or 4:00 p.m.

24-Hour Time

  • 4 digits — no a.m. or p.m.
  • After noon, keep counting (13, 14, 15…)

Example: 0830 or 1600

Tip: For p.m. times, add 12 to the hour! (3:00 p.m. → 3 + 12 = 1500)

Explicit Instruction Points

Ensure students understand that a.m. stands for ante meridiem (before noon) and p.m. stands for post meridiem (after noon).

Demonstrate the Noon and Midnight edge cases on the board. Midnight (12:00 a.m.) is 0000; 12:01 a.m. is 0001; 12:59 a.m. is 0059. Noon (12:00 p.m.) is 1200; 12:01 p.m. is 1201.

Have students practice saying 24-hour times out loud (e.g. 1430 is "fourteen thirty hours" or "fourteen thirty").

Key Concept Review

Ask: "If the time was 11:05 a.m. (morning), what would the 24-hour time be?" (1105). Compare it with 2305 to show the stark difference.

Interactive Quiz: 24h to 12h

Convert 0045 hours to 12-hour time.
Explanation:

Correct! 00 means midnight hour. Change 00 to 12 and add a.m.: 12:45 a.m.

Terrific! 00 at the start means midnight hour. It is 12:45 a.m.

Important Edge Case

Emphasise this slide! Midnight is a notorious confusion point. Explain that 0000 is 12:00 a.m. on the dot. Therefore, 0045 is 45 minutes past midnight, or 12:45 a.m.

Interactive Quiz: 12h to 24h

Convert 12:15 p.m. (Noon) to 24-hour time.
Explanation:

Correct! At noon, keep the hour as 12. Remove the colon and p.m.: 1215. Do not add 12!

Superb! Noon stays as 12. So 12:15 p.m. becomes 1215.

Noon Exception

Explain that because 12:00 p.m. is the dividing line (noon), adding 12 would result in 2400, which is incorrect. Noon hours (12:00 p.m. to 12:59 p.m.) simply have their colons and labels removed, keeping the 12.

Timeline Jump Strategy

Instead of subtracting, we use a timeline and make three jumps.

Example: A train leaves Brisbane at 1:30 a.m. and arrives in Cairns at 10:30 p.m. How long is the trip?
3 Steps:
  1. Jump to next hour: 1:30 a.m. → 2:00 a.m. (+30 min)
  2. Jump the hours: 2:00 a.m. → 10:00 p.m. (+20 hours)
  3. Jump the minutes: 10:00 p.m. → 10:30 p.m. (+30 min)
Total: 20 h + 30 min + 30 min = 21 hours

How to explain the jumps

Explain that breaking the problem into three simple parts (minutes to nearest hour, full hours, remaining minutes) keeps computations incredibly simple and avoids the 'crossing midday' subtraction errors.

Walk through the SVG arrows on the timeline carefully, matching them to each step on the left.

Timeline Jumps: Example 2

Example: A plane leaves Perth at 10:45 a.m. and lands in Sydney at 7:15 p.m. How long was the flight?
3 Steps:
  1. Jump to next hour: 10:45 a.m. → 11:00 a.m. (+15 min)
  2. Jump the hours: 11:00 a.m. → 7:00 p.m. (+8 hours)
  3. Jump the minutes: 7:00 p.m. → 7:15 p.m. (+15 min)
Total: 8 h + 15 min + 15 min = 8 hours 30 minutes

Guided Practice Notes

Point out how 7:15 p.m. corresponds to 1915 hours on the timeline. Encourage students to notice that having both systems visible side-by-side helps confirm their time conversions instantly.

Whiteboard Practice 2: Barrier Reef Cruise

A reef cruise leaves Cairns at 9:30 a.m. and returns at 4:20 p.m. How long was the cruise?
Draw your jumps, then click Reveal Solution Jumps to check.
Jump to 10:00 a.m. (+30m), then to 4:00 p.m. (+6h), then to 4:20 p.m. (+20m).

Guided Practice Notes

Review the calculation: 30 minutes + 6 hours + 20 minutes. Since the minutes add up to 50, which is less than 60, we don't need to convert any minutes to hours. The duration is simply 6 hours 50 minutes.

Whiteboard Practice 3: Sydney to Hobart Leg

A yacht leaves Sydney at 11:45 a.m. Monday and reaches Eden at 5:15 p.m. Tuesday. How long did this leg take?
Tip: This crosses midnight — think about jumping a full day.
Jump to noon Monday (+15m), jump 24 hours to noon Tuesday, then to 5:00 p.m. (+5h), then to 5:15 p.m. (+15m).

Overnight Challenge Explanation

This is a major extension point. Explain that Tuesday 5:00 p.m. is 29 hours after Monday 12:00 noon.

Let's break down 29 hours: Monday 12:00 noon to Tuesday 12:00 noon is exactly 24 hours (1 full day). Then from Tuesday 12:00 noon to Tuesday 5:00 p.m. is another 5 hours. 24 + 5 = 29 hours.

Whiteboard Practice 4: Indian Pacific

A train leaves Adelaide at 1430 Monday and reaches Cook at 1915 Tuesday. How long is the trip?
Work this out on your handout. Write your answer in hours and minutes.

Teacher Guide & Answer Key

Encourage students to use their worksheets to solve this independently. Have a volunteer present their jumps on the board.

Step-by-step Solution:

  • Start time: Monday 1430 (2:30 p.m. Monday)
  • End time: Tuesday 1915 (7:15 p.m. Tuesday)
  • Jump 1: 1430 to 1500 (3:00 p.m. Monday) → 30 minutes
  • Jump 2: 1500 Monday to 1900 Tuesday → 28 hours (Monday 3 p.m. to Tuesday 3 p.m. is 24 hours. Tuesday 3 p.m. to Tuesday 7 p.m. is 4 hours. 24 + 4 = 28 hours)
  • Jump 3: 1900 to 1915 Tuesday → 15 minutes
  • Total: 28 hours + 30 mins + 15 mins = 28 hours 45 minutes

Whiteboard Practice 5: The Ghan

The Ghan leaves Darwin at 0615 and arrives at Katherine at 2245. How long was the journey?
Work this out on your handout or mini-whiteboard. Draw jumps on the timeline.

Teacher Guide & Answer Key

Step-by-step Solution:

  • Start time: 0615 (6:15 a.m.)
  • End time: 2245 (10:45 p.m.)
  • Jump 1: 0615 to 0700 → 45 minutes
  • Jump 2: 0700 to 2200 → 15 hours (22 - 7 = 15)
  • Jump 3: 2200 to 2245 → 45 minutes
  • Total: 15 hours + 45 mins + 45 mins = 15 hours + 90 mins = 16 hours 30 minutes
  • Alternative direct method: 2245 - 0615 = 16 hours 30 minutes (since minutes subtraction 45 - 15 is positive, vertical subtraction works easily here. Show this as an alternative shortcut!).

Lesson Summary

What We Learned:

  • 12-hour time uses a.m./p.m.  |  24-hour time uses 4 digits (no colons, no letters)
  • Noon/Midnight: 12:00 p.m. = 1200  |  12:00 a.m. = 0000
  • Timeline jumps: (1) minutes to next hour → (2) jump the hours → (3) jump the minutes
