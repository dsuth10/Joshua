# Year 5 poetry sequence across 40 lessons

Confirmed timetable: 10 weeks, four 60-minute sessions per week. Assessment remains in Week 7. The text order follows the current proposal; assessment conditions and class-specific adjustments remain unresolved.

## Timing and resource status

For a standard teaching lesson use 5 minutes retrieval/fluency, 10 minutes vocabulary and reading orientation, 15 minutes explicit modelling, 15 minutes guided practice, 10 minutes independent response and 5 minutes checking: 60 minutes total. Adapt this balance for diagnostic, rehearsal and reflection lessons. Assessment sessions follow the actual instrument rather than this template.

Week 7 contains four timetable slots, not four hours of assessment. Lessons 26-27 are provisional reservations; move the assessment within Week 7 or release the contingency slot once its duration and conditions are known. Essential assessed skills are taught by Lesson 24.

Core poems have checked source links in [poem notes](../Research/Poem_Source_Notes.md), but prepared classroom editions, excerpts, practice poems, response models and organisers are still to be selected or created. Resource entries below are preparation requirements, not claims that worksheets exist. The assessment poem remains reserved.
