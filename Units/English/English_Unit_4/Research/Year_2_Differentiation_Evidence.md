## Teaching and evidence

Keep access to the same poems through teacher reading and brief shared discussion. For independent reading, provide a short companion text matched to demonstrated phonics and word knowledge; classic poem extracts are not automatically Year 2 decodable texts. Label teacher-created adaptations clearly.

Prioritise literal understanding and simple evidence-based inference, characters/settings, describing words, reasons for preferences, rhythmic patterns and rereading for meaning. Use pictures to support vocabulary and comprehension; teach decoding through sound-letter knowledge rather than guessing words from pictures.

Record separately: independent word reading; understanding after teacher read-aloud; oral or drawn responses; prompted versus independent performance. Do not equate an adult-read task with independent reading.

Week 7: retain the class assessment week. Select an appropriate short unfamiliar text and response route only after the teacher confirms the individual assessment/curriculum plan. Adapt teaching now; do not substitute Year 2 criteria for Year 5 grading without that decision.
