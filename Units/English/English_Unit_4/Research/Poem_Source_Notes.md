# Poem source notes

Checked 11 September 2026. These are teacher preparation sources. Use a consistent classroom edition so students can locate evidence reliably. The instructional suggestions below are planning judgements.

| Poem | Source checked | Planning use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| My Country | [Official Dorothea Mackellar site](https://www.dorotheamackellar.com.au/my-country/) | Teach contrast, imagery and repetition across the whole poem, rather than only its best-known stanza. |
| Mulga Bill's Bicycle | [Rio Grande's Last Race and Other Verses, Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/304/304-h/304-h.htm) | Establish the difference between the character's confidence and what happens; connect rhythmic reading with narrative understanding. |
| The Man from Ironbark | [The Man from Snowy River and Other Verses, Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/213/pg213-images.html) | Contrast what the reader knows with what the character believes. Preview the frightening barber prank, violence and language before choosing classroom excerpts. |
| Clancy of the Overflow | [Same Paterson collection](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/213/pg213-images.html) | Investigate the speaker's imagined country life and city dissatisfaction as a viewpoint. |
| The Man from Snowy River | [Same Paterson collection](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/213/pg213-images.html) | Use whole-story orientation followed by manageable close-reading sections. |

## Classroom preparation
- Number stanzas/lines against the chosen edition; do not invent line references across editions.
- Introduce unfamiliar words and historical setting as needed for meaning, then return to the actual poem.
- Treat these representations as particular perspectives; do not present them as the only Australian experiences or viewpoints.
- Select short unfamiliar practice poems separately and keep the assessment poem reserved. Include a non-rhyming contrast so students do not conclude that rhyme defines poetry.
- Teacher-made prose paraphrases can support comprehension and poetry/prose comparison, but retain the original beside them so the paraphrase does not replace analysis.
