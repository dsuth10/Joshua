# Year 1 & 2 English Teaching Sequence: Australian Animals (Term 2)

**Unit Focus**: Examining, creating and sharing informative texts.
**Year Levels**: Year 1 & Year 2 (Composite).
**Duration**: 10 Weeks (40 Lessons).
