# Year 5 English — Unit 2: Informative Texts
## Sequence 2, Lesson 10 — Floods: Expanded Noun Groups

**Time:** 45 minutes
**Core Text:** Floods Archive — Brisbane River System sub-page; Brisbane History sub-page
**Learning Intention:** I can explain how expanded noun groups provide a fuller description of a person, place, thing or idea. (AC9E5LA06)

---

### Differentiation Key

- **Year 5:** Core Year 5 pathway — assessed against Year 5 achievement standard. Focus: complex expansions including prepositional phrases and embedded clauses.
- **Lucas (Y2):** Differentiated pathway — assessed against Year 2 achievement standard. Focus: simple expansions using articles and adjectives. (AC9E2LA07)

---

### Lesson Phases

#### 1. Activate (10 mins)
- **Teacher Action:** Display the following three phrases on the board:
  - "water"
  - "the murky floodwater"
  - "the rapidly rising, sediment-laden floodwaters of the Bremer River."
- **Discussion:** Ask students to Turn and Talk: What changes between these three phrases? What is the core noun? What words are doing the describing? Why would an author choose the longest version over the shortest?
- **Differentiation (Lucas):** Provide a whiteboard marker and ask him to circle the word "water" in each phrase. Ask him to draw what he pictures for phrase 1 vs phrase 2.

#### 2. Explore (15 mins)
- **Teacher Action:** Introduce the concept of **Expanded Noun Groups**. Explain the formula:
  - **Article** (The/A/An) + **Pre-modifier** (Adjectives) + **Noun** (The core thing) + **Post-modifier** (Prepositional phrase or embedded clause).
- **Modelling:** Display an excerpt from the Floods Archive (Brisbane History sub-page). Think aloud to locate a noun group.
  - *Example:* "the devastating 1974 flood event that reshaped Brisbane".
  - *Unpack:* Article ("the") + Pre-modifiers ("devastating 1974") + Noun ("flood event") + Post-modifier embedded clause ("that reshaped Brisbane").
- **Differentiation (Lucas):** Work with SSO. Introduce a simplified formula: Article + Adjective + Noun. (e.g., "the muddy river").

#### 3. Model (5 mins)
- **Teacher Action:** Jointly unpack a second expanded noun group from the Brisbane River System sub-page.
- **Activity:** Write the noun "rain" on the board. Build an expansion step-by-step with the class:
  - Add an article: *the* rain.
  - Add pre-modifiers: *the torrential, unceasing* rain.
  - Add a post-modifier (prepositional phrase): *the torrential, unceasing rain over the catchment area*.

#### 4. Connect (15 mins)
- **Student Action:** Complete the **Lesson 10 Student Worksheet** (Extreme Worksheet).
  - *Task 1:* Hunt for 3 expanded noun groups in the Floods Archive and break them down into their components.
  - *Task 2:* Take a provided simple noun (e.g., "clouds", "damage") and expand it into a detailed noun group of their own using an embedded clause.
- **Differentiation (Lucas):** Complete the **Lesson 10 Lucas Handout**.
  - Starting with the word "water", he adds one adjective and one describing phrase (e.g., "the muddy water from the river").
  - Includes a Draw and Label activity to demonstrate comprehension of his expanded noun group. (AC9E2LA07)

---

### Formative Assessment
- **Monitor:** Check student worksheets during the Connect phase. Ensure Year 5 students are correctly identifying and generating post-modifiers (especially prepositional phrases and embedded clauses), not just stringing adjectives together.
- **Check-in with Lucas:** Ensure he can distinguish between the naming word (noun) and the describing word (adjective).
