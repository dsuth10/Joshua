Explore: Authoritative Sources

What makes a source credible and authoritative?

  • Experience: Does the person have direct involvement? (e.g., SES volunteer)
  • Qualifications: Are they an expert? (e.g., hydrologist, meteorologist)
  • Publication Status: Is the information from a reliable organisation? (e.g., Bureau of Meteorology, Government Commission)

Using authoritative sources makes your information report trustworthy.
