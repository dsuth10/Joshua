Let's look at the opening paragraph of the Bushfires Archive:

"Bushfires are severe, uncontrolled outdoor fires that burn through areas of forest, bushland or grassland, which are common across the dry Australian landscape."

Notice these three critical ingredients:

  • Classification / Definition: Identifies what a bushfire is objectively.
  • Expanded Noun Group: "the dry Australian landscape" adds precise description.
  • Complex Sentence: Links a main clause to a relative dependent clause starting with "which...".
