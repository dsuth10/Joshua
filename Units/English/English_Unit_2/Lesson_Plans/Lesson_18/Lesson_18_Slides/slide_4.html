Explore: Purpose of the Opening

An opening classification paragraph serves three essential purposes in an information report:

1. Define & Classify

Clearly introduce the natural disaster topic. Classify it into a broader group (e.g., natural hazards, weather phenomena).

2. Set the Register

Use objective and authoritative language. Speak in the third person to make the writing factual and credible.

3. Establish the Scope

Introduce the main aspects that the rest of the report will explore (e.g., location, causes, impacts, preparation).
