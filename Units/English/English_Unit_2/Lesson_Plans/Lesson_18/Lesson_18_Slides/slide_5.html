Model: Joint Construction (Tornadoes)

Let's examine how a professional writer drafts an opening classification paragraph:

"Tornadoes are violently rotating columns of air that extend from a thunderstorm to the ground, which typically develop during severe convective storms."
Classification defines exactly what tornadoes are in a factual way.
Expanded Noun Group "violently rotating columns of air" adds rich and precise detail.
Complex Sentence combines a main clause with a dependent clause starting with "which typically...".
