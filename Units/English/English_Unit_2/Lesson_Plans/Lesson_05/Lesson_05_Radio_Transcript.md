# Transcript: ABC Radio North Queensland — Emergency Broadcast
**Date:** Wednesday, 2 February 2011  
**Time:** 8:15 PM AEST  
**Location:** Townsville Studios (Live broadcast to Far North Queensland)  
**Presenter:** Kieran Weir

---

**[00:00] SOUND: Low, heavy atmospheric static. The sound of wind whistling through a microphone in the background.**

**KIERAN WEIR:** You’re listening to ABC North Queensland. It’s quarter past eight on Wednesday night. If you’re just joining us, we are in emergency broadcast mode. 

The latest from the Bureau—and it’s not the news we wanted—Yasi has maintained its Category 5 status. It is a monster. It is currently sitting about 140 kilometres east of Innisfail and it is moving fast. We are looking at a landfall somewhere between Mission Beach and Cardwell around midnight. 

If you are in those zones, you should be in your place of shelter. Do not—I repeat, do not—be on the roads now.

**[00:45]**

**KIERAN WEIR:** We’ve got Sarah on the line from Mission Beach. Sarah, can you hear me?

**SARAH (on phone, voice trembling):** Yes, Kieran... I’m here. We’re in the community shelter.

**KIERAN WEIR:** What’s it like there right now?

**SARAH:** It’s... it’s terrifying, to be honest. The wind... it doesn’t sound like wind anymore. It sounds like a jet engine is sitting right outside the door. The whole building is vibrating. We’ve got about two hundred people in here, lots of families. We’re just... we’re just waiting. Every time a gust hits, you can hear the sheet metal on the roofs nearby just screaming.

**KIERAN WEIR:** Stay safe, Sarah. Stay away from the windows. We’ll be here with you all night.

**[01:30]**

**KIERAN WEIR:** For those of you just tuning in, the main message remains the same: this is a life-threatening event. The storm surge is the big worry now. We’re expecting up to five metres. That is a wall of water, folks. If you are in a low-lying area and you haven't moved... it’s too late to move now. Get to the highest point of your house. 

Stay tuned to this frequency. We’ll have another update from the Bureau in ten minutes. This is ABC North Queensland.
