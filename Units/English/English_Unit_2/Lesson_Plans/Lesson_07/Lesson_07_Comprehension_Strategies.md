# Lesson 7: Comprehension Strategies (Skimming, Scanning & Confirming)

**Unit:** Year 5 English — Unit 2: Examining, Creating and Sharing Informative Texts
**Focus:** Using comprehension strategies to navigate a text for a specific purpose.
**Core Text:** Cyclone Archive (Cyclone Mahina)
**Duration:** 45-60 minutes

---

## 1. Learning Intention
I can use comprehension strategies (skimming, scanning, confirming) to navigate a text for a specific purpose. (AC9E5LY04, AC9E5LY05)

## 2. Success Criteria
- I can **skim** a text to quickly find the main idea of a section.
- I can **scan** a text to locate specific keywords or facts.
- I can **confirm** a fact by reading the sentence carefully.
- I can use these strategies to find three facts about the impacts of Cyclone Mahina.

---

## 3. Introduction (Warm-up)
**Activate (10 mins):**
- **Discuss:** "If you were looking for a specific book in a library, would you read every single book from the start? No. What do you do instead?"
- Introduce the three key strategies: Skimming, Scanning, and Confirming.
- Display **Slide 1 & 2**: "What is Skimming?" (Reading fast to find the main idea. Like riding a speedboat across the water).
- Display **Slide 3**: "What is Scanning?" (Looking for specific words, numbers, or facts. Like using a metal detector on the beach).
- Display **Slide 4**: "What is Confirming?" (Slowing down to read carefully and check you have the right information. Like digging up the treasure).

---

## 4. Core Activities

### Part A: Explicit Modelling (15 mins)
- Open the **Cyclone Mahina** archive page on the interactive whiteboard.
- **Model Skimming:** Look at the "A Disaster Without Warning" heading. Skim the paragraph, reading just the first line. Think aloud: "This section looks like it's about the lack of weather warnings."
- **Model Scanning:** "I want to know how high the storm surge was." Scan the page specifically looking for the word "surge" or numbers. "Ah! I see the number 13."
- **Model Confirming:** Read the sentence carefully: "The wave recorded in the aftermath reached 13 metres above normal sea level." Ask the class: "Did I confirm the height of the storm surge?"

### Part B: Independent Research Task (20 mins)
- **Goal:** Set the research task. Provide students with the **Lesson 07 Fact Finding Handout**.
- Students are required to find three facts about the *impacts* of Cyclone Mahina on people, ships, or the environment.
- For each fact, they must record:
  1. The Fact
  2. The strategy they used (Skim/Scan/Confirm)
  3. Where they found it (Heading or section name)
- Display **Slide 5**: "Your Turn" to keep instructions visible.
- Ensure students are not just reading top-to-bottom, but actively using the headings to navigate.

---

## 5. Conclusion (Consolidation)
**Review (5 mins):**
- Bring the class back together.
- Have 2-3 students share a fact they found, explaining *how* they found it using skimming or scanning.
- **Discuss:** "Why is it important to confirm your fact by reading the whole sentence instead of just spotting a keyword?" (To ensure accuracy and context).

---

## 6. Differentiation

### Support (Lucas - Year 2 Pathway)
- **Goal:** With teacher or peer support, find the answer to one guided question: "What is one impact of Cyclone Mahina?" Write the answer in a simple sentence. (AC9E2LY05)
- **Resource:** Provide **Lesson_07_Handout_Lucas_Mahina.docx** (printed). 
- **Action:** Lucas uses the simplified handout. The teacher or SSO supports Lucas to scan the "Fast Facts" section to find a number. Lucas draws a picture of the impact (e.g., ships destroyed) and traces or copies a simple sentence underneath.

### Extension
- Fast finishers must find a fact about the *historical context* of the disaster (e.g., lack of warnings, early pearling industry) and write a paragraph explaining why that fact makes the disaster more significant.
