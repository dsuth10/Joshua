📖 Key Vocabulary

These terms will appear on your handout and in the assessment:

Sequence

A set of things arranged in a particular order.

E.g. The image sequence showed three floods in order.

Chronological

Arranged in the order in which events happened over time.

E.g. 1974, 2011, 2022 — in chronological order.

Salient

The most prominent or eye-catching element in an image.

E.g. The salient image was the flooded rooftop.

Caption

Text placed beneath or beside an image to explain or describe it.

E.g. The caption told us the year and location of the photo.

Foreground / Background

Foreground: what is closest to the viewer and most visible. Background: what is further away and less prominent.
