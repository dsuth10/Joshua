Core Concepts: Salience & Layout

Salience

What draws the eye first due to size, vivid colour, or sharp contrast. Emphasizes focal elements.

Effect on Meaning

Instantly establishes priority. Foregrounding key figures forces the reader to acknowledge their central role.

Layout

The organized interaction of text, images, captions, and data panels across the full document space.

Effect on Meaning

Presents complex multimedia information clearly. Multiplies meaning without creating cognitive overload.
