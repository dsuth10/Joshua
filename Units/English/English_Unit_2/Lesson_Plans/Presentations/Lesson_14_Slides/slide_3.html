Core Concepts: Framing & Placement

Framing

The distance and angle of the camera shot. Controls emotional proximity and context scale.

Effect on Meaning

Close-ups build personal empathy. Wide shots establish overall destruction and immense geographic scale.

Placement

Where an image sits on the page relative to headings, sidebars, and text blocks.

Effect on Meaning

Guides the reading path. Anchors visual evidence directly next to relevant data panels or pull quotes.
