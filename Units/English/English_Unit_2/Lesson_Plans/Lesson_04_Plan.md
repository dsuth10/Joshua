# Lesson 04: Time and Place (Context)

**Unit:** Year 5 English — Unit 2: Examining, Creating and Sharing Informative Texts  
**Sequence:** 1 — Text Immersion: Purpose, Audience & Text Types  
**Core Text:** Cyclone Archive (Tracy & Mahina sub-pages)  
**Duration:** 60 Minutes  

---

## Learning Intention
I can explain how a text reflects the time and place in which it was created.

## Success Criteria
- I can define **context** as the historical and geographical background of a text.
- I can identify historical clues (dates, technology, language) in the Cyclone Archive.
- I can explain how geographical context (location) influences the information in the text.

## Australian Curriculum Mapping (v9)
- **Year 5:** AC9E5LY01 — Explain how texts reflect the time and place in which they were created.
- **Lucas (Y2):** AC9E2LY05 — Use comprehension strategies to build literal and inferred meaning.

---

## Introduction (Warm-up) — 10 Mins
**Discussion:**
- Display a map of Australia showing cyclone activity.
- Question: "Why do cyclones happen in Queensland more than in Victoria?"
- Discuss: How does our **location** (geography) determine what we need to know and what we write about?
- Introduce the word **Context**: The 'Where' and 'When' of a story or text.

---

## Core Activity 1: Defining Context — 10 Mins
**Direct Instruction:**
- Context = Time + Place.
- **Time (Historical):** When was the text written? What was happening in the world? What technology did they have? (e.g., 1974 vs 2024).
- **Place (Geographical):** Where was the text written? Why is this location important to the topic?

---

## Core Activity 2: Comparing Eras (Tracy vs Mahina) — 15 Mins
**Class Exploration:**
- Open the **Cyclone Tracy (1974)** and **Cyclone Mahina (1899)** sub-pages.
- Look at the images and the "Quick Facts" sections.
- **Observe:**
    - How is the language different? (e.g., "wireless telegraphy" vs "satellite imaging").
    - How do the photos look? (Black and white, grainy, or hand-drawn sketches).
    - What do these clues tell us about the **Time** the text describes?

---

## Core Activity 3: Modelling Analysis — 10 Mins
**Teacher Modelling:**
- Select a specific excerpt from the **Cyclone Tracy** page.
- *Think Aloud:* "I see that in 1974, communication was very different. The text mentions 'radio reports' and 'landlines'. This reflects the **context** of the 1970s because they didn't have mobile phones or the internet to warn people."
- Write a model response on the board:
    > "This text reflects the historical context of the 1970s because it mentions radio as the primary warning system, which was the most common technology available at that time and place."

---

## Core Activity 4: Independent Practice — 10 Mins
**Student Task:**
- Students choose one Cyclone sub-page.
- Write 2–3 sentences explaining how that page reflects its historical or geographical context.
- Use the provided **Sentence Starters**.

**Differentiation (Lucas - Y2):**
- Look at the primary image on the **Cyclone Tracy** page.
- Answer: "When did this happen? How do you know? (Look at the cars, the clothes, the buildings)."
- Teacher or peer to scribe Lucas's observations.

---

## Conclusion (Consolidation) — 5 Mins
**Reflection:**
- "If we wrote a text about a cyclone today, what clues would we include that show it was made in 2026?"
- (Expected: AI warnings, social media alerts, high-def drone footage).
