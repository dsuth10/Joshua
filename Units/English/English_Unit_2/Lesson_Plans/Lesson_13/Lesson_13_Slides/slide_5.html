What Is Image Sequencing?

Authors arrange images in a deliberate order to build meaning. There are several types:

Type Description Example
🕐 Chronological Images ordered by time — shows change across years or events. 1974 flood → 2011 flood → 2022 flood
⚡ Cause & Effect First image shows the cause; later images show the results. Storm clouds → rising water → flooded streets
🔄 Before & After Two images comparing the same place before and after an event. Home before flood / same home after flood
🔄 Life Cycle Images showing stages of a repeating process or how-to steps. Flood → recede → rebuild → next flood
