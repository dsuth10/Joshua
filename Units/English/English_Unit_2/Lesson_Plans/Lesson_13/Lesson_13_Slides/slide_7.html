📝 Model: Annotating an Image Sequence

Three steps to analyse any image sequence:

Step 1: NAME Step 2: NOTICE Step 3: EXPLAIN

What is literally shown in each image? (Describe it factually)

What changes from one image to the next? (Water level, damage, scale)

What effect does this have on the reader's understanding?

"The sequence moves from normal conditions → partial inundation → severe flooding. This builds a sense of escalation — the reader understands the situation is worsening."
