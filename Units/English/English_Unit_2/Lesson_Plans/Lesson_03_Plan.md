# Lesson 03: Structural Navigation Features

**Unit:** Year 5 English — Unit 2: Examining, Creating and Sharing Informative Texts  
**Sequence:** 1 — Text Immersion: Purpose, Audience & Text Types  
**Core Text:** Cyclone Archive  
**Duration:** 60 Minutes  

---

## Learning Intention
I can explain how structural navigation features help readers find and understand information.

## Success Criteria
- I can identify navigation features (hub page, sub-pages, headings, internal links).
- I can explain the purpose of different navigation features.
- I can compare digital navigation features to book features (Contents, Index, Glossary).

## Australian Curriculum Mapping (v9)
- **Year 5:** AC9E5LA03 — Describe the characteristic stages and phases of informative texts.
- **Lucas (Y2):** AC9E2LA03 — Identify how texts across the curriculum are organised differently and use language features depending on purposes.

---

## Introduction (Warm-up) — 10 Mins
**Class Navigation Challenge:**
- Display the Cyclone Archive Hub Page.
- Challenge: "Can you find the page about **Cyclone Tracy** in under 30 seconds?"
- Discuss: What made it easy to find? (Heading, image, link).
- What if there were no links or headings? How would you find it then?

---

## Core Activity 1: Exploring Features — 15 Mins
**Direct Instruction:**
- Examine the **Cyclone Archive** navigation features together.
- Define and discuss the purpose of:
    - **Hub Page:** The "front door" or home base.
    - **Sub-pages:** Specific pages for different events.
    - **Headings & Subheadings:** Signposts for what is in a section.
    - **Pull Quotes:** Highlighting key facts or quotes.
    - **Internal Links:** "Portals" to more information.
- **Comparison:** How are these similar to a book's Table of Contents, Index, or Glossary? How are they different? (e.g., internal links are faster than an Index).

---

## Core Activity 2: Modelling Annotation — 15 Mins
**Teacher Modelling:**
- Select the **Cyclone Tracy sub-page**.
- Model annotating the page (digital or printed) for navigation features.
- *Think Aloud:* "I see a large bold heading at the top. This tells me exactly what this page is about. I also see a 'Back to Hub' button, which helps me navigate home."
- Record the feature and its purpose on the class display.

---

## Core Activity 3: Independent Practice — 15 Mins
**Student Task:**
- Students explore the full Cyclone Archive.
- Record **5 navigation features** on the **Navigation Feature Recording Sheet**.
- For each feature, write a short explanation of how it helps the reader.

**Differentiation (Lucas - Y2):**
- Identify and draw/label **2 navigation features** (e.g., Heading, Image) from the Cyclone Tracy page.
- Complete the sentence starter: "This helps the reader because..."

---

## Conclusion (Consolidation) — 5 Mins
**Reflection:**
- "Which navigation feature do you think is the most helpful for someone who is in a hurry?"
- Share findings from the recording sheets.
