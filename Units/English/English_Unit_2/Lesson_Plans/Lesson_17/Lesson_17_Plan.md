# Lesson 17: Planning an Information Report

## Learning Intention
I can plan an information report using structural features modelled on the Bushfires Archive. (AC9E5LY06, AC9E5LA03)

## Materials
- Lesson_17_Presentation.pptx
- Lesson_17_Worksheet.docx (Student Handout - Part B Planning Sheet)
- Lesson_17_Lucas_Handout.docx (Differentiated Planning Scaffold)
- Lesson_17_Assessment.docx
- Bushfires Archive (hub page)
- Research resources (other natural disaster archives)

## Teaching & Learning Sequence

### Activate (10 mins)
- Explore the **Bushfires Archive** together as a class.
- Introduce the students' own report topic: they may choose one natural disaster topic to research and report on for their upcoming assessment (Part B).
- **Discuss:** What natural disaster are you most interested in? Why is planning an important step before we start writing?

### Explore (15 mins)
- Show the planning scaffold (Part B planning sheet) to the class.
- Examine how the **Bushfires Archive hub page** serves as a structural model for an information report.
- **Trace the structure:**
  1. General statement (Classification)
  2. Physical description/science (What causes it?)
  3. Historical events (Famous examples)
  4. Human impact (How it affects people)
  5. Future/response (Preparation and prevention)
- Discuss how following a structure helps the reader understand the information clearly.

### Model (10 mins)
- **Joint Construction:** The teacher models completing the planning scaffold for a new hypothetical topic (e.g., Tsunami or Volcano) using the Bushfires Archive as a structural reference.
- Think aloud to demonstrate the decision-making process: *"First, I need my general statement to introduce what a tsunami is. Then, I'll need some facts about the physical science behind it."*

### Connect (10 mins)
- **Independent Practice:** Students begin completing their own planning scaffold for their chosen natural disaster topic.
- Students must include at least 3 facts sourced from the archives or their own research.
- Teacher circulates to assist with topic selection and structural planning.

## Differentiation
- **Lucas (Y2):** With teacher support, complete a simplified planning scaffold: "My topic is...", "I will write about three things: 1. ___ 2. ___ 3. ___", "I will include a picture of...". (AC9E2LY06)

## Assessment
- Formative: Observation during the independent planning session (checking for understanding of the structural stages).
- Formative: Review of the students' planning scaffolds.
- Summative: Lesson 17 Microsoft Forms Assessment (10 questions on the structural features of an information report).
