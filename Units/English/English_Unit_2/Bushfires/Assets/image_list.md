# Image Assets Required

This document outlines all the image assets needed for the Bushfire Archive website, based on the placeholders found in the HTML files.

## Home Page (`index.html`)
1. **Black Saturday Card:** Atmospheric photo of thick orange smoke rolling over the hills of Kinglake, with a single silhouette of a gum tree.
2. **Ash Wednesday Card:** Archival 1980s style photo of volunteer fire trucks speeding down a dusty rural road under a dark ash sky.
3. **Black Summer Card:** Satellite imagery showing massive smoke plumes extending from the Australian east coast out over the Pacific Ocean.

## Ash Wednesday (`Ash_Wednesday/index.html`)
4. **Hero Image:** A vintage-style photograph from 1983 showing a long line of yellow CFA and MFS trucks silhouetted against a deep red sky in the Adelaide Hills.
5. **Scale of Destruction Map:** A map of South-Eastern Australia showing large clusters of fire icons in South Australia (Adelaide Hills) and Victoria (Dandenong ranges, Great Ocean Road).
6. **National Shock News:** A 1980s newspaper front page, slightly yellowed, showing a massive headline 'ASH WEDNESDAY' featuring a silhouette of a destroyed home.
7. **The Old Guard Photo:** A photo of firefighters in the older 1980s-era yellow protective coats and round helmets, dragging a thick canvas hose through smoking ruins.

## Black Saturday (`Black_Saturday/index.html`)
8. **Hero Image:** A powerful cinematic shot of the Great Dividing Range engulfed in an orange glow, with towering pyrocumulonimbus clouds forming over the peaks.
9. **The Fire Map:** A map of Victoria highlighting the Kilmore East and Murrindindi fire areas, showing the huge footprint created by the south-westerly wind change.
10. **Community Loss Aerial:** A striking aerial photograph showing total destruction in a forested township. Only brick chimneys and twisted metal roofs remain amongst blackened trees.
11. **The Exhaustion Photo:** A dramatic photo of an exhausted firefighter resting against a yellow CFA truck, face covered in soot, silhouetted against a smoky red sky.

## Black Summer (`Black_Summer/index.html`)
12. **Hero Image:** A breathtaking satellite composite image of eastern Australia, showing tens of thousands of square kilometres of smoke plumes drifting across the Tasman Sea.
13. **Coastal Evacuation Photo:** A striking photo taken from a rescue boat offshore, showing the shoreline under a dark crimson sky, with a bright orange glow along the entire coastline.
14. **Environmental Tragedy Photo:** A heart-wrenching photo of a volunteer kneeling in blackened scrubland, offering a water bottle to a severely burned and dehydrated koala.
15. **Military Response Photo:** A large military helicopter landing in a smoky field to deliver supplies to cut-off towns, with exhausted volunteer firefighters looking on.

## Prevention & Prep (`Prevention_Prep/index.html`)
16. **Ember Attack Diagram:** An educational diagram showing a suburban house. A red arrow shows wind blowing glowing 'embers' from a distant forest fire directly into the home's leaf-filled gutters and under a wooden deck.

## The Frontline (`The_Frontline/index.html`)
17. **Hero Image:** A close-up, dramatic shot of a firefighter's helmet and goggles reflecting the orange glow of a distant fire front. They are speaking into a shoulder-mounted radio.
18. **PPE Breakdown Graphic:** A highly detailed breakdown graphic of a bush firefighter's Personal Protective Equipment (PPE). Labels point to the wide-brimmed yellow helmet, fire-retardant proban jacket, flash-hood protecting the neck, and heavy leather boots.
