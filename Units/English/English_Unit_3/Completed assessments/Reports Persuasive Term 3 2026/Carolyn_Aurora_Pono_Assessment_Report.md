## 💫 Wishes! (Inquiry-Based Goals for Your Revision)

### Goal 1: Complete Sentence Fragments in Body Paragraphs (W3)
* **Look closely at your sentence:**  
  > “While environmentalist fight and protest to protect the environment and the animals that live in it as well.” (P2)
* **Coaching Question:** A sentence starting with 'While' needs a second half to complete the thought! How could you rewrite this so that environmentalists are the main subject taking action: 'Meanwhile, passionate environmentalists fight to protect...'?
* **Self-Check Task:** `[ ]` I have turned this dependent clause into a complete, standalone sentence.

### Goal 2: Resolve Contradictory Scientific Claims (W1)
* **Look closely at your sentence:**  
  > “Palm oil is an edible vegetable oil extracted from the fruit of palm trees... and if we extract the oil from the ground, it will cause erosion...” (P1)
* **Coaching Question:** In the first sentence you correctly explain that palm oil comes from the fruit of trees, but then you say we extract oil from the ground! How can you edit this to clarify that clearing tree roots from the ground causes erosion?
