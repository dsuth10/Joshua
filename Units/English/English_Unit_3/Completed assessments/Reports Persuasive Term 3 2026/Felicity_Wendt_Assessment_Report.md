## 💫 Wishes! (Inquiry-Based Goals for Your Revision)

### Goal 1: Punctuate Rhetorical Questions Correctly (W3)
* **Look closely at your sentence:**  
  > “Who wouldn’t want to peek into the eyes of an orangutan in its natural rainforest home.” (P4)
* **Coaching Question:** Notice how this sentence starts with the question word 'Who'! Why should this sentence end with a question mark (?) instead of a full stop?
* **Self-Check Task:** `[ ]` I have changed the full stop to a question mark at the end of my rhetorical question.

### Goal 2: Fix the Consequence Homophone (W3)
* **Look closely at your sentence:**  
  > “If farmers keep chopping down the trees than we will have no orangutans left in the world.” (P3)
* **Coaching Question:** Look at the word 'than'. Which spelling do we use for cause and effect: 'than' (for comparing things) or 'then' (what happens next)?
* **Self-Check Task:** `[ ]` I have corrected 'than' to 'then' in my 'If... then...' sentences.
