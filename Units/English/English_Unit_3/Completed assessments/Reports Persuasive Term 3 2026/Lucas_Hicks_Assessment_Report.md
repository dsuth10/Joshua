## 📌 Supplementary Diagnostic Observations

* **Spelling Monitoring Strategy:**  
  * *Observations:* Profound diagnostic observation. While P1–P4 demonstrate mostly functional spelling ('personal protection', 'allergic', 'library'), P5 suffers an acute breakdown into phonetic approximations: 'tack keyer' (take care), 'frens' (friends), 'happy yer' (happier), 'bekoos yor' (because your). Plural apostrophes in 'pet's' (P3) and 'School's' (P5). Urgent phonics and word-study target.
* **Punctuation & Syntactic Conventions:**  
  * *Observations:* Sentences in P1 are clipped and repetitive. P3 and P5 lack capitalisation after periods and omit full stops before new independent clauses.
* **Task Guidance & Text Length Considerations:**  
  * *Observations:* At 222 words, the draft meets the minimum 200-word guideline, but requires greater elaboration of reasons.
