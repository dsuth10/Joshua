## 📌 Supplementary Diagnostic Observations

* **Spelling Monitoring Strategy:**  
  * *Observations:* Generally secure on common words. Phonetic error: 'Farrell' (feral). Note mid-sentence capitalization slips: 'because You will have', 'because They're cute', 'from bullies and Haters'.
* **Punctuation & Syntactic Conventions:**  
  * *Observations:* P3 is heavily run-on with five consecutive clauses joined by capitalised 'And'. Full stops needed to break speech dialogue from narrative exposition.
* **Task Guidance & Text Length Considerations:**  
  * *Observations:* At 254 words, the text meets the 200-word guideline, but could be strengthened by developing analytical reasons alongside narrative examples.

## 💫 Wishes! (Inquiry-Based Goals for Your Revision)

### Goal 1: Break Narrative Storytelling into Separate Sentences (W3)
* **Look closely at your sentence:**  
  > “And then you have your lunch money back And then you have their lunch money.” (P3)
* **Coaching Question:** You have chained your funny story together using 'And then... And then'! How could you place a full stop between these two actions to make the final punchline stand out on its own?
* **Self-Check Task:** `[ ]` I have replaced 'And then' with a full stop and a new sentence.

### Goal 2: Fix Mid-Sentence Capital Letters (W3)
* **Look closely at your sentence:**  
  > “Personal protection is important in Queensland child safety Because animals can protect you from bullies and Haters.” (P3)
* **Coaching Question:** Look at the words 'Because' and 'Haters'. Why should 'because' have a lowercase 'b' when it joins two parts of the same sentence, and why does 'haters' not need a capital?
* **Self-Check Task:** `[ ]` I have fixed capital letters that appeared in the middle of my sentence.
