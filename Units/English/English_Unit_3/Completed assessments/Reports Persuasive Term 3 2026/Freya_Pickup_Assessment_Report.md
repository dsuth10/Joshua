## 💫 Wishes! (Inquiry-Based Goals for Your Revision)

### Goal 1: Upgrade Spoken Playground Phrases to Formal Writing (W3)
* **Look closely at your sentence:**  
  > “Why can't you just plant the food somewhere else you doing habitat destruction. So just imagine waking up to see no orangutangs no forest I would be heartbroken would you get sad? So, I'm just say just plant food somewhere else.” (P2)
* **Coaching Question:** Phrases like 'you doing habitat destruction' and 'I'm just say' sound like talking out loud! How could you rewrite this formally: 'Agricultural clearing causes severe habitat destruction. In my opinion, farmers must...'?
* **Self-Check Task:** `[ ]` I have rewritten conversational phrases into formal, complete written sentences.

### Goal 2: Complete Fragmented Sentences (W3)
* **Look closely at your sentence:**  
  > “Although it is sad just to see an orangutang to be wiped out.” (P3)
* **Coaching Question:** A sentence starting with 'Although' is only half a thought! What must happen in the second half? For example: 'Although it is sad to see orangutans wiped out, [what must we do to stop it]?'
* **Self-Check Task:** `[ ]` I have added a main clause to complete my 'Although' sentence.
