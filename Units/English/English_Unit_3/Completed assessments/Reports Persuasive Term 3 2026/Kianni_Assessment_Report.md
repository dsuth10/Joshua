## 💫 Wishes! (Inquiry-Based Goals for Your Revision)

### Goal 1: Align Your Paragraph Signposts (W2)
* **Look closely at your sentence:**  
  > “Secondly, the rainforest has been affected by palm oil as well.” (P2)
* **Coaching Question:** Notice how Paragraph 2 begins with 'Secondly', but Paragraph 1 did not have a 'Firstly'! How could you open Paragraph 1 with a signpost, or change Paragraph 2 to an opening like 'Furthermore' or 'In addition'?
* **Self-Check Task:** `[ ]` I have matched my paragraph signposts so my numbering is smooth and logical.

### Goal 2: Untangle Repeated Words in Complex Sentences (W3)
* **Look closely at your sentence:**  
  > “Palm oil though palm oil is not taking homes it is taking their health.” (P3)
* **Coaching Question:** You have repeated 'palm oil' twice in five words! How could you replace the second mention with a pronoun like 'it', and use a comma after 'homes' to make this contrast crystal clear?
