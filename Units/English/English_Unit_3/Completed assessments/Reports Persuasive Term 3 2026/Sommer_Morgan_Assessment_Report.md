## 💫 Wishes! (Inquiry-Based Goals for Your Revision)

### Goal 1: Break Up Long Opening Run-on Sentences (W3)
* **Look closely at your sentence:**  
  > “Imagine being a tiny little orangutang, trying to find food, but then you hopped off of your mother's back then you look back at your mother and you see a human, cutting down a tree and your mother was looking at the human like saying 'please don't let this tree fall on me.'” (P1)
* **Coaching Question:** This sentence is over 50 words long with four 'and' and 'then' joins! Where could you place a full stop after hopping off your mother's back, so the human with the chainsaw arrives in a dramatic new sentence?
* **Self-Check Task:** `[ ]` I have split this long opening into two distinct, dramatic sentences.

### Goal 2: Connect Your Captivity Point to Your Main Argument (W2)
* **Look closely at your sentence:**  
  > “If humans keep orangutangs in captivity they will have no happiness, no entertainment, and they will become obedient.” (P5)
* **Coaching Question:** Your conclusion suddenly talks about 'captivity' and becoming 'obedient', but your essay was about tree clearing! How can you add a sentence explaining that displaced baby orangutans end up locked in small cages?
* **Self-Check Task:** `[ ]` I have linked my habitat loss argument to why animals end up in captivity.
