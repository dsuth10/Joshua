ANNOTATED MODEL

Fair does not mean neutral mush

Both drafts use real concerns, but each pushes readers towards a different judgement. Message A calls the camp a “deafening invasion”, which exaggerates noise and alarms residents. Message B explains pollination, but “welcome from everyone” dismisses people affected by odour or mess. The council should replace loaded labels with specific descriptions, give no-touch safety advice and explain lawful management.
