Malia believes signing would be wrong. But the choice has consequences for other people.

POINT OF VIEW

What can we hear because Malia tells it?

“I can’t sign the letter.”

First person gives readers Malia’s private weighing: her fear of expulsion, her concern for Mrs Harwono, and the moment she decides what she can live with.

What would be harder to know in a scene told only from outside Malia?

ANNOTATED MODEL

Keep two truths visible

Claim: Malia’s refusal is presented as principled, but not easy.

Evidence: She believes signing means she must say she supports palm oil.

Competing pressure: She worries about Mrs Harwono and hears Putu’s anger.

Inference: This suggests that acting on a belief can still hurt people a character cares about.

Point of view: Because Malia narrates her own hesitation, readers can understand her reasoning even if they judge her choice differently.
