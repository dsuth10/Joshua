Annotated model

Persuade without pretending certainty

5 minutes
Position: The council should strengthen seasonal rules in mapped turtle-sensitive areas while keeping necessary public spaces safely lit.
Evidence: Official guidance explains that night lighting can disturb nesting turtles and disorient hatchlings.
Chain: Therefore, purpose-specific lights should be shielded, directed, low-intensity and adaptively controlled.
Fair concern: A beachfront business may reasonably worry about safe access and evening trade.
Response: However, national guidance calls for solutions that meet safety and wildlife objectives together.
Boundary + action: The pathway cannot prove an exact number protected; consult, monitor and improve the rule.
