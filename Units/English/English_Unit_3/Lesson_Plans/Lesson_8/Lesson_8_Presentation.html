ANNOTATED MODEL

A strong opinion has four jobs

Rules should usually be followed. Still, bending a rule can be fair when urgent harm is happening and safer options have failed. Mrs Harwono wants the petition paused because it names the school and needs principal approval. Malia believes waiting protects something unfair. Hiding her online plan also risks trouble for her teacher and classmates. So Malia's choice is understandable, but not fully fair yet. She should try another approved way first. Only then is limited rule-bending easier to defend.
