# Lesson 11 v2: Words With Exact Jobs

> Version 2 — teacher-led individual route for roughly ages 9–10. Practice precision on **varied Berani language**, then write **one persuasive paragraph** using a large word bank.  
> Presentation uses explicit “Your job:” instructions and **no bottom DO/WORK/RECORD/TIME/FINISH/CHECK panels**.  
> **Word-choice talk is oral** — no written “prove / justify your word choice” stems on slides.  
> Original files remain: `Lesson_11_Plan.md`, `Lesson_11_Presentation.html`.

## Lesson purpose

Students examine how precise vocabulary carries exact meaning, tone and context. They rehearse that skill across many words from *Berani*, then write **one persuasive paragraph** to Uncle about Ginger Juice’s enclosure, choosing exact words from a shared bank.

## Curriculum focus

- **Year 5 - AC9E5LA03: describe how language features contribute to meaning.**
- **Year 6 - AC9E6LA03: explain how vocabulary choices are adapted for purpose and context.**

## Learning intention

We are learning to select vocabulary for exact meaning, tone and context.

## Success criteria

- I can infer a word's meaning from contextual evidence.
- I can explain what a precise word adds that a near-synonym misses.
- I can recognise when a word is grammatical but contextually wrong.
- I can write one persuasive paragraph using exact words that fit my purpose.
- I can talk about why I chose an exact word (classroom conversation — not a written stem).

## Finished workbook response

One persuasive paragraph to Uncle, arguing that Ginger Juice needs a larger, better space, using exact words selected from the lesson word bank.

## Design rule (v2)

**Do not recycle the same four words in every activity.**  
SC1–SC3 and early conversion practice use **different precise words** from *Berani* through Ari (Pages 69–71). The finale is a **persuasive paragraph** with a large optional word bank (not a forced four-term rewrite).

**Justification is teacher-led talk**, not a slide task or workbook stem.

## Core sequence

### Hook — 2 minutes · Hook slide

Some words look almost the same, but only one does the exact job. Preview: many Berani words today; later **one persuasive paragraph** to Uncle using a word bank.

### Four checks — 3 minutes · Step 1

Reveal MEANING → FEELING → PLACE → SENTENCE. Product callout names the **persuasive paragraph** finale.

---

### SC1 — Infer from context (varied words)

#### Round 1 — Sort — 5 min · Step 2

Match clues to **explosive, guilt, indignation, elation**.

#### Round 2 — Cloze — 5 min · Step 3

Extracts for **conflicted, betraying, woefully, glower**.

#### Round 3 (CORE+) — Scene infer — 3 min · Step 4

Ari 49–51: **nostalgia** and **mesmerised**.

---

### SC2 — Precise vs near-synonym (varied words)

#### Round 1 — 5 min · Step 5

**explosive** vs difficult; **indignation** vs grumpiness. After check, read the nuance; talk orally if useful.

#### Round 2 — 5 min · Step 6

**celestial, wrought, in lieu, captivity** — new scenes, same skill.

#### Round 3 (CORE+) — 4 min · Step 7

Closest-but-wrong strip: **guilt / woefully / glower / nostalgia** (students name the weaker near-word).

---

### SC3 — Grammatical but contextually wrong (varied words)

#### Round 1 — Sort — 4 min · Step 8

Boundary tests with **explosive** and **indignation** (FITS / DOES NOT FIT).

#### Round 2 — Reveal — 4 min · Step 9

**guilt** (two cards) and **mesmerised** — decide first, then click to reveal.

#### Round 3 — Rule — 2 min · Step 10

Copy: Who says it? • To whom? • How serious? • Exact feeling?

---

### SC4 — Conversion practice → persuasive paragraph

#### Round 1 — Model sentence — 3 min · Step 11

Vague → **woefully** + **nostalgia** (teacher build).

#### Round 2 — Individual sentence — 4 min · Step 12

Convert Ari’s “felt bad” sentence using **guilt / betraying** (or other exact fits). Optional oral share; no written stem.

#### Word bank — 3 min · Step 13

Show the persuasive job (dominant on slide) and large word bank. Students star 4–6 words they might use. Do not write the paragraph yet.

#### Upgrade the words — 5 min · Step 14

Interactive: bland persuasive paragraph at the top. Students click a yellow plain word; a **mixed bank** appears (strong exact words **and** weaker near-misses, shuffled). Green = strong upgrade; amber = close but weaker — try again. Talk orally about power, not just “right/wrong.”

#### Model persuasive paragraph — 4 min · Step 15

Teacher builds a short model persuading Uncle (ask → evidence → feeling → push → close).

#### Big write — 10 min · Step 16

Independent: **one persuasive paragraph** to Uncle about Ginger Juice’s space. Word bank stays on screen. Timer optional (10:00).

#### Self-check — 3 min · Step 17 · Exit — 2 min · Step 18

Self-check ticks for paragraph, clear ask, exact words, fit, natural voice. Exit: write **one exact word** from today. Be ready to talk about it with the teacher.

- Oral rehearsal to teacher/self before writing.
- Skip CORE+ (Steps 4 and 7) if time is tight.

## Extension

- Transfer the precision skill to a new Berani sentence not on the deck.

## Accuracy and privacy boundaries

- Use only verified details from *Berani* through Ari (Pages 69–71) and the approved unit sequence.
- Do not ask students to disclose personal experiences of cultural labels, grief, rule-breaking or trauma.
