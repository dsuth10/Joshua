Persuade Ibu to turn back

CORE14 MIN

Ginger Juice tries to persuade Ibu not to go on to the dangerous place. Use only what she can sense, remember and understand as your reasons.

  • Argue from the treetops: why home is safer
  • Argue from the burned ground: what has been lost
  • Argue from the human market: the danger she senses
  • Use sensory evidence as your reasons
  • Include two purposeful figurative choices

Boundary: Ginger Juice may sense and infer danger; she cannot explain the human system.
