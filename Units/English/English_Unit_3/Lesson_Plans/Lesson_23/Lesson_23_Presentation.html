WORD WORK

Not every “but” is a rebuttal

CORE5 MIN
MODEL

Acknowledge. Answer. Return to the claim.

CORE6 MIN

Some people may worry that removing an animal from a restaurant could affect its customers or workers. However, a responsible rescue plan can seek specialist advice and consider people affected by the change. The animal’s welfare still matters because keeping a wild animal as a mascot does not meet its needs.

Model only: this is a practice argument built from the unit’s economic-impact example, not a quotation from the novel.
