ANNOTATED MODEL

Predict without pretending you know

CORE5 MIN

Ari and Malia could become allies because both chapters show them learning that people’s actions have consequences. Ari promises to help Ginger Juice after he sees evidence of cruelty, even though Uncle’s business interests block him. Malia begins to understand Papa’s message about kindness after she sees the street girl. Because both characters are being pushed toward responsibility, their connection might give Ginger Juice’s situation more attention. This is a prediction, not a certainty: the chapters do not yet show what they will do together.
