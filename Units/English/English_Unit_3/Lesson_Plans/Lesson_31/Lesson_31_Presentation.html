BERANI • LESSON 31

Make your reasons do their job.

A strong persuasive paragraph does not merely state a reason. It proves, explains and connects it to the audience.

PEEL BODY PARAGRAPHS • 55-MINUTE CORE
ENTRY

Which paragraph earns belief?

4 min
A

We need a Storytime Dog because it would be good.

B

A trained Storytime Dog should be introduced because a calm, non-judgemental reading audience can help hesitant readers practise with greater confidence.

Choose one. Which sentence gives a reader more to believe — and why?
PURPOSE

Today: build two paragraphs readers can follow.

2 min

Product: draft the first two body paragraphs of your individual persuasive text.

NOTICE

The point tells the reader where to look.

4 min
POINT

A trained Storytime Dog should be introduced because it can give hesitant readers a calm audience for regular reading practice.

What is this paragraph trying to prove? Underline the precise benefit.
EVIDENCE

Evidence must be traceable.

5 min
NOT EVIDENCE“Everyone knows dogs help.”
EVIDENCE NOTEAccurate paraphrase or short quotation from an identified source.
HONEST GAP“Needs checking” — pause rather than invent proof.

Evidence answers: How do I know this is reasonable?

MODEL

Watch the reasoning bridge.

6 min

Point A trained Storytime Dog should be introduced because it can give hesitant readers a calm audience for regular reading practice.

Evidence [Insert an accurate, traceable source note from your research.]

Explanation This matters because students who worry about errors may be more willing to practise when the audience feels safe; regular practice can support their reading confidence.

Link For this reason, a well-managed program could make reading practice more accessible for the students it is designed to support.

PRECISION

Year 6: add precision, not puff.

5 min
PLAIN

The dog helps readers. It is good.

PRECISE

Because a trained, carefully supervised reading dog offers a non-judgemental audience, hesitant readers may feel safer to practise aloud.

Notice: complex sentence • expanded noun group (a trained, carefully supervised reading dog) • cautious, evidence-aware language.

STUDIO

Drafting studio: two body paragraphs

12 min

Write → read aloud → revise

  • Start each paragraph with a different, precise point.
  • Use only an identified, accurate evidence note — or leave a clear needs-checking marker.
  • Explain the reasoning: This matters because…
  • Link each reason back to your audience and opinion.
