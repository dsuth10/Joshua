NOTICE

Which paragraph guides the reader?

4 min
A

Our school needs this. It helps. Students are happy. This is good.

B

Our school needs this reading program because it can build students’ confidence. For example, regular practice can help nervous readers participate. Therefore, the program is a practical support for learning.

MODEL

Edit meaning before hunting every error.

6 min
1. Read one paragraph aloud quietly.2. Box its main point.3. Draw arrows between ideas that belong together.4. Add, move or replace one word where the connection breaks.

Ask: “Can a reader tell why this detail proves my point?”

STUDIO

Self-editing studio

12 min

12 minutes • three passes

  1. Meaning: trace one paragraph’s point → detail → explanation.
  2. Precision: improve one vague noun group or connector.
  3. Conventions: check sentence flow, punctuation and two uncertain words.
  4. Make one revision in a different colour or with a revision mark.
