LESSON 24 ALTERNATIVE | SYNTHESIS

Three clues. One careful forecast.Three clues. What could happen next?

A good prediction is built from evidence, not a guess dressed as certainty.Use clues. Do not pretend the future has happened.

DO Invite a quick notice: what makes a forecast careful?

WORK Think-pair-share.

RECORD One possible connection.

FINISH A student states a possible, not confirmed, outcome.

CHECK Listen for cautious language.

Prediction: The Lantern Walk could use a changed route and lantern.
