MODEL

Model: evidence does not become certainty

5 MIN

Evidence: Ari has a rescue contact and Malia offers support.

Inference: They may be able to coordinate a next step.

Prediction: Ginger Juice could be removed from the restaurant if Ari follows through and the organisation can act. This does not prove when, how or whether the rescue will succeed.

PLAN

Build a three-storyline forecast

3 MIN
MALIA: evidence → possible role   |   ARI: evidence → possible choice   |   GINGER JUICE: evidence → possible change

Then connect all three with one cautious resolution sentence.

WRITE

Write the midpoint prediction

12 MIN

9–11 sentences

  • Predict a possible resolution for Malia, Ari and Ginger Juice
  • Use at least one accurate detail for each storyline
  • Explain how the narrative lines are coming together
  • Use cautious prediction language
  • Name one condition or uncertainty
REVISE

Separate what you know from what you predict

4 MIN
□ Three storylines included. □ Three accurate details. □ Connections explained. □ Prediction signal used. □ One condition named. □ No future event presented as confirmed.
