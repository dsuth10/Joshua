# Lesson 14 - Adapted Evidence Lesson

This subdirectory contains a complete adapted pathway for the Great Barrier Reef Lesson 14 alternative.

## Teach with

- `Lesson_14_GBR_Adapted_Presentation.html` - teacher-controlled classroom presentation
- `Lesson_14_GBR_Adapted_Student_Pack.docx` - three-page student reading and writing pack
- `Lesson_14_GBR_Adapted_Teacher_Guide.md` - preparation, lesson sequence, model answers and support

## Optional reading formats

- `Lesson_14_GBR_Adapted_Reading_Article.html` - screen and print version of the one-page reading
- `Lesson_14_GBR_Adapted_Reading_Article.pdf` - print-ready one-page PDF

## Lesson outcome

Students find facts in a short information text and use two facts as evidence in a five-to-six-sentence persuasive paragraph.

## Rebuild and quality checks

The `scripts` folder contains the student-pack builder, article PDF renderer and presentation audit. The source images are in `assets`.
