MODEL

Model: from event to a persuasive claim

Event: Ari calls for help even though he fears Uncle’s response.

Consequence: A rescue team can remove Ginger Juice from the cage.

Theme claim: Berani suggests that bravery can mean acting for another living thing even when the action may bring conflict.

Why it matters: This claim could become the centre of a blog post encouraging readers to notice and respond to harm.
