BERANI • LESSON 35

Your writing is a guide. Your voice does the persuading.

A spoken opinion is not a reading test. Use your written argument to remember the route, then talk to a real audience with purposeful voice.

SPOKEN ASSESSMENT PREPARATION • 55-MINUTE CORE
PURPOSE

The individual spoken task

3 min
Opinion→Arguments + support→Fair response→Action

Your individual presentation is suggested to be 1–3 minutes. The writing guides your structure; it is not a script to read word-for-word.

NOTICE

Which speaker sounds more connected?

4 min
READING

Our school should introduce this program because it would benefit students and the community.

SPEAKING FROM CUES

Our school should introduce this program. [pause] It gives students a practical chance to benefit—and it gives our community a reason to act.

MODEL

Annotate what the voice should do

6 min

Our school should introduce this program. [pause] It would support students through a practical next step. [slightly stronger volume] I ask the school leaders here today to consider this proposal.
