ANNOTATED MODEL

Build one complete memory room

The old blue bicycle made visits to my cousin feel like expeditions. I would push hard up the first hill while its loose bell rattled with every bump. At the crest, the road opened towards the river and the wind cooled my face. My cousin always waited beside the red gate, pretending not to watch for me. By the time I arrived, the difficult climb already felt like part of the adventure. Even now, the sound of a bicycle bell brings that road back to me.
