THE FRAME

Four jobs in every paragraph

Listen for: the four parts. Every sentence must stay on one focus.

1

Topic sentence

Names the one focus of this paragraph.

2

Detail 1

A fact, action or image that develops the focus.

3

Detail 2

A second belonging detail that builds the picture.

4

Closing meaning

Leaves the reader with feeling, emphasis or reflection.
