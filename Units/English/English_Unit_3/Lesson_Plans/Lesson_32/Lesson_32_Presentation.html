BERANI • LESSON 32

A fair concern can strengthen your case.

Persuasive writers do not hide reasonable concerns. They name one fairly, respond with checked reasoning, then leave their audience with a clear next step.

MODEL

Read the model as a writer—not as a source.

6 min
