LESSON 29 | PERSUASIVE CONCLUSIONS

End with a future readers can picture.

Hope is persuasive when it is connected to a credible next step.

A good ending says what could happen and what to do first.

Think: What makes hope believable?

DO Invite a quick turn-and-talk; do not name the original text.

MODEL

Make hope believable.

Over-promise

The garden will fix every playground problem.

Credible possibility

The garden could help create another useful lunchtime choice.

Use conditional language when the outcome depends on a real next step: can help, could become, may allow.Use possible words: could, can help, may.

DO Think aloud: replace the certainty word.

WORK Whole class.

RECORD One possible-word replacement.

FINISH Students explain why “could” is not weak.

CHECK It is precise because it matches what the writer can honestly claim.
