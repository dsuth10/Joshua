MODEL

Model: a conclusion earns its hope

Return: Restate your opinion without copying the thesis.

Future: Show the worthwhile change your action could help create.

Call: Tell this audience the next realistic action.

Because ________, we should ________. This can help ________. Start by ________.
COMPARE

Which conclusion is more persuasive?

A“Our idea is good. Please agree with us.”
B“Our school should act on this proposal. By taking this next step, we can help create a fairer future. Begin by …”
PLAN

Plan your final move

My opinion, restatedThe future I want to make possibleThe action this audience can take now

Use only evidence you have checked. Keep the promise realistic.

STUDIO

Write and test the ending

Conclusion studio · 8 minutes

  1. Write 2–4 sentences that return to your opinion.
  2. Show the future your proposal could help create.
  3. End with a clear action for your audience.
  4. Underline the action; circle the hopeful future.
REHEARSE

Does the ending give the audience a next step?

Reader: “I hear your action is …”
Reader: “Your hopeful future is believable because …”
Writer: Revise one vague or over-promising phrase.
