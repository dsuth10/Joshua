ANNOTATED MODEL

Make the appeal fair and forceful

CORE

Uncle Kus should not keep Ginger Juice as a restaurant attraction because safety without freedom is not enough. Ari’s painting attempt is kind, yet it cannot give her room to climb or choose where to go. Ginger Juice’s narration makes the cost of captivity clear: when visitors hurt her, there is “nowhere to hide.” Uncle may believe food and protection show care, but he dismisses her needs and profits from her confinement. He should seek help that puts Ginger Juice’s welfare before the business.
