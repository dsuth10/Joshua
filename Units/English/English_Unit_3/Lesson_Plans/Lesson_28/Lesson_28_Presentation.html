MODEL

A plan holds evidence—not invented proof.

Reason: a specific benefit or argument.

Evidence note: an accurate paraphrase or short quotation from an identified source.

Source detail: enough information for a reader to trace it.

Honest gap: write needs checking if evidence is incomplete.

Models can show structure. Your assessment plan must use your own checked sources and notes.

STUDIO

Build the persuasive plan

Planning studio • 11 minutes

  • Identify your audience and action.
  • State a clear opinion.
  • Plan two or more developed reasons.
  • Attach evidence notes and source details.
  • Plan one fair concern and response.
  • End with what you want your audience to do.
