## 🔍 Spotting the Difference: Bias vs. Balance

```
  ⚠️ BIASED TEXT                       ⚖️ BALANCED TEXT
  • Uses ANGRY or BIG words            • Uses CALM and NEUTRAL words
  • Only tells ONE side                • Shows BOTH sides fairly
  • Says "ALWAYS" or "NEVER"           • Uses "MAY", "COULD", "SUGGESTS"
```

## 📝 Paragraph 1: Biased (One-Sided & Strong)

*Write 3 sentences using strong, one-sided words:*

1. **Sentence 1 (Stance):**  
   It is a complete ___________________ that ____________________________________________________.

2. **Sentence 2 (Reason):**  
   This is terrible because it completely ruins _________________________________________________.

3. **Sentence 3 (Demand):**  
   Everyone knows that we must immediately ___________________________________________________!

---

## 📝 Paragraph 2: Balanced (Fair & Calm)

*Write 3 sentences showing both sides with calm words:*

1. **Sentence 1 (Side 1):**  
   While some people argue that ________________________________________________________________,

2. **Sentence 2 (Side 2):**  
   others raise valid concerns about ___________________________________________________________.

3. **Sentence 3 (Conclusion):**  
   Therefore, evidence suggests that we could __________________________________________________.
