# Lesson 9 — Week 3

| | |
|---|---|
| **Book** | *Supper* (Alba Series, Book 9) |
| **Phonics Focus** | 'er' sound — er, ir, ur |
| **Writing Focus** | Setting description: What can you see, hear, feel? |
| **Week Theme** | Narrative Elements: Settings and Combining |

---

## Learning Intentions

- **Reading:** I am learning to read words where the /ɜː/ sound is spelled er, ir, or ur
- **Writing:** I am learning to describe a setting using my senses (see, hear, feel)
- **Spelling:** I am learning the different ways to spell the 'er' sound

## Success Criteria

- I can read words with er, ir, and ur in the story
- I can write a setting description using at least 2 senses
- I can spell at least 3 words with 'er' sound patterns correctly

---

## Reading (≈20 minutes)

### Introduction (5 min)
- "We've described Alba as a CHARACTER — what she looks like and what she's like. Now we're going to look at WHERE the story happens."
- Show *Supper*. "Where do you think Alba is? What time of day might it be?"
- **Phonics preview:**

| er | ir | ur |
|----|-----|-----|
| supper | bird | turn |
| her | stir | burn |
| water | girl | hurt |

- "These three spellings all make the same /ɜː/ sound."

### Guided Reading (10 min)
- Lucas reads *Supper* aloud.
- Prompt for 'er' sound words: "Which spelling is the /ɜː/ sound using here?"
- **Setting focus:** While reading, pause and ask:
  - "What can you see in this part of the story?"
  - "If you were there, what would you hear?"
  - "What would the air feel like?"

### Comprehension (5 min)
- **Setting description:** "Describe where Alba is in this story. Use as many details as you can."
- **Literal:** "What did Alba eat for supper? Where did she find it?"
- **Visualisation:** "Close your eyes. Imagine you are standing next to Alba. What do you see, hear, and feel?"

---

## Writing (≈25 minutes)

### Explicit Teaching: Setting Description (5 min)
- Explain: "A setting is WHERE and WHEN a story happens. Good writers describe settings so the reader can IMAGINE being there."
- Introduce the **Senses Organiser:**

| Sense | What might you notice? |
|-------|------------------------|
| 👁️ See | What does the place look like? Colours, shapes, objects |
| 👂 Hear | What sounds are there? Loud? Quiet? |
| 🖐️ Feel | Is it hot, cold, wet, windy? What textures? |

- Model: *"The kitchen was dark and small. Alba could hear water dripping. The floor felt cold under her feet."*
- Point out: one sentence per sense.

### Guided Writing (10 min)
- Together, describe the setting from *Supper* using the senses organiser.
- Fill in the organiser together:
  - **See:** dark room, small table, food on the bench
  - **Hear:** dripping, wind outside, quiet footsteps
  - **Feel:** cold floor, warm food, scared
- Convert each sense into a sentence together. Lucas writes with support.

### Independent Writing (10 min)
- Lucas writes his own 3-sentence setting description for a scene from *Supper*.
- **Requirement:** Include at least 2 different senses.
- Teacher aide prompts: "You've told me what you can see. Now tell me what you can hear or feel."
- Encourage adjectives and descriptive vocabulary.

---

## Spelling (≈15 minutes)

### Sound Sort (5 min)
- Teacher aide says words. Lucas writes each under the correct column (er, ir, or ur):
  - her, bird, turn, water, stir, burn, sister, girl, fur
- Discuss: "Is there a pattern for when we use each spelling?" (er is most common, ir and ur are trickier)

### Word Building (5 min)
- Build word chains:
  - **her → herd → bird → burn → turn → fur**
  - **stir → sir → fir → fur → fur**
- Lucas reads each word after building.

### Dictation (5 min)
- Dictate 5 words:
  1. supper
  2. bird
  3. turn
  4. her
  5. hurt
- Lucas writes, then underlines the 'er' sound spelling in each word.

---

## Differentiation

| If struggling | If progressing well |
|---------------|---------------------|
| Focus on er only; introduce ir/ur as exposure | Add smell and taste to the senses organiser |
| Provide sentence frames: "I can see ___. I can hear ___." | Challenge: describe a setting from an EARLIER Alba book without looking at the pictures |
| Dictate 3 words instead of 5 | Include less common spellings (e.g., *worm*, *work* — or words) |
