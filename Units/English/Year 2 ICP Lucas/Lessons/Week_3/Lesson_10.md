# Lesson 10 — Week 3

| | |
|---|---|
| **Book** | *Howling Wind* (Alba Series, Book 10) |
| **Phonics Focus** | 'ow' sound — ow, ou |
| **Writing Focus** | Combining character + setting in a paragraph |
| **Week Theme** | Narrative Elements: Settings and Combining |

---

## Learning Intentions

- **Reading:** I am learning to read words where the /aʊ/ sound is spelled ow or ou
- **Writing:** I am learning to combine a character description and a setting description in one paragraph
- **Spelling:** I am learning the two main ways to spell the 'ow' sound

## Success Criteria

- I can read words with ow and ou in the story
- I can write a paragraph that describes BOTH a character AND a setting
- I can spell at least 3 words with 'ow' sound patterns correctly

---

## Reading (≈20 minutes)

### Introduction (5 min)
- Show *Howling Wind*. "This sounds dramatic! What do you think happens?"
- Discuss what a howling wind sounds like. "Have you ever been outside in a really strong wind?"
- **Phonics preview:**

| ow | ou |
|----|-----|
| howl | loud |
| down | ground |
| town | sound |
| growl | found |

- "Both spellings make the /aʊ/ sound."

### Guided Reading (10 min)
- Lucas reads *Howling Wind* aloud.
- Prompt for /aʊ/ words: "Is the /aʊ/ sound spelled ow or ou in this word?"
- **Dual focus:** As reading, note BOTH:
  - What the setting is like (the wind, the weather, the location)
  - How Alba reacts (what she does, how she feels)

### Comprehension (5 min)
- **Setting + character combined:** "Describe where Alba is AND how she's feeling at the same time."
- **Inferential:** "How does the setting (the wind) affect what Alba does?"
- **Prediction:** "What do you think might happen next in the story?"

---

## Writing (≈25 minutes)

### Explicit Teaching: Combining Character + Setting (5 min)
- Explain: "Good stories don't just describe a place OR a person — they show how the character FITS into the setting."
- Model on the whiteboard:
  - Setting only: *"The wind was howling. It was very cold."*
  - Character only: *"Alba was scared. She was shivering."*
  - **Combined:** *"The wind howled around tiny Alba. She was scared and shivering as she crouched behind a rock."*
- "See how the combined version tells us about the place AND the character at the same time?"

### Guided Writing (10 min)
- Use a **Character-in-Setting Planner:**

| Setting detail | Character reaction |
|----------------|-------------------|
| Howling wind | Alba crouched down |
| Dark sky | She felt scared |
| Cold ground | She shivered |

- Together, convert each pair into a combined sentence:
  - *"The wind howled and Alba crouched down low."*
  - *"The sky was dark, and she felt scared."*
- Combine 2–3 sentences into a short paragraph.

### Independent Writing (10 min)
- Lucas writes a 3–4 sentence paragraph that describes a scene from *Howling Wind*.
- **Requirements:**
  - At least 1 sentence about the setting
  - At least 1 sentence about the character
  - At least 1 compound sentence using "and" or "but"
- Teacher aide observes the combination of elements and sentence structure.

---

## Spelling (≈15 minutes)

### ow vs ou Sort (5 min)
- Lucas sorts words into ow or ou:
  - howl, loud, down, ground, town, sound, growl, found, brown, shout
- Discuss: "Can you spot a pattern?" (ow often at end of word or before n/l; ou often in the middle before nd/t)

### Word Building (5 min)
- Build word chains:
  - **how → howl → growl → crowd → crown → brown → down → town**
  - **out → shout → loud →ound → found → ground**
- Lucas reads each word aloud.

### Dictation (5 min)
- Dictate 5 words:
  1. howl
  2. loud
  3. ground
  4. down
  5. shout
- After writing, Lucas labels each as ow or ou.

---

## Differentiation

| If struggling | If progressing well |
|---------------|---------------------|
| Write character and setting as separate sentences first, then try combining | Write 2 paragraphs: one for the beginning of the storm, one for the end |
| Focus on ow spelling only initially | Include tricky ow/ou words in dictation (e.g., *flower*, *mountain*) |
| Provide sentence frame: "The ___ was ___ and Alba felt ___." | Add dialogue: what would Alba say in this situation? |
