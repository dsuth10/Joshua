# Lesson 12 — Week 3

| | |
|---|---|
| **Book** | *The Truth* (Alba Series, Book 12) |
| **Phonics Focus** | 'oo' sound — oo, ew, u-e, u |
| **Writing Focus** | Combining all elements: character + setting + problem |
| **Week Theme** | Narrative Elements: Settings and Combining |

---

## Learning Intentions

- **Reading:** I am learning to read words where the long 'oo' sound is spelled oo, ew, or u-e
- **Writing:** I am learning to put character, setting, and events together into a short narrative paragraph
- **Spelling:** I am learning the different ways to spell the 'oo' sound

## Success Criteria

- I can read words with oo, ew, and u-e in the story
- I can write a paragraph that includes a character, a setting, and at least one event (problem)
- I can spell at least 3 words with 'oo' sound patterns correctly

---

## Reading (≈20 minutes)

### Introduction (5 min)
- "This is the last Alba book! Book 12 — *The Truth*. What do you think the truth might be?"
- Briefly recap the whole Alba storyline: "Alba was shrunk, she had to get the last apple pip to her dad. What happened along the way?"
- **Phonics preview:**

| oo | ew | u-e | u |
|-----|-----|------|---|
| truth | new | rude | ruby |
| food | grew | tube | super |
| moon | flew | June | music |

### Guided Reading (10 min)
- Lucas reads *The Truth* aloud.
- This is the final book — celebrate the achievement: "You've read all 12 Alba books!"
- Prompt for 'oo' sound words as they appear.
- **Story arc focus:** "How does the story end? Does Alba succeed? How does she feel?"

### Comprehension (5 min)
- **Summary:** "Can you summarise the whole Alba story — from Book 1 to Book 12 — in 4–5 sentences?"
- **Character growth:** "How has Alba changed from the beginning to the end?"
- **Opinion:** "What was your favourite part of Alba's adventure? Why?"

---

## Writing (≈25 minutes)

### Explicit Teaching: Putting It All Together (5 min)
- Review what we've learned about narrative writing:
  - **Character:** who, what they look like, what they're like (Weeks 2)
  - **Setting:** where and when, described using senses (Week 3, Lesson 9)
  - **Events/Problem:** what happens, how the character reacts (Week 3, Lesson 11)
- Show a model paragraph that combines all three:
  - *"Tiny, brave Alba stood at the edge of the dark river. The wind howled around her and the water was freezing cold. Suddenly, a big wave crashed towards her! Alba gasped, but she grabbed a stick and held on tight."*
- Label each element: character (Tiny, brave Alba), setting (dark river, howling wind), problem (big wave), reaction (grabbed a stick).

### Guided Writing (10 min)
- Together, plan a short narrative paragraph using the **Narrative Elements Planner:**

| Element | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Character** | Who? What are they like? |
| **Setting** | Where? When? What can you see/hear/feel? |
| **Problem** | What goes wrong? |
| **Reaction** | How does the character feel and respond? |

- Fill in about a scene from *The Truth* or a new imagined scene.
- Together, write 3–4 sentences that combine all elements into a paragraph.

### Independent Writing (10 min)
- Lucas writes his own short narrative paragraph (4–5 sentences).
- **Requirements:**
  - Introduce a character with at least 1 describing word
  - Describe the setting using at least 1 sense
  - Include a problem and a character reaction
  - Use at least 1 compound sentence
- **This is a key milestone** — Lucas is writing a mini-narrative for the first time.
- Teacher aide notes the quality as a baseline for Week 4 drafting.

## Spelling (≈15 minutes)

### Long 'oo' Sort (5 min)
- Sort words by spelling:

| oo | ew | u-e |
|----|-----|------|
| food | new | tube |
| moon | grew | June |
| room | flew | rude |
| tooth | drew | use |

- Discuss: "oo is the most common. ew often at the end. u-e is a split digraph like a-e and i-e."

### Word Building (5 min)
- Build word chains:
  - **moon → soon → spoon → spool → pool → cool → tool**
  - **new → few → flew → grew → drew**
  - **rude → rule → tube → tune → June**

### Dictation (5 min)
- Dictate 5 words:
  1. truth
  2. food
  3. new
  4. moon
  5. tube
- Lucas writes, then identifies the 'oo' sound spelling in each.

## Differentiation

| If struggling | If progressing well |
|---------------|---------------------|
| Provide the narrative planner pre-filled; Lucas writes sentences from it | Write a longer paragraph (6+ sentences) with multiple events |
| Reduce to 3 elements: character + setting only, add problem later | Include dialogue in the narrative paragraph |
| Focus on oo spelling only | Include all four 'oo' spellings in dictation |
