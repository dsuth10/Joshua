# Lesson 13 — Week 4

|---|---|

---

## Learning Intentions

- **Reading:** I am learning to apply my decoding skills to a new series of books
- **Writing:** I am learning to plan a narrative with a beginning, middle, and end before I start writing
- **Spelling:** I am revising CVC, CVCC, CCVC, and CCVCC spelling patterns

## Success Criteria

- I can read a new decodable text independently, applying phonics strategies
- I can complete a story map with a character, setting, problem, and solution
- I can spell CVC–CCVCC words with 80%+ accuracy in revision dictation

---

## Reading (≈20 minutes)

### Introduction to the New Series (5 min)
- "We've finished all 12 Alba books — well done! Now we're starting a new series."
- Show the new book. Discuss the cover, title, and any predictions.
- "We'll use the same reading strategies: blending sounds, looking for digraphs and long vowel patterns."

### Guided Reading (10 min)
- Lucas reads the new book aloud.
- Teacher aide observes: Which phonics patterns from the Alba series does Lucas apply automatically? Which does he still need prompting for?
- Praise transfer: "You read that digraph perfectly — just like in the Alba books!"

### Comprehension (5 min)
- **Narrative structure:** "Can you identify the beginning, middle, and end of this story?"
- **Character:** "Who is the main character? What are they like?"
- **Comparison:** "How is this story similar to or different from the Alba books?"

---

## Writing (≈25 minutes)

### Explicit Teaching: The Story Map (5 min)
- Explain: "Before writing a story, good writers PLAN first. Today we're going to make a story map."
- Draw the story map on the whiteboard:

```
┌──────────────────┐
│    CHARACTER      │  Who is in the story? What are they like?
└──────────────────┘
         │
┌──────────────────┐
│     SETTING       │  Where and when does it happen?
└──────────────────┘
         │
┌──────────────────┐
│    BEGINNING      │  How does the story start?
└──────────────────┘
         │
┌──────────────────┐
│  PROBLEM/MIDDLE   │  What goes wrong?
└──────────────────┘
         │
┌──────────────────┐
│  SOLUTION/END     │  How is the problem fixed? How does the story end?
└──────────────────┘
```

- Model filling in a story map for one of the Alba books as an example.

### Guided Planning (10 min)
- Together, plan Lucas's OWN story using the story map.
- Guide with questions:
  - "Who is your character? You could use Alba, or someone from the new books, or make up your own."
  - "Where does your story happen?"
  - "What problem will your character face?"
  - "How will they solve it?"
- Write brief notes/keywords in each box — NOT full sentences yet.

### Independent Planning (10 min)
- Lucas adds more detail to his story map independently.
- Encourage him to add:
  - An adjective for the character
  - A sensory detail for the setting
  - A feeling for the character when the problem happens
- Teacher aide supports but does not write for Lucas.
- **Save this story map** — it will be used in Lessons 14–16.

## Spelling (≈15 minutes)

### Revision Blitz (5 min)
- Quick-fire oral spelling: Teacher aide says a word, Lucas spells it aloud.
- Mix of all patterns from Weeks 1–3:
  - CVC: *got, ran, hid*
  - CCVC: *plan, grab, trip*
  - CCVCC: *stamp, drink, clump*
  - Digraphs: *chips, shop, catch*

### Word Sort Revision (5 min)
- Lucas sorts 12 words into categories:

| CVC | CCVC | CVCC | CCVCC |
|-----|------|------|-------|
| got | plan | lost | stamp |
| ran | grab | fast | drink |
| hid | trip | jump | clump |

### Dictation (5 min)
- Dictate 6 words (revision):
  1. plan
  2. stamp
  3. chips
  4. lost
  5. drink
  6. grab
- **Target:** 5/6 correct (80%+). Note any persistent errors for targeted revision.

## Differentiation

| If struggling | If progressing well |
|---------------|---------------------|
| Provide a partially completed story map to fill in gaps | Plan a story with TWO problems (complication + second complication) |
| Use Alba as the character (familiar context) | Create an original character with detailed personality traits |
| Reduce dictation to 4 words | Add 2 long vowel words to dictation (revision of ae, ee, oe, etc.) |
