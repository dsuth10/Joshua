# Lesson 7 — Week 2

| | |
|---|---|
| **Book** | *Apple Trees* (Alba Series, Book 7) |
| **Phonics Focus** | 'ee' sound — ee, ea, e, y, ey |
| **Writing Focus** | Character description: Personality and actions |
| **Week Theme** | Building Blocks: Recounting and Character Descriptions |

---

## Learning Intentions

- **Reading:** I am learning to read words where the long 'e' sound is spelled different ways (ee, ea, y)
- **Writing:** I am learning to describe what a character is like on the INSIDE — their personality and how they act
- **Spelling:** I am learning the different ways to spell the long 'e' sound

## Success Criteria

- I can read words with ee, ea, and y (as long 'e') in the story
- I can write 2–3 sentences about Alba's personality using evidence from the story
- I can spell at least 3 words with long 'e' patterns correctly

---

## Reading (≈20 minutes)

### Introduction (5 min)
- Recap: "What did we learn about Alba's appearance yesterday? Today we're going to think about what she's like on the INSIDE."
- Show *Apple Trees*. "This book is about apple trees. Remember the apple pip Alba is carrying? Why is it important?"
- **Phonics preview:** Write long 'e' word examples from the book:

| ee | ea | y | ey |
|----|-----|---|-----|
| tree | reach | happy | key |
| seed | eat | story | monkey |
| green | leaf | city | valley |

### Guided Reading (10 min)
- Lucas reads *Apple Trees* aloud.
- Prompt for long 'e' words: "What sound do those letters make together?"
- **Character tracking:** While reading, note on sticky notes or whiteboard:
  - "What did Alba DO?" (actions)
  - "How did Alba FEEL?" (emotions)
  - "What does this tell us about what kind of person she is?" (personality traits)

### Comprehension (5 min)
- **Character analysis:** "Is Alba determined? Kind? Clever? What makes you think that?"
- **Evidence:** "Can you point to a part of the story that shows us what Alba is like?"
- **Connection:** "Do you know anyone who is like Alba? How?"

---

## Writing (≈25 minutes)

### Explicit Teaching: Personality Description (5 min)
- Explain: "Yesterday we described what Alba LOOKS like. Today we describe what she IS like — her personality."
- Model the difference:
  - **Appearance:** *"Alba is small with brown hair."* (what we see)
  - **Personality:** *"Alba is brave and determined."* (what she's like inside)
- Show how to use EVIDENCE: *"Alba is brave because she keeps going even when she is scared."*
- Key pattern: **Character is [trait] because [evidence from story].**

### Guided Writing (10 min)
- Use a **Character Personality Organiser:**

| Personality trait | Evidence from the story |
|-------------------|------------------------|
| Brave | She escaped from danger |
| Clever | She made a plan |
| Determined | She keeps going even when it's hard |

- Together, turn each row into a sentence:
  - *"Alba is brave because she escaped from danger."*
  - *"She is clever because she made a plan."*
- Lucas writes with support, choosing his own words where possible.

### Independent Writing (10 min)
- Lucas writes 2–3 sentences about Alba's personality.
- **Prompt:** "What kind of person is Alba? Pick one or two words that describe her personality, and explain WHY."
- Encourage the pattern: ___ is [trait] because ___.
- **Word bank on whiteboard:** brave, kind, clever, tough, strong, determined, scared, smart

---

## Spelling (≈15 minutes)

### Long 'e' Sound Hunt (5 min)
- Go back through *Apple Trees* together. Lucas finds and writes down every word with a long 'e' sound.
- Sort them by spelling: ee, ea, y, or ey.
- Discuss: "Which spelling is most common? (ee and ea are the big ones)"

### Word Building (5 min)
- Build long 'e' word chains:
  - **tree → free → freeze → breeze** (ee)
  - **eat → beat → beam → dream** (ea)
  - **happy → puppy** (y)
- Emphasise: "ee is often in the middle, y is often at the end when it sounds like /ee/."

### Dictation (5 min)
- Dictate 5 words:
  1. tree
  2. eat
  3. green
  4. happy
  5. seed
- Lucas writes, then circles the long 'e' spelling in each word.

---

## Differentiation

| If struggling | If progressing well |
|---------------|---------------------|
| Focus on ee and ea only; save y for later | Include ey words in dictation (e.g., *key*, *valley*) |
| Provide the personality trait; Lucas finds the evidence | Write a comparison: "Alba is ___ but she is also ___" |
| Use sentence frame: "___ is ___ because ___." | Combine appearance + personality into a full character paragraph |
