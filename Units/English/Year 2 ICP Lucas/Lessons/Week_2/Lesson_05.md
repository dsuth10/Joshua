# Lesson 5 — Week 2

| | |
|---|---|
| **Book** | *A Bit of Luck* (Alba Series, Book 5) |
| **Phonics Focus** | ck, ng, qu, wh |
| **Writing Focus** | Recounting events using sequence words |
| **Week Theme** | Building Blocks: Recounting and Character Descriptions |

---

## Learning Intentions

- **Reading:** I am learning to read words ending in -ck and -ng, and words starting with qu- and wh-
- **Writing:** I am learning to recount events from a story using the words *first*, *next*, *then*, and *finally*
- **Spelling:** I am learning to spell words with -ck, -ng, qu-, and wh-

## Success Criteria

- I can read -ck and -ng words fluently in the story
- I can write a 3–4 sentence recount using at least 2 sequence words
- I can spell at least 4 words with the target patterns correctly

---

## Reading (≈20 minutes)

### Introduction (5 min)
- Recap Alba's journey so far: "What's happened in Books 1–4?"
- Show *A Bit of Luck*. "What does 'a bit of luck' mean? When have you been lucky?"
- **Phonics preview:** Write on whiteboard: **luck, quick, bang, thing, whack**
- Teach: "-ck makes the /k/ sound at the end; -ng makes the /ŋ/ sound; qu- says /kw/; wh- says /w/."

### Guided Reading (10 min)
- Lucas reads *A Bit of Luck* aloud.
- Prompt for -ck words: "You see c and k together at the end — they make one /k/ sound."
- Prompt for -ng words: "Those two letters make the /ŋ/ sound — like in 'sing'."
- After reading, list the events on the whiteboard in order (teacher aide writes as Lucas tells).

### Comprehension (5 min)
- **Sequencing:** "What happened first in this story? What happened next? How did it end?"
- **Inferential:** "Why was Alba lucky? What could have happened if she wasn't lucky?"
- **Vocabulary:** "What does ___ mean in this sentence?" (choose a word from the text)

---

## Writing (≈25 minutes)

### Explicit Teaching: Recount Writing (5 min)
- Explain: "A recount tells what happened in order. We use special words to show the order."
- Write the sequence words on the whiteboard: **First, Next, Then, Finally**
- Model a simple recount of *A Bit of Luck*:
  - *"First, Alba went to find help. Next, she heard a bang. Then, she hid behind a rock. Finally, she was safe."*
- Point out: "Each sentence starts with a sequence word. The reader knows exactly what happened and when."

### Guided Writing (10 min)
- Together, plan a recount of the story using a simple 4-box template:

| First | Next | Then | Finally |
|-------|------|------|---------|
| | | | |

- Lucas tells what happened at each stage. Teacher aide writes keywords in each box.
- Together, turn each box into a full sentence. Lucas writes with support.

### Independent Writing (10 min)
- Lucas writes his own 3–4 sentence recount of *A Bit of Luck*.
- **Scaffold:** The 4-box template and sequence words stay visible.
- Remind: "Start each sentence with a sequence word. Don't forget your capitals and full stops."
- Teacher aide supports spelling but encourages Lucas to "have a go" first.

---

## Spelling (≈15 minutes)

### Pattern Sort (5 min)
- Lucas sorts words into columns:

| -ck | -ng | qu- | wh- |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| luck | bang | quick | when |
| back | ring | quit | what |
| kick | long | quiz | which |

- Read each word aloud after sorting.

### Word Building (5 min)
- Build word chains with letter tiles:
  - **luck → lick → kick → wick → wick → quick**
  - **bang → rang → ring → king → thing**
- Ask: "Which sounds are the same? Which changed?"

### Dictation (5 min)
- Dictate 5 words:
  1. luck
  2. bang
  3. quick
  4. when
  5. thing
- Lucas writes, then checks by segmenting each word aloud.

---

## Differentiation

| If struggling | If progressing well |
|---------------|---------------------|
| Provide sentence starters: "First, Alba ___." | Write a 5–6 sentence recount with additional detail |
| Focus on -ck and -ng only; reduce qu/wh | Add descriptive words to the recount (adjectives from last week) |
| Use the 4-box template as a direct copy scaffold | Challenge: "Can you recount the story from Alba's point of view?" |
