# Lesson 6 — Week 2

| | |
|---|---|
| **Book** | *A Brave Escape* (Alba Series, Book 6) |
| **Phonics Focus** | 'ae' sound — ai, ay, a-e, a |
| **Writing Focus** | Character description: Appearance |
| **Week Theme** | Building Blocks: Recounting and Character Descriptions |

---

## Learning Intentions

- **Reading:** I am learning to read words where the long 'a' sound is spelled different ways (ai, ay, a-e)
- **Writing:** I am learning to describe what a character looks like using adjectives
- **Spelling:** I am learning the different ways to spell the long 'a' sound

## Success Criteria

- I can read words with ai, ay, and a-e in the story
- I can write 2–3 sentences describing what Alba looks like
- I can spell at least 3 words with long 'a' patterns correctly

---

## Reading (≈20 minutes)

### Introduction (5 min)
- Show the cover of *A Brave Escape*. "What does 'brave' mean? What do you think Alba is escaping from?"
- **Phonics focus:** Explain that the 'ae' sound (long 'a') can be spelled different ways.
- Write examples on the whiteboard in columns:

| ai | ay | a-e |
|----|-----|------|
| rain | day | brave |
| wait | play | escape |
| train | stay | safe |

- "These all have the same /eɪ/ sound, but they're spelled differently."

### Guided Reading (10 min)
- Lucas reads *A Brave Escape* aloud.
- When encountering a long 'a' word, pause: "What spelling of the /eɪ/ sound is in this word?"
- Highlight the character descriptions in the text: "What words does the author use to tell us about Alba?"
- Note any adjectives or descriptions of Alba as they appear.

### Comprehension (5 min)
- **Character focus:** "What did Alba do in this story that was brave? What does that tell us about her?"
- **Literal:** "How did Alba escape?"
- **Inferential:** "Would you describe Alba as a brave character? Why or why not?"

---

## Writing (≈25 minutes)

### Explicit Teaching: Character Description — Appearance (5 min)
- Explain: "When we describe a character, we can tell the reader what they LOOK like."
- Model with Alba. Draw a simple stick figure and label:
  - **Hair:** What colour? What style?
  - **Size:** How big/small?
  - **Clothes:** What is she wearing?
  - **Special features:** What makes her different?
- Write a modelled description: *"Alba is very small — only 10 centimetres tall. She has brown hair and wears a red jacket."*

### Guided Writing (10 min)
- Use a **Character Appearance Organiser** (teacher aide draws on the whiteboard):

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Size | |
| Hair | |
| Clothes | |
| Special features | |

- Together, fill in details about Alba based on illustrations in the books.
- Convert each row into a sentence together:
  - "Alba is ___." / "She has ___." / "She wears ___."

### Independent Writing (10 min)
- Lucas writes his own 2–3 sentence description of Alba's appearance.
- Encourage adjectives from Week 1: "Remember our describing words? Use them here!"
- **Prompt:** "Tell the reader what Alba looks like. Imagine someone has never seen her."
- Teacher aide notes whether Lucas includes adjectives and writes in complete sentences.

---

## Spelling (≈15 minutes)

### Sound Same, Spelled Different (5 min)
- Teacher aide says a word. Lucas identifies the long 'a' sound, then decides the spelling:
  - "rain" — where is the /eɪ/ sound? — ai in the middle
  - "play" — ay at the end
  - "brave" — a, then consonant, then e (a-e)
- Rule of thumb: ai often in the middle, ay often at the end of a word

### Word Building (5 min)
- Build long 'a' words with letter tiles:
  - **rain → train → trail → tail → tale**
  - **day → play → stay → stray**
  - **brave → gave → cave → came → game**

### Dictation (5 min)
- Dictate 5 words:
  1. brave
  2. rain
  3. play
  4. safe
  5. wait
- After writing, Lucas labels the long 'a' spelling in each word (underline ai, ay, or a-e).

---

## Differentiation

| If struggling | If progressing well |
|---------------|---------------------|
| Focus on ai and a-e only (save ay for revision later) | Include the less common "a" spelling (e.g., *apron*, *baby*) |
| Provide the character organiser pre-filled; Lucas writes sentences from it | Write a character description of a second character from the books |
| Use illustrations as direct visual prompts for description | Add feelings/personality hints: "Alba looks brave because ___" |
