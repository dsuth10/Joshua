# Lesson 2 — Week 1

| | |
|---|---|
| **Book** | *The Plan* (Alba Series, Book 2) |
| **Phonics Focus** | CVC, CVCC, CCVC words (initial consonant clusters) |
| **Writing Focus** | Simple sentences with detail (who + what) |
| **Week Theme** | Foundations: Sentence Writing |

---

## Learning Intentions

- **Reading:** I am learning to read words with initial consonant blends (e.g., *plan*, *grab*, *trip*)
- **Writing:** I am learning to add detail to my sentences by saying who and what
- **Spelling:** I am learning to blend and spell CCVC words

## Success Criteria

- I can read CCVC words in the story by blending the initial consonant cluster
- I can write a sentence that tells who did something and what they did
- I can spell at least 3 CCVC words correctly

---

## Reading (≈20 minutes)

### Review and Introduction (5 min)
- Quick retell of *Lost*: "What happened to Alba in the last book?"
- Show the cover of *The Plan*. "Alba has a plan! What do you think her plan might be?"
- **Word preview:** Write 3 CCVC words from the book on a whiteboard: *plan, grab, trip*
- Model blending: "p-l together make /pl/, then /a/, then /n/ — plan."

### Guided Reading (10 min)
- Lucas reads *The Plan* aloud.
- When encountering CCVC words, prompt: "What are the first two sounds together? Now blend them with the rest."
- Praise accurate decoding: "Great blending! You put those sounds together really well."
- If Lucas reads a word incorrectly, ask: "Does that make sense? Let's look at the sounds again."

### Comprehension (5 min)
- **Literal:** "What is Alba's plan?"
- **Sequencing:** "What happened first? What happened next?"
- **Connection:** "Have you ever made a plan to do something? What was it?"

---

## Writing (≈25 minutes)

### Modelled Writing (5 min)
- Teacher aide writes a sentence about the story, emphasising who + what:
  - *"Alba made a plan."*
- Think aloud: "I'm telling the reader WHO — Alba — and WHAT she did — made a plan."
- Compare with Lesson 1: "This sentence tells us more. It tells us what she did, not just what she is."

### Shared Writing (10 min)
- Together, compose 2 sentences, with Lucas contributing ideas:
  - **Prompt:** "Who else is in the story? What did they do?"
  - e.g., *"Alba grabbed the map. She ran to the shop."*
- For each sentence, ask: "Who are we writing about? What did they do?"
- Lucas writes each word as they compose together. Teacher aide supports spelling of tricky words.

### Independent Writing (10 min)
- Lucas writes 2 sentences about *The Plan* independently.
- **Prompt card:**
  - Who? ___________
  - What did they do? ___________
- Remind: "Start with a capital. End with a full stop. Use finger spaces."
- Teacher aide circulates, praising attempts and noting spelling patterns used.

---

## Spelling (≈15 minutes)

### Consonant Blend Warm-Up (3 min)
- Teacher aide says initial blends, Lucas repeats and identifies the two letter sounds:
  - /pl/ — p, l → /pr/ — p, r → /gr/ — g, r → /tr/ — t, r → /bl/ — b, l

### Word Building with Blends (7 min)
- Using letter tiles, build CCVC words from the book:
  - **plan → plop → plot → blot → blob**
  - **grab → gran → grin → grip → trip**
- Lucas reads each word after building it.
- Ask: "What changed? Which sound is different?"

### Dictation (5 min)
- Teacher aide dictates 5 words (mix of CVC and CCVC):
  1. plan
  2. grab
  3. trip
  4. got (revision)
  5. stop
- After writing, Lucas checks each word by tapping out the sounds on the table.

---

## Differentiation

| If struggling | If progressing well |
|---------------|---------------------|
| Pre-teach blends orally before reading | Include CCVCC words in dictation (e.g., *stamp*, *blend*) |
| Provide letter tiles for writing support | Ask Lucas to write 3 sentences with a connective: "and" |
| Reduce dictation to 3 words | Challenge: "Can you think of another word that starts with /pl/?" |
