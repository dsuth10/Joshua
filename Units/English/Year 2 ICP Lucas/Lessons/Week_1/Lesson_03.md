# Lesson 3 — Week 1

| | |
|---|---|
| **Book** | *Alba Slips Up* (Alba Series, Book 3) |
| **Phonics Focus** | CVC, CVCC, CCVC, CCVCC words |
| **Writing Focus** | Expanding sentences with adjectives |
| **Week Theme** | Foundations: Sentence Writing |

---

## Learning Intentions

- **Reading:** I am learning to read longer words with consonant clusters at the beginning and end
- **Writing:** I am learning to make my sentences more interesting by adding describing words (adjectives)
- **Spelling:** I am learning to blend and spell CCVCC words

## Success Criteria

- I can blend CCVCC words (e.g., *slips*, *stamp*, *drink*) while reading
- I can write a sentence that includes at least one adjective
- I can spell at least 3 CCVCC words correctly

---

## Reading (≈20 minutes)

### Review and Introduction (5 min)
- Retell chain: "Tell me one thing that happened in *Lost* and one thing from *The Plan*."
- Show the cover of *Alba Slips Up*. Discuss: "What does 'slips up' mean? Has that ever happened to you?"
- **Word preview:** Write on the whiteboard: *slips, stamp, drink, clump*
- Model: "sl-i-p-s — I blend the beginning /sl/, then /ɪ/, then /ps/ at the end."

### Guided Reading (10 min)
- Lucas reads *Alba Slips Up* aloud.
- For CCVCC words, guide: "Find the vowel in the middle first. Now blend the sounds before it, then after it."
- Encourage self-correction: "You said ___. Does that sound right? Look at the word again."
- After the first read, choose one page to re-read for expression: "How would Alba sound when she says this?"

### Comprehension (5 min)
- **Literal:** "What did Alba slip on? What happened after she slipped?"
- **Inferential:** "Was this part of her plan? How do you think she felt?"
- **Connection to character:** "What kind of person is Alba? Is she brave, scared, clever?" Begin a simple character trait discussion.

---

## Writing (≈25 minutes)

### Explicit Teaching: Adjectives (5 min)
- Explain: "An adjective is a describing word. It tells us more about a thing or a person."
- Show two sentences:
  - *"Alba fell."*
  - *"Little Alba fell on the wet ground."*
- Ask: "Which sentence gives you a better picture in your head? What words helped?"
- Underline the adjectives: **little**, **wet**.

### Guided Writing (10 min)
- Together, upgrade simple sentences from the story by adding adjectives:
  - *"Alba ran."* → *"Tiny Alba ran fast."*
  - *"The path was long."* → *"The dark path was very long."*
- **Brainstorm adjectives together:** Write a list of useful adjectives on the whiteboard (small, big, fast, dark, wet, cold, hot, old, sad, glad).
- Lucas chooses which adjective fits and writes the expanded sentence.

### Independent Writing (10 min)
- Lucas writes 2–3 sentences about *Alba Slips Up*, trying to include at least one adjective in each.
- **Prompt:** "Write about what happened to Alba. Try to use a describing word."
- **Word bank available:** The adjective list stays on the whiteboard for reference.
- Teacher aide notes which adjectives Lucas uses and whether they enhance meaning.

---

## Spelling (≈15 minutes)

### Segmenting Challenge (5 min)
- Teacher aide says CCVCC words aloud. Lucas uses fingers to count the sounds:
  - *slips* (4 sounds: s-l-i-ps) → discuss how -ps is two sounds
  - *stamp* (5 sounds: s-t-a-m-p)
  - *drink* (5 sounds: d-r-i-n-k)
  - *clump* (5 sounds: c-l-u-m-p)
- Correct together if Lucas miscounts: "Let's say it slowly and tap each sound."

### Word Building (5 min)
- Using letter tiles, build CCVCC words:
  - **slip → slips → clips → claps → clamp → clump**
- Emphasise: "We're changing one sound at a time. Which sound changed?"
- Lucas reads each word aloud after building.

### Dictation (5 min)
- Dictate 5 words (building on previous lessons):
  1. slips
  2. stamp
  3. drink
  4. plan (revision)
  5. lost (revision)
- After writing, Lucas re-reads each word to check it looks right.

---

## Differentiation

| If struggling | If progressing well |
|---------------|---------------------|
| Provide the adjective orally for Lucas to add to a sentence frame | Ask Lucas to use 2 adjectives in one sentence (e.g., "The small, wet path") |
| Reduce CCVCC dictation to 3 words + 2 CVC revision | Add a sentence-combining challenge: join 2 sentences with "and" |
| Use sound boxes (Elkonin boxes) for segmenting | Ask: "Can you think of a word that rhymes with *stamp*?" |
