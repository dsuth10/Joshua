# Lesson 1 — Week 1

| | |
|---|---|
| **Book** | *Lost* (Alba Series, Book 1) |
| **Phonics Focus** | CVC and CVCC words |
| **Writing Focus** | Simple sentences (subject + verb) |
| **Week Theme** | Foundations: Sentence Writing |

---

## Learning Intentions

- **Reading:** I am learning to read CVC and CVCC words accurately in a story
- **Writing:** I am learning to write a simple sentence with a capital letter and a full stop
- **Spelling:** I am learning to hear and write the sounds in CVC words

## Success Criteria

- I can decode CVC and CVCC words while reading *Lost*
- I can write at least 2 simple sentences about the story
- I can segment and spell at least 5 CVC words correctly

---

## Reading (≈20 minutes)

### Book Introduction (5 min)
- Show the cover of *Lost*. Read the title together.
- **Predict:** "What do you think this book might be about? Who might be lost?"
- Introduce Alba briefly: "This is Alba. She's been shrunk to just 10 centimetres tall! In this story, she's lost."
- Picture walk: Flip through the pages, discussing what Lucas notices in the illustrations.

### Guided Reading (10 min)
- Lucas reads the text aloud with teacher aide support.
- **Decoding strategy:** When Lucas gets stuck, prompt: "Look at the sounds. What's the first sound? The middle? The end?"
- Support blending of CVC words (e.g., *l-o-s-t*, *r-a-n*, *g-o-t*).
- For CVCC words, model blending: "Let's blend all four sounds together."
- Re-read for fluency after decoding tricky words.

### Comprehension (5 min)
- **Literal:** "Where is Alba? What happened to her?"
- **Inferential:** "How do you think Alba feels? How do you know?"
- **Retell:** "Can you tell me what happened in the story in your own words?"

---

## Writing (≈25 minutes)

### Modelled Writing (5 min)
- Teacher aide models writing a simple sentence about the book:
  - *"Alba is lost."*
- Think aloud: "I start with a capital letter for the name. I leave a finger space. I finish with a full stop."
- Point out: subject (Alba) + verb (is) + extra detail (lost).

### Guided Writing (10 min)
- Together, compose a second sentence: *"She ran fast."*
- Lucas contributes the words while teacher aide guides:
  - "Who are we writing about? What did she do?"
  - Remind: capital letter, finger spaces, full stop.
- Write the sentence together on a whiteboard first, then Lucas copies into his book.

### Independent Writing (10 min)
- Lucas writes 1–2 simple sentences about *Lost* on his own.
- **Prompt:** "Write a sentence about what Alba did in the story."
- **Scaffold:** Provide a sentence starter if needed: *"Alba ___."*
- Teacher aide observes, prompts for capitals and full stops, but allows Lucas to attempt spelling independently first.

---

## Spelling (≈15 minutes)

### Sound Talk (5 min)
- Oral segmenting: Teacher aide says a CVC word, Lucas identifies each sound.
- Words from the book: **got, ran, hid, big, top, lost**
- Use fingers to tap out each sound: "g-o-t — got."

### Word Building (5 min)
- Using letter tiles or magnetic letters, Lucas builds CVC words:
  - **got → hot → lot → lost** (word chain — change one sound at a time)
  - **ran → man → map → tap**
- Discuss: "Which sound changed? Which letter did we swap?"

### Dictation (5 min)
- Teacher aide dictates 5 CVC/CVCC words for Lucas to write:
  1. got
  2. ran
  3. big
  4. lost
  5. hid
- Lucas writes each word, then checks by tapping out the sounds.

---

## Differentiation

| If struggling | If progressing well |
|---------------|---------------------|
| Reduce to 3 dictation words | Add a CVCC word to dictation (e.g., *fast*, *just*) |
| Provide a fully modelled sentence to copy | Ask Lucas to write 3 sentences independently |
| Focus on 2-sound words first, then build to 3 | Encourage adding an adjective (e.g., "Alba is very lost.") |
