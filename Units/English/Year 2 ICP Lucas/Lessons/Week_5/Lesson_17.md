---

## Learning Intentions

- **Reading:** I am learning to read like a writer — noticing how authors describe characters in interesting ways
- **Writing:** I am learning to revise my story by adding better descriptions and more interesting words
- **Spelling:** I am learning to proofread my own writing for spelling

## Success Criteria

- I can identify one technique the author uses to describe a character in today's book
- I can add at least 2 improvements to my story draft (a better describing word, more detail, or a feeling)
- I can find and correct at least 2 spelling mistakes in my own writing

---

## Reading (≈20 minutes)

### Introduction (5 min)
- "This week, we're making your story the BEST it can be. Today is about REVISING — making it better."
- "While we read today, look for how the author describes characters. What words do they use? Can we steal any tricks?"

### Guided Reading (10 min)
- Lucas reads the book aloud.
- **Writer's notebook approach:** Pause at descriptive moments:
  - "That's a great describing word. Let's write it down."
  - "Notice how the author shows us the character is scared without SAYING 'scared'."
- Keep a running list of "good words and phrases" on the whiteboard.

### Comprehension — Author's Craft (5 min)
- "What words did the author use to describe the character? Which one was your favourite?"
- "How did the author SHOW us the character's feelings instead of just telling us?"
- "Is there any word or phrase from this book we could use in YOUR story?"

---

## Writing (≈25 minutes)

### Explicit Teaching: Revision Strategies (5 min)
- Explain: "Revising means making your writing BETTER — not fixing mistakes (that's editing, which we do tomorrow)."
- Three revision strategies:
  1. **Add a describing word:** *"the dog"* → *"the big, angry dog"*
  2. **Show, don't tell:** *"Alba was scared"* → *"Alba's hands were shaking"*
  3. **Add a feeling:** *"She ran away"* → *"She ran away, her heart pounding"*
- Model revising one sentence from the Milo example.

### Guided Revision (10 min)
- Lucas retrieves his complete draft from Week 4.
- Read the whole story aloud together.
- Teacher aide asks revision questions for each section:
  - **Beginning:** "Can we add a better describing word for your character?"
  - **Middle:** "Can we show how the character feels instead of just saying it?"
  - **Ending:** "Can we add one more sentence about how the character feels at the end?"
- Lucas marks revisions on his draft using a different coloured pencil (additions above the line or in the margins).

### Independent Revision (10 min)
- Lucas re-reads his story one more time and makes at least 1 more improvement on his own.
- **Check against success criteria:**
  - Does the character have at least one describing word?
  - Does the story include a feeling or emotion?
  - Is there at least one "interesting" word (not just basic vocabulary)?
- Teacher aide notes the revisions for the editing session tomorrow.

---

## Spelling (≈15 minutes)

### Proofreading Own Writing (10 min)
- Lucas reads through his story draft slowly, pointing at each word.
- Circle any words that "don't look right."
- Together, check circled words:
  - Try writing the word 2–3 different ways to see which looks correct
  - Use phonics knowledge: "What pattern should this word use?"
  - Teacher aide confirms the correct spelling; Lucas writes it correctly above the line.

### Personal Spelling List (5 min)
- From the proofreading, create a **Personal Spelling List** of 5–8 words Lucas misspelled in his story.
- These will be practised in Lessons 18 and 19's spelling segments.
- Write the list clearly; Lucas keeps it for reference.

---

## Differentiation

| If struggling | If progressing well |
|---------------|---------------------|
| Teacher aide suggests specific revisions; Lucas chooses which to add | Revise for sentence variety: "Can you start a sentence a different way?" |
| Focus on adding ONE adjective and ONE feeling word | Add simile or comparison: "The wind howled like a wolf" |
| Limit proofreading to the beginning section only | Proofread the entire draft independently before checking with teacher aide |
