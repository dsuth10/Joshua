---

## Learning Intentions

- **Reading:** I am learning to read for punctuation — noticing how authors use full stops, commas, and capital letters to help the reader
- **Writing:** I am learning to edit my story for punctuation, capital letters, and sentence structure
- **Spelling:** I am learning to correct spelling errors from my own draft

## Success Criteria

- I can identify full stops, capital letters, and commas in today's book
- I can edit my draft to include correct punctuation in every sentence
- I can correctly spell at least 4 words from my personal spelling list

---

## Reading (≈20 minutes)

### Introduction (5 min)
- "Yesterday we revised our stories to make them more interesting. Today we're EDITING — fixing the technical stuff: punctuation, capitals, and spelling."
- "While we read today, I want you to notice the punctuation marks."

### Guided Reading (10 min)
- Lucas reads the book aloud.
- **Punctuation focus:** When Lucas encounters punctuation, pause:
  - "What does this full stop tell you to do?" (pause, end of sentence)
  - "Why is there a capital letter here?" (start of sentence, name)
  - "What does this comma do?" (short pause, list)
- Count punctuation marks on one page together.

### Comprehension (5 min)
- "How does the punctuation help you read the story?" (phrasing, fluency, meaning)
- "What would happen if there were no full stops?" (confusion, run-on)
- Read one sentence WITH punctuation, then WITHOUT, and compare.

---

## Writing (≈25 minutes)

### Explicit Teaching: Editing Checklist (5 min)
- Introduce the **Editing Checklist:**

| ✅ Check | What to look for |
|----------|-----------------|
| ✏️ Capital letters | Every sentence starts with one; names have capitals |
| ⏹️ Full stops | Every sentence ends with a full stop (or ! or ?) |
| ✋ Finger spaces | Clear spaces between every word |
| 🔗 Compound sentences | "and", "but", "so" used correctly; no run-on sentences |
| 📝 Spelling | Words look right; phonics patterns correct |

- Model editing one paragraph using the checklist.

### Guided Editing (10 min)
- Lucas retrieves his revised draft from Lesson 17.
- Go through the story sentence by sentence:
  - **Teacher aide reads each sentence aloud.** Lucas checks:
    - Does it start with a capital?
    - Does it end with punctuation?
    - Are there finger spaces?
    - Is any spelling incorrect?
  - Mark corrections using a coloured pencil.
- Pay special attention to compound sentences: check that "and"/"but" are used correctly and there's no misplaced full stop.

### Independent Editing (10 min)
- Lucas continues editing the rest of his story independently using the checklist.
- Teacher aide observes but allows Lucas to self-correct first.
- After independent editing, teacher aide does a final check and notes any remaining errors for Lucas to fix.

---

## Spelling (≈15 minutes)

### Personal Spelling List Practice (5 min)
- Retrieve Lucas's personal spelling list from Lesson 17.
- **Look, say, cover, write, check** for each word:
  1. Look at the word carefully
  2. Say it aloud, segmenting the sounds
  3. Cover the word
  4. Write it from memory
  5. Check — does it match?

### Spelling Corrections in Draft (5 min)
- Go through any remaining misspelled words in the edited draft.
- Lucas writes each corrected word three times.
- Add any new misspelled words to the personal spelling list.

### Dictation — Personal Words (5 min)
- Teacher aide dictates 5 words from Lucas's personal spelling list in sentences:
  - e.g., *"The bird flew over the tree."*
- Lucas writes the full sentence, focusing on correct spelling of the target word.

---

## Differentiation

| If struggling | If progressing well |
|---------------|---------------------|
| Focus on capitals and full stops only (save commas for extension) | Add comma editing: check for commas in lists and after time connectives |
| Edit one paragraph at a time, not the whole story | Self-edit the entire story before teacher aide checks |
| Reduce personal spelling list to 3 words | Add challenge words to personal spelling list |
