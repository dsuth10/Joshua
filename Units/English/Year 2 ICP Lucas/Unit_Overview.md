---

## Unit Context

Lucas requires intensive support in English, currently operating at the Beginning/Exploring range of the Year 2 achievement standard. This unit provides daily one-hour sessions with a teacher aide, building foundational literacy skills through a structured reading–writing–spelling cycle. Each lesson uses decodable readers (Alba Series, then a second series) to anchor all three elements, ensuring phonics, comprehension, and composition develop together.

The final assessment requires Lucas to write a narrative using a known character, demonstrating:
- Sequenced events with a beginning, middle, and end
- Punctuated simple and compound sentences
- Topic-specific vocabulary
- Legible handwriting
- Regular spelling patterns with attempts at less common patterns

## Scope and Sequence

### Week 1 — Foundations: Sentence Writing

| Lesson | Book | Phonics Focus | Reading Focus | Writing Focus | Spelling Focus |
|--------|------|---------------|---------------|---------------|----------------|
| 1 | *Lost* | CVC, CVCC | Predicting, decoding CVC words, retelling | Writing simple sentences (subject + verb) | CVC word building and segmenting |
| 2 | *The Plan* | CVC, CVCC, CCVC | Sequencing events, identifying characters | Writing simple sentences with detail (who, what) | CCVC blends — initial consonant clusters |
| 3 | *Alba Slips Up* | CVC, CVCC, CCVC, CCVCC | Making connections, reading with expression | Expanding sentences with adjectives | CCVCC words — blending and segmenting |
| 4 | *Hot Chips for Lunch* | sh, ch, th, tch | Identifying the problem in the story | Writing compound sentences using "and", "but" | Consonant digraphs — sh, ch, th, tch |

**Learning Intentions — Week 1:**
- Read decodable texts with increasing accuracy and fluency
- Write simple and compound sentences with correct boundary punctuation
- Identify and spell CVC, CVCC, and CCVC words; recognise consonant digraphs

**Success Criteria — Week 1:**
- Reads CVC and CCVC words accurately in context
- Writes at least 2 sentences with a capital letter and full stop
- Segments and spells CVC/CCVC words correctly in isolation

### Week 2 — Building Blocks: Recounting and Character Descriptions

| Lesson | Book | Phonics Focus | Reading Focus | Writing Focus | Spelling Focus |
|--------|------|---------------|---------------|---------------|----------------|
| 5 | *A Bit of Luck* | ck, ng, qu, wh | Retelling events in order | Recounting: What happened first, next, then, finally | Words ending in -ck, -ng; wh- and qu- words |
| 6 | *A Brave Escape* | 'ae': ai, ay, a-e, a | Identifying character traits from actions | Character description: What does Alba look like? | Long 'a' spellings — ai, ay, a-e |
| 7 | *Apple Trees* | 'ee': ee, ea, e, y, ey | Inferring feelings from text evidence | Character description: How does Alba feel and act? | Long 'e' spellings — ee, ea, y |
| 8 | *The Long Road Home* | 'oe': o-e, ow, oa, o | Comparing events across Alba books | Recount with character focus: retelling from Alba's point of view | Long 'o' spellings — o-e, oa, ow |

**Learning Intentions — Week 2:**
- Use sequence words (first, next, then, finally) to recount events
- Describe a character's appearance, feelings, and actions using adjectives and verbs
- Read and spell words with long vowel patterns (ae, ee, oe)

**Success Criteria — Week 2:**
- Writes a 3–4 sentence recount using time-order words
- Includes at least 2 descriptive words when writing about a character
- Correctly spells at least 3 words with the target long vowel pattern

### Week 3 — Narrative Elements: Settings and Combining

| Lesson | Book | Phonics Focus | Reading Focus | Writing Focus | Spelling Focus |
|--------|------|---------------|---------------|---------------|----------------|
| 9 | *Supper* | 'er': er, ir, ur | Identifying where and when the story takes place | Setting description: What can you see, hear, feel? | 'er' sound spellings — er, ir, ur |
| 10 | *Howling Wind* | 'ow': ow, ou | Using text clues to visualise the setting | Combining character + setting in a paragraph | 'ow' sound spellings — ow, ou |
| 11 | *River Flight* | 'ie': igh, i-e, y, i | Identifying the problem and solution in the story | Story events: Writing a problem for a character | Long 'i' spellings — igh, i-e, y |
| 12 | *The Truth* | 'oo': oo, ew, u-e, u | Summarising the whole Alba story arc | Combining all elements: character + setting + problem | 'oo' sound spellings — oo, ew, u-e |

**Learning Intentions — Week 3:**
- Describe settings using sensory language (see, hear, feel)
- Identify and write story problems and solutions
- Combine character, setting, and events into a short paragraph
- Read and spell words with 'er', 'ow', 'ie', and 'oo' vowel patterns

**Success Criteria — Week 3:**
- Writes a setting description using at least 2 senses
- Writes a short paragraph that includes a character, a setting, and an event
- Correctly spells at least 3 words with the target vowel pattern

### Week 4 — Drafting: Planning and Writing Short Narratives

| Lesson | Book | Phonics Focus | Reading Focus | Writing Focus | Spelling Focus |
|--------|------|---------------|---------------|---------------|----------------|
| 13 | *Second Series — Book 1* | Revision: CVC–CCVCC | Narrative structure: beginning, middle, end | Planning a narrative using a story map | Revision: CVC, CVCC, CCVC words |
| 14 | *Second Series — Book 2* | Revision: consonant digraphs | Character motivation: Why does the character act? | Drafting the beginning: introducing character + setting | Revision: sh, ch, th, ck, ng |
| 15 | *Second Series — Book 3* | Revision: long vowels (ae, ee, oe) | Identifying how problems escalate | Drafting the middle: the problem and what happens | Revision: ai, ay, a-e, ee, ea, o-e, oa |
| 16 | *Second Series — Book 4* | Revision: long vowels (er, ow, ie, oo) | How stories resolve — satisfying endings | Drafting the ending: solution and character feelings | Revision: er, ir, ur, ow, ou, igh, oo, ew |

**Learning Intentions — Week 4:**
- Plan a narrative with a clear beginning, middle, and end
- Draft a narrative that includes a character, setting, problem, and solution
- Use compound sentences, descriptive vocabulary, and punctuation in drafting
- Consolidate spelling of all phonics patterns studied

**Success Criteria — Week 4:**
- Completes a story map with character, setting, problem, and solution
- Drafts a narrative of at least 6–8 sentences across beginning, middle, and end
- Uses at least 2 compound sentences with correct punctuation
- Spells revision words with 80%+ accuracy in dictation

---

### Week 5 — Refining: Editing, Publishing, and Assessment

| Lesson | Book | Phonics Focus | Reading Focus | Writing Focus | Spelling Focus |
|--------|------|---------------|---------------|---------------|----------------|
| 17 | *Second Series — Book 5* | Application: mixed patterns | Reading like a writer — noticing how authors describe characters | Revising the draft: Adding character detail and vocabulary | Proofreading for spelling in own writing |
| 18 | *Second Series — Book 6* | Application: mixed patterns | Reading for punctuation — how authors use full stops, commas, capitals | Editing the draft: Punctuation, capitals, and sentence structure | Spelling corrections from own draft |
| 19 | *Second Series — Book 7* | Application: mixed patterns | Sharing reading — reading aloud with fluency and expression | Publishing: Writing the final copy with legible handwriting | Final spelling check on published piece |
| 20 | *Second Series — Book 8* | Application: mixed patterns | Celebrating reading — choosing a favourite Alba book to revisit | Assessment task: Complete the narrative using a known character | Demonstrating spelling knowledge in assessment |

**Learning Intentions — Week 5:**
- Revise writing for character detail, vocabulary, and descriptive language
- Edit writing for punctuation, capital letters, and sentence structure
- Publish a legible, polished narrative
- Complete the assessment task independently

**Success Criteria — Week 5:**
- Adds at least 2 improvements to the draft during revision
- Corrects punctuation and spelling errors during editing
- Produces a legible final copy using consistent unjoined letters
- Completes the assessment task: a narrative with sequenced events, punctuated sentences, topic-specific vocabulary, and regular spelling patterns

## Assessment Continuum

| Level | Indicators | Evidence |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| **Beginning** | Writes a text to narrate using simple sentences; experiments with boundary punctuation; uses familiar vocabulary | Early drafts (Lessons 1–4) |
| **Exploring** | Creates a written text to narrate for an audience; organises simple and compound sentences; experiments with topic-specific vocabulary | Mid-unit writing samples (Lessons 5–12) |
| **Working With** | Creates a multimodal text including an image to inform; uses text structures to organise and link ideas; punctuates simple and compound sentences; uses topic-specific vocabulary | Draft narrative (Lessons 13–16) |
| **Connecting** | Creates a written text with sequenced events to narrate for an audience; uses narrative text structures and basic noun/verb groups; punctuates simple and compound sentences; uses topic-specific vocabulary to build descriptions | Revised narrative (Lessons 17–18) |
| **Applying** | Creates a cohesive written text with sequenced events; uses extended noun and verb groups; punctuates correctly including capitals for titles and commas; uses precise vocabulary | Final published piece (Lessons 19–20) |
