# Week 1, Lesson 1: The World of _Paper Planes_

**Learning Intention:** Understand the Australian social and cultural context of _Paper Planes_.

**Success Criteria:**

- I can describe the Australian landscape as depicted in the novel.
- I can make predictions about themes using book covers and initial text.
- I can identify key locations (real and fictional) on a map of Australia.

---

## 1. Introduction: The Aussie Landscape (15 mins)

- **Visual Warm-up:** Display images of regional Australia (dusty roads, red earth, vast horizons). Ask students: _How does this feel different from where we live?_
- **Aussie Slang:** Introduce terms like 'mate' and 'fair go'. Discuss how these reflect Australian values.
- **Novel Entry:** Show the cover of _Paper Planes_. What does the artwork suggest about the setting?

## 2. Explore: Themes & Dreams (20 mins)

- **Prediction Brainstorm:** Based on the cover and title, what themes might we encounter?
  - _Family_ (Dylan and his dad)
  - _Rivalry_ (Competing with others)
  - _Dreams_ (The flight of the plane)
- **Dylan's World:** Read the back cover blurb. Discuss Dylan's life in "Waleup". Is it a real place? Why might an author invent a town?

## 3. Connect: Mapping Waleup (20 mins)

- **Reading:** Read Chapter 1 (or listen to the audiobook).
- **Map Activity:**
  - On the provided Australian map, identify the general region Dylan lives in (Western Australia/Regional).
  - Mark locations mentioned (Waleup, Perth).
- **Reflection:** How does the setting of a remote town affect the story's "vibe"?

## 4. Conclusion (5 mins)

- **Exit Ticket:** Write one prediction for Dylan's adventure.

---

## Differentiation Strategies

- **Reluctant Readers:** Use the audiobook for Chapter 1. Provide a "slang scavenger hunt" card during the reading.
- **Extension:** Research Lawrence Hargrave (mentioned later in the book) and his connection to Australian aviation.
