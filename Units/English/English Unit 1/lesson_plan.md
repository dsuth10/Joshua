# Lesson Plan: Figurative Language - Similes, Metaphors, and Personification

**Year Level:** Year 5  
**Subject:** English  
**Unit:** English Unit 1  
**Duration:** 60 Minutes

---

## Learning Intentions (WALT)
- **W**e **A**re **L**earning **T**o identify and use similes, metaphors, and personification to enhance our writing.

## Success Criteria (WILF)
- **W**hat **I** **L**ook **F**or:
  - I can define similes, metaphors, and personification.
  - I can identify examples of these devices in text.
  - I can create my own original similes, metaphors, and personification.

---

## Australian Curriculum Alignment
- **AC9E5LE02:** Identify and explain how figurative language choices, including similes, metaphors, and personification, contribute to meaning in texts.

---

## Teaching Sequence

### 1. Introduction (10 Minutes)
- **Hook:** Display three sentences on the board:
  1. "The sun was hot."
  2. "The sun was like a giant orange ball of fire."
  3. "The sun glared down at the parched earth."
- **Discussion:** Which sentence is most interesting? Why?
- **Definitions:** 
  - **Simile:** Comparing two things using "like" or "as".
  - **Metaphor:** Saying one thing *is* another (not literal).
  - **Personification:** Giving human qualities to non-human things.

### 2. I Do (10 Minutes)
- **Teacher Model:** Show examples of each and explain why they are effective.
  - *Simile:* "Her smile was as bright as the sun."
  - *Metaphor:* "The classroom was a zoo."
  - *Personification:* "The wind whispered through the trees."
- **Think Aloud:** Demonstrate how to turn a boring sentence into a figurative one.

### 3. We Do (15 Minutes)
- **Guided Practice:** Students help the teacher complete sentences on the board.
  - "The thunder was..." (Simile)
  - "The library is..." (Metaphor)
  - "The alarm clock..." (Personification)
- **Mini-Quiz:** Show images and have students shout out a figurative device for each.

### 4. You Do (20 Minutes)
- **Independent Practice:** Students complete the **Figurative Language Worksheet**.
  - Task 1: Identify similes, metaphors, and personification in provided snippets.
  - Task 2: Create their own examples based on image prompts.
  - Task 3: Write a short paragraph using at least one of each device.

### 5. Conclusion/Review (5 Minutes)
- **Share:** Students share their favourite example from their worksheet.
- **Exit Ticket:** Write one example of personification on a sticky note to place on the door.
