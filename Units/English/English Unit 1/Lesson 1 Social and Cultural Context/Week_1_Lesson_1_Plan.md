# Lesson Plan: Social and Cultural Context in *Paper Planes*

**Week 1, Lesson 1**  
**Subject:** Year 5 English  
**Text:** *Paper Planes* by Steve Worland  
**Theme:** Deep Navy & Vibrant Orange  

---

## 🎯 Learning Intention
Students will understand how social and cultural contexts (location, resources, and 'capital') influence characters and their journeys in a narrative.

## ✅ Success Criteria
- I can describe the differences between the regional setting of Waleup and the global setting of Tokyo.
- I can identify the different types of 'capital' (resources) available to Dylan and Jason.
- I can explain how these contexts affect the characters' motivations and opportunities.

---

## 1. Introduction (10 mins)
- **Brainstorm:** What does 'Context' mean? 
- **Definition:** Context is the environment (where, when, who) that surrounds a person or story.
- **Quick Look:** Show the map of Waleup vs. the Tokyo skyline. How do they feel different?

## 2. Core Activities (40 mins)

### Part 1: Mapping the Journey (Location Contrast)
- **Discussion:** Compare Waleup (dusty, isolated, small) to Tokyo (neon-lit, crowded, international).
- **Activity:** Students complete the "Two Worlds" T-Chart on their handout, listing 3 sensory details for each location.

### Part 2: The Wind Tunnel vs. The Heart (Capital Analysis)
- **Deep Dive:** Discuss Jason's context. He has a wind tunnel, money, and professional coaching. This is **Economic Capital**.
- **Deep Dive:** Discuss Dylan's context. He has Grandpa's wisdom, Waleup's space, and his father's struggle. This is **Cultural and Social Capital**.
- **Question:** Does Jason's money make him a better person? Does Dylan's lack of money stop him from being a champion?

### Part 3: Class Discussion - Is it a Level Playing Field?
- **Debate:** In the World Championships, everyone throws paper. Is the competition fair if some kids have more resources than others?
- **Connection:** How does the author, Steve Worland, show that 'heart' and 'cultural capital' are just as important as 'economic capital'?

---

## 3. Conclusion (10 mins)
- **Consolidation:** Exit Ticket. Complete the 5-question Microsoft Forms quiz (Lesson 1 Assessment).
- **Reflection:** One thing you learned about how a character's background shapes their story.

---

## 🚀 Differentiation

### Support (Reluctant Readers/Lucas)
- Use picture prompts for Waleup and Tokyo.
- Provide a word bank for the T-Chart (e.g., *dusty, quiet, bright, noisy*).
- Focus on one character (Dylan) and their "happy place" vs. the "big city".

### Extension
- Research "Cultural Capital" and write a short paragraph on what Dylan inherits from his Grandfather that isn't money.
- Predict how Jason might act differently if he lived in Waleup.
