# Lesson Plan: Mastering Speech Marks

**Unit:** Year 5 English / Punctuation Focus  
**Target Group:** Year 5 Students  
**Learning Intention:** To correctly identify and use speech marks (inverted commas) and the associated punctuation (commas, full stops, question marks, and capital letters) in direct speech.

---

## 1. Introduction: What is Direct Speech? (10 Mins)

- **Concept:** Explain that speech marks are like "containers" for words that are actually spoken aloud.
- **The Golden Rule:** Only the spoken words go inside the "66" and "99".
- **Visual:** Use the "Paper Planes" context—imagine Dylan talking to Jack.

---

## 2. Explicit Teaching: The Rules of the Road (15 Mins)

Focus on the 4 key rules:

1.  **The Capital Start:** Every piece of speech MUST start with a capital letter.  
    _Example:_ Dylan said, "**H**ow far will it fly?"
2.  **The Punctuation Hug:** The punctuation (comma, full stop, etc.) lives _inside_ the closing speech marks.  
    _Example:_ "That was amazing**!**" cheered Jason.
3.  **The Reporting Clause Comma:** If the speech comes after the speaker, use a comma before the first speech mark.  
    _Example:_ Jack whispered**,** "I'm sorry, Dylan."
4.  **New Speaker, New Line:** When a new person talks, we start a fresh line.

---

## 3. Sentence Variety Examples (10 Mins)

- **Statement:** "I think it will work," Dylan whispered.
- **Question:** "Can I help you with that?" asked Grandfather.
- **Exclamation:** "Look at it go!" shouted Kevin.
- **Interrupted Speech:** "My plane," Dylan said, "is made of old school paper."

---

## 4. Guided Practice (15 Mins)

- **Whiteboard Activity:** Fix the "Broken Sentences" (sentences missing speech marks or with misplaced punctuation).
- **Context:** Use scenes from the _Paper Planes_ novel to keep it relevant.

---

## 5. Independent Application (10 Mins)

- Students complete the **Speech Marks Worksheet**.
- **Differentiation (Lucas):** Match the speaker to the speech bubble. Add speech marks to one simple sentence.
- **Extension:** Write a short dialogue between Dylan and Jason using at least one interrupted speech sentence.

---
