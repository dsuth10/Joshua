# Year 5 English: Unit 1 Teaching Sequence
## *Paper Planes* by Steve Worland

| Week | Sequence | Lesson | Learning Intention | Teaching and Learning Sequence | Reading | Differentiation | Resources |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **1** | **1** | Understand the Australian social and cultural context of *Paper Planes*. | **Introduction:** Discuss the Aussie landscape. Show covers of *Paper Planes*. <br>**Explore:** Predict themes (family, rivalry, dreams). <br>**Connect:** Map locations mentioned in the start of the book. | *Paper Planes* - Chapter 1 | **Reluctant Readers:** Use the audiobook for Chapter 1. Point out Australian slang used (e.g., 'mate', 'fair go'). <br>**Lucas:** Focus on identifying a single character and one setting word. | Novel, Audiobook, Australian Map. |
| **1** | **1** | **2** | Identify the main characters and their initial relationships. | **Recall:** Who is Dylan? Where does he live? <br>**Visualise:** Draw Dylan's house based on text descriptions. <br>**Analyse:** Discuss Dylan's relationship with his father. | *Paper Planes* - Chapters 2-3 | **Support:** Provide a character profile template with prompts. <br>**Lucas:** Describe Dylan using one adjective. Write one simple sentence: "Dylan lives with Dad." | Drawing materials, Character Profile worksheet. |
| **1** | **1** | **3** | Explore the "call to adventure" and the theme of dreams. | **Define:** What is a dream? What does Dylan want? <br>**Identify:** Find the moment Dylan discovers paper planes. <br>**Predict:** How will this discovery change his life? | *Paper Planes* - Chapters 4-5 | **Reluctant Readers:** Listen to the discovery scene. Highlight key emotive words in the text. <br>**Lucas:** Use a picture of a paper plane. Label parts. | Audiobook, Paper for folding. |
| **1** | **1** | **4** | Recognise the structure of the opening sequence of a narrative. | **Identify:** Beginning, Middle, End of the discovery. <br>**Sequence:** List the main events of the first week. <br>**Reflect:** Why did the author start here? | *Paper Planes* - Revision of Ch 1-5 | **Extension:** Compare the book's start to the film's start (if accessible). <br>**Lucas:** Retell the story using three pictures. | Sequence cards, Film clip (optional). |
| **2** | **1 continued** | **5** | Describe how the setting of Waleup reflects Dylan's life. | **Discuss:** Is Waleup a real place? How does it look? <br>**Analyse:** How does the isolated setting affect Dylan? <br>**Write:** A short paragraph describing the heat/dust. | *Paper Planes* - Chapters 6-7 | **Support:** Word bank of 'dusty', 'dry', 'vast', 'remote'. <br>**Lucas:** Identify "hot" and "dry" as keywords. | Setting word bank. |
| **2** | **1 continued** | **6** | Understand point of view in the narrative. | **Define:** First person vs. Third person. <br>**Practice:** Retell a scene from the Dad's point of view. <br>**Discuss:** How does Dylan's perspective influence our feelings? | *Paper Planes* - Chapter 8 | **Reluctant Readers:** Role-play a conversation between Dylan and Dad. <br>**Lucas:** Use "I" statements in role-play. | Role-play prompts. |
| **2** | **1 continued** | **7** | Examine the use of informal language to establish character. | **Find:** Examples of Australian idioms. <br>**Analyse:** Why does the author use 'mate' so often? <br>**Compare:** Dylan's speech vs. official speech at school. | *Paper Planes* - Chapters 9-10 | **Support:** Provide a glossary of Australian slang used. <br>**Lucas:** Match slang words to pictures (e.g., 'esky'). | Slang glossary. |
| **2** | **1 continued** | **8** | Sequence events leading to the first regional competition. | **Recall:** How does Dylan get to the competition? <br>**Summarise:** The rules of the competition. <br>**Predict:** Who will be Dylan's main rival? | *Paper Planes* - Chapter 11 | **Reluctant Readers:** Listen to the competition scene. Map the 'flight path' of planes. <br>**Lucas:** Draw Dylan throwing a plane. Write: "Dylan throws far." | Audiobook, Paper planes. |
| **3** | **2** | **9** | Analyse the relationship between Dylan and Grandfather. | **Compare:** How does Grandpa treat Dylan compared to Dad? <br>**Identify:** Wisdom shared by Grandpa. <br>**Reflect:** The importance of family history in the story. | *Paper Planes* - Chapters 12-13 | **Support:** T-Chart comparing Dad and Grandpa. <br>**Lucas:** Identify Grandpa as "happy". Write: "Grandpa loves Dylan." | T-Chart worksheet. |
| **3** | **2** | **10** | Explore figurative language used to describe the planes' flight. | **Define:** Simile and Metaphor. <br>**Find:** "Like a bird", "Slicing the air". <br>**Write:** Create original similes for their own planes. | *Paper Planes* - Chapter 14 | **Reluctant Readers:** Listen for descriptive sounds. Use sentence starters: "The plane flew like a..." <br>**Lucas:** Use sensory words: "fast", "high". | Simile starters. |
| **3** | **2** | **11** | Identify the conflict between Dylan and Jason. | **Define:** Protagonist vs. Antagonist. <br>**Discuss:** Why is Jason mean? <br>**Compare:** Jason's resources vs. Dylan's resources. | *Paper Planes* - Chapters 15-16 | **Support:** Venn diagram of Dylan and Jason. <br>**Lucas:** Identify Jason as "angry". Write: "Jason is mean." | Venn diagram. |
| **3** | **2** | **12** | Track the emotional journey of the main character. | **Discuss:** Feeling scared vs. feeling brave. <br>**Map:** Dylan's mood over the first half of the book. <br>**Predict:** Will Dylan give up? | *Paper Planes* - Chapter 17 | **Reluctant Readers:** Use an 'Emotional Thermometer' to track Dylan's feelings. <br>**Lucas:** Use emoji cards to show Dylan's feelings. | Emotional thermometer. |
| **4** | **2 continued** | **13** | Use noun groups to expand descriptions of characters. | **Identify:** Simple nouns (Dylan, plane). <br>**Expand:** "The dusty, determined boy", "The sleek, white paper glider". <br>**Apply:** Rewrite a sentence with expanded noun groups. | *Paper Planes* - Chapter 18 | **Support:** Scaffolding for noun groups (Adjective + Adjective + Noun). <br>**Lucas:** Add one adjective to a name: "Old Grandpa". | Noun group scaffold. |
| **4** | **2 continued** | **14** | Examine the impact of secondary characters on the plot. | **Focus:** Kevin. What is his role? <br>**Analyse:** How does Kevin help Dylan? <br>**Reflect:** The importance of friendship in the novel. | *Paper Planes* - Chapters 19-20 | **Reluctant Readers:** Listen to the interactions between Dylan and Kevin. <br>**Lucas:** Draw Dylan and Kevin as friends. | Friendship mapping worksheet. |
| **4** | **2 continued** | **15** | Understand the concept of 'foreshadowing' in the narrative. | **Define:** Hints about the future. <br>**Identify:** Any clues about Japan/World Championships. <br>**Discuss:** Why does the author want us to know this? | *Paper Planes* - Chapter 21 | **Extension:** Find clues in earlier chapters that led to this point. <br>**Lucas:** Predict one thing that will happen next using a picture prompt. | Prediction chart. |
| **4** | **2 continued** | **16** | Summarise the midpoint of the novel. | **Recall:** Major events so far. <br>**Check:** Do we have a clear beginning and middle? <br>**Reflect:** How has Dylan changed since Chapter 1? | *Paper Planes* - Revision of Ch 12-21 | **Reluctant Readers:** Create a storyboard of the 'Middle' sequence. <br>**Lucas:** Arrange 5 pictures in order of the story. | Storyboard template. |
| **5** | **3** | **17** | Explore the setting of the State Championships. | **Contrast:** Waleup vs. the big city. <br>**Describe:** The atmosphere of the stadium. <br>**Discuss:** Sensory details (noise, lights, crowds). | *Paper Planes* - Chapters 22-23 | **Support:** Comparison table for Regional vs. State settings. <br>**Lucas:** Identify "loud" and "busy". Write: "Many people are here." | Sensory word bank. |
| **5** | **3** | **18** | Analyse character motivation during high-pressure events. | **Discuss:** Why does Jason Cheat? Why does Dylan stay honest? <br>**Reflect:** Does the setting make the choices harder? <br>**Identify:** Dylan's moral compass. | *Paper Planes* - Chapter 24 | **Reluctant Readers:** Listen to the tension during the throw. <br>**Lucas:** Use 'Stop/Go' signs to indicate good/bad choices. | Decision cards. |
| **5** | **3** | **19** | Recognise the use of complex sentences for effect. | **Identify:** Main vs. Dependent clauses. <br>**Analyse:** "As the plane left his hand, Dylan held his breath." <br>**Practice:** Create a complex sentence about a flight. | *Paper Planes* - Chapter 25 | **Support:** Sentence strips to join clauses together. <br>**Lucas:** Connect two simple ideas with "and": "The plane flies and it is white." | Clause connectors. |
| **5** | **3** | **20** | Evaluate the significance of 'winning' in the story. | **Discuss:** Is winning the most important thing to Dylan? <br>**Analyse:** Dad's reaction to the State win. <br>**Compare:** Dylan's joy vs. Jason's anger. | *Paper Planes* - Chapter 26 | **Reluctant Readers:** Draw the 'Winning Moment'. Use emotive words for the caption. <br>**Lucas:** Draw a trophy. Write: "Dylan win." | Emotive word list. |
| **6** | **3 continued** | **21** | Investigate the history of paper planes (Aviation connection). | **Research:** Revisit the references to Hargrave. <br>**Connect:** How does Dylan's passion link to real science? <br>**Discuss:** Why the author included this 'factual' element. | *Paper Planes* - Chapter 27 | **Support:** Short fact-sheet on Lawrence Hargrave. <br>**Lucas:** Look at pictures of old planes and paper planes. Match them. | Hargrave Fact Sheet. |
| **6** | **3 continued** | **22** | Analyse the shift in perspective: Seeing the world from Japan. | **Describe:** The Tokyo setting. <br>**Contrast:** Australian vs. Japanese culture mentioned. <br>**Reflect:** How Dylan feels being an 'outsider'. | *Paper Planes* - Chapters 28-29 | **Reluctant Readers:** Use the audiobook to hear the descriptions of Tokyo. <br>**Lucas:** Identify the Japanese flag. Match 'Hello' in English and Japanese. | Map of Japan, Flag images. |
| **6** | **3 continued** | **23** | Examine the relationship between Dylan and Kimi. | **Compare:** How is Kimi different from Dylan? <br>**Identify:** Shared passion for paper planes. <br>**Discuss:** The theme of global friendship. | *Paper Planes* - Chapters 30-31 | **Support:** Comparison chart (Kimi vs. Dylan). <br>**Lucas:** Identify Kimi as a friend. Write: "Kimi is nice." | Comparison worksheet. |
| **6** | **3 continued** | **24** | Track the resolution of the conflict with Dad. | **Analyse:** The turning point for Dad. <br>**Discuss:** Why did he finally watch the video? <br>**Reflect:** The role of the eagle's flight in the father's healing. | *Paper Planes* - Chapter 32 | **Reluctant Readers:** Listen to the scene where Dad wakes up. Discuss feelings. <br>**Lucas:** Identify "Happy Dad". Write: "Dad is okay." | Feeling cards. |
| **7** | **4** | **25** | Identify the 'Climax' of the narrative. | **Define:** The highest point of action. <br>**Discuss:** The Final Throw in Tokyo. <br>**Identify:** Key technical details of the flight described. | *Paper Planes* - Chapters 33-34 | **Support:** Plot mountain diagram. Mark the climax. <br>**Lucas:** Identify the most exciting part using a 'Volume' scale. | Plot mountain worksheet. |
| **7** | **4** | **26** | Review the narrative text structure for assessment preparation. | **Sequence:** Map the whole book into 5 main plot points. <br>**Analyse:** Transition words between chapters. <br>**Prepare:** How would you extend this story? | *Paper Planes* - Revision of the whole novel | **Reluctant Readers:** Use the audiobook to listen to the final chapter. Discuss 'What happens next?' <br>**Lucas:** Use a story-wheel to show the cycle of the novel. | Story-wheel. |
| **7** | **4** | **27** | Understand the Assessment Task 1.1 requirements. | **Read:** The assessment sheet. <br>**Unpack:** What is an 'adaptation'? (Prequel, Sequel, Alternative ending). <br>**Brainstorm:** Ideas based on *Paper Planes*. | Assessment Task Sheet | **Support:** Provide 3 specific 'Seeds' for adaptations (e.g., Kimi visits Waleup). <br>**Lucas:** Choose one 'Seed' based on a picture. | Ideas bank. |
| **7** | **4** | **28** | Use the planning table to organise narrative ideas. | **Draft:** Who is your main character? What is the setting? <br>**Align:** Is it consistent with Steve Worland's world? <br>**Discuss:** Your purpose and audience. | *Paper Planes* context | **Reluctant Readers:** Use a verbal planning template (Voice recorder). <br>**Lucas:** Use the Lucas ICP story map (Beginning, Middle, End). | Planning templates. |
| **8** | **4 continued** | **29** | Practice writing 'Hook' openings for the adaptation. | **Analyse:** Successful hooks from the novel. <br>**Write:** Three different ways to start your story. <br>**Peer Review:** Which hook makes the reader want to continue? | Draft Hooks | **Support:** "Once upon a time" vs. "The plane whistled through the air..." <br>**Lucas:** Write one strong opening sentence: "The plane flew fast." | Opening sentence starters. |
| **8** | **4 continued** | **30** | Develop characters using dialogue and action. | **Refresh:** How to punctuate speech. <br>**Apply:** Write a scene where two characters talk about a flight. <br>**Check:** Does the dialogue sound like them (mate, slang)? | Draft Dialogue | **Reluctant Readers:** Use speech bubbles to plan dialogue first. <br>**Lucas:** Put words in Dylan's mouth using a picture. | Punctuation guide. |
| **8** | **4 continued** | **31** | Create cohesion using paragraphing and linking words. | **Practice:** Transition words (Suddenly, Later that day, Meanwhile). <br>**Organise:** Group ideas into logical sequences. <br>**Reflect:** How do paragraphs help the reader? | Draft Paragraphs | **Support:** Paragraph planning cards (Topic sentence + Detail). <br>**Lucas:** Use "First", "Next", "Then" cards to link three sentences. | Cohesion word list. |
| **8** | **4 continued** | **32** | Integrate figurative language into the narrative adaptation. | **Apply:** Add one simile and one metaphor to the draft. <br>**Review:** Does the language make the scene more vivid? <br>**Share:** Read a 'sparkle' sentence to the class. | Draft with Fig. Lang. | **Support:** Word bank of emotive adjectives and verbs. <br>**Lucas:** Use one "like" sentence (e.g., "Fast like a bird"). | Figurative language bank. |
| **9** | **5** | **33** | Draft the 'Middle' (Problem/Conflict) of the adaptation. | **Write:** The core challenge of your new story. <br>**Analyse:** Is the tension building? <br>**Self-Check:** Are you keeping the 'Aussie' voice? | Assessment Draft | **Reluctant Readers:** Dictate the middle section. Transcribe for review. <br>**Lucas:** Write the problem: "The plane broke." | Drafting paper. |
| **9** | **5** | **34** | Draft the 'Ending' (Resolution) of the adaptation. | **Write:** How does the story finish? <br>**Check:** Is it a satisfying ending for the audience? <br>**Analyse:** What did the character learn? | Assessment Draft | **Support:** Reflection questions: "Happy or Sad? Why?" <br>**Lucas:** Write the ending: "Dylan is happy." | Drafting paper. |
| **9** | **5** | **35** | Review and Edit: Grammar and Punctuation (Self-Audit). | **Check:** Tense consistency, complex sentences, noun groups. <br>**Fix:** Spelling errors using a dictionary. <br>**Verify:** Paragraph breaks. | Draft Assessment | **Support:** Editing checklist (CUPS - Capital, Usage, Punctuation, Spelling). <br>**Lucas:** Focus on full stops and capital letters. | Dictionaries, CUPS Checklist. |
| **9** | **5** | **36** | Review and Edit: Peer Feedback and Conferencing. | **Interaction:** Share draft with a partner. <br>**Listen:** Listen to feedback. <br>**Refine:** Change one thing to make the story clearer. | Draft Assessment | **Reluctant Readers:** Listen to their story being read back to them. Mark spots to fix. <br>**Lucas:** Show story to a friend. Find the "Best Part". | Peer Feedback sheet. |
| **10** | **5 continued** | **37** | Final Polish: Publishing the written narrative. | **Publish:** Finalise the written text. <br>**Format:** Ensure neatness and correct paragraphing. <br>**Check:** Did you meet the 200-400 word requirement? | Final Narrative | **Extension:** Add an illustration of your own paper plane design. <br>**Lucas:** Re-write neatly in final book. Add a drawing. | Stationery, Word processor. |
| **10** | **5 continued** | **38** | Multimodal: Creating a digital or audio element. | **Task:** Record a reading of the 'Highlight' of your story. <br>**Enhance:** Add sound effects (wind, crowds). <br>**Share:** Prepare for the class 'Premiere'. | Published Text | **Support:** Use simplified recording software. <br>**Lucas:** Record a 3-sentence summary of his story. | Voice recorders, Tablets. |
| **10** | **5 continued** | **39** | Assessment: Shared response and reflection. | **Present:** Share multimodal elements with the group. <br>**Listen:** Provide positive feedback to peers. <br>**Reflect:** Complete a 'Self-Reflection' on the writing process. | Student Works | **Reluctant Readers:** Focus on the 'Audience' reaction. <br>**Lucas:** Show his picture and read his one "Best Sentence". | Reflection prompt cards. |
| **10** | **5 continued** | **40** | Unit Review and Celebration. | **Celebrate:** The 'Paper Planes' Flight Day! <br>**Reflect:** How did Dylan's story help our own writing? <br>**Wrap-up:** Final novel discussion and wrap-up activities. | Novel, Own Planes | **All:** Inclusive flight competition based on own story plane. <br>**Lucas:** Launch his plane with the class. | Paper, Ribbons for awards. |
