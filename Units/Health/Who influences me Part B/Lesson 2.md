# Lesson 2: Health Messages & Problem-Solving

## Learning Intentions
We are learning to:
* Identify and understand the Australian guidelines for achieving healthier and safer living for young people.
* Apply a structured six-step problem-solving model to create a personal health message for Year 5 or 6 students.
* Gather, evaluate, and cite credible health information from trustworthy online sources.
* Assess the effectiveness, truth, and importance of our self-designed health messages on personal wellbeing.

## Success Criteria
I can:
* Identify the 7 Australian guidelines for healthier and safer living.
* Complete all steps of the six-step problem-solving model:
  1. **Identify the problem**: Select a guideline and formulate the promoting question for Year 5/6 peers.
  2. **Explore the problem**: Research and gather 6 verified facts from 3 credible online sources.
  3. **Create ideas**: Brainstorm educational points and key methods for getting the message across.
  4. **Formulate the message**: Craft a concise, memorable, and action-oriented one-sentence health message.
  5. **Act**: Propose and draft a promotional design (poster, script, roleplay, or badge).
  6. **Review the outcome**: Evaluate the credibility, communication strategy, and overall impact on health, safety, and wellbeing.
* Utilize Australian English spelling (e.g., *behaviours*, *organise*, *wellbeing*) and metric measurements in all documents.

## Curriculum Mapping
* **Curriculum Focus**: Health and Physical Education (HPE)
* **Year Level**: Years 5 & 6 Band
* **Content Descriptor**: Recognise how media and important people in the community influence personal attitudes, beliefs, decisions and behaviours (ACPPS057 / AC9HP6G01: *analyse how behaviours and community and media messages influence health, safety and wellbeing*).
* **General Capabilities**: Literacy (comprehending, composing, visual literacy), Critical and Creative Thinking, ICT Capability.
