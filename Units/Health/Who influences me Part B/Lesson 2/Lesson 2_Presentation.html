The Australian guidelines on achieving healthier and safer living help young people protect their health, safety, and physical wellbeing. Below are the 7 core guidelines. 01

Fruit & Veg Intake

Eat at least two serves of fruit and three serves of vegetables every day to keep your body energised and healthy.

02

Daily Physical Activity

Participate in at least 60 minutes of moderate to vigorous activity every single day to boost strength, sleep, and mood.

03

Screen Time Limits

Have no more than two hours of screen time per day to protect your sleep, eyesight, and maintain active hobbies.

04

Adequate Sleep

Get high-quality sleep every single night (9-11 hours for ages 5-13) to allow your brain and body to grow and recharge.

05

Sun Safety

Be sun safe: Slip on a shirt, Slop on SPF 30+, Slap on a hat, Seek shade, and Slide on sunglasses to prevent skin damage.

06

Road Safety Rules

Always follow road safety rules. Wear helmets on bikes/scooters, use pedestrian crossings, and buckle seatbelts.

07

Water Safety Choices

Make safe choices around water environments. Swim between red & yellow flags, check depths, and respect warning signs.

01) IDENTIFY THE PROBLEM

The first step in any problem-solving model is to pin down exactly what you are trying to solve. In health promotion, this means asking: "How can we persuade or support our Year 5 or 6 peers to adopt healthier choices?"

SYSTEM_LOGIC:

Simply stating a rule (e.g., "Eat fruit") is rarely effective. We must identify what prevents peers from following the guideline (barriers) and frame it as an opportunity for communication.

02) EXPLORE THE PROBLEM (RESEARCH)

To convince others, your message must be backed by accurate, trustworthy science. You must gather credible information. Government health sites (.gov.au), medical councils, and safety organisations (e.g., SunSmart) are excellent, highly reliable databases.

TASK: Research your selected guideline. Find 3 highly credible website addresses and record 6 distinct, scientific facts that prove why this guideline matters to health and safety.

CREDIBILITY_MATRIX

TRIPLE FILTER:

1. **PURPOSE:** Why was the site created? (To educate or to sell?)

2. **INFORMATION:** Is the data accurate, cited, and up-to-date?

3. **MESSENGER:** Is the publisher a qualified medical/safety expert?

03) CREATE IDEAS & ARRANGE CONTENT

Having gathered your 6 facts, you must now select the **2 most important facts** that will grab the attention of Year 5/6 students. Students have busy lives—they need to know what affects them directly!

Think about how your message should be communicated. Will you use humor, catchy rhymes, visual comparisons, or shocking facts? Design a strategy to cut through the noise and grab their attention.

CAMPAIGN_TRIGGERS

TRIGGER HOW IT WORKS
HUMOUR Relaxes audience, makes memory stick
URGENCY Shows the immediate impact of choices
SLOGANS Short, rhythmic phrases (e.g., Slip, Slop, Slap)

04) THE HEALTH MESSAGE SENTENCE

Your core health message must be distilled into **a single, active, memorable sentence** that directly addresses your guideline.

GOLDEN_RULES_FOR_SENTENCES:

• Active Voice: Start with verbs (e.g., "Shield your skin" instead of "Sunscreen should be worn").

• Short & Sharp: Avoid long, winding paragraphs. Aim for under 15 words.

• Actionable: Clearly outline exactly what the student should do next.

05) ACT — DESIGN YOUR PROMOTION

An amazing message needs an equally amazing delivery channel! How will you launch your campaign to peers? Select your promotional medium: a highly visual poster, a snappy podcast or video script, a stylish badge layout, or a digital social media card.

TASK: Draft a layout or write down a script outline showing how your message, chosen facts, and visual components will combine to create an outstanding campaign.

MEDIA_SELECTION

• POSTER: Best for high school wall placement; highly visual.

• AUDIO/VIDEO: Best for assembly announcements or school news broadcasts.

• BADGE/BANNER: Best for everyday wearable reminders.

06) REVIEW FOR EFFECTIVENESS

The final phase is critical: **evaluation**. Before distributing your campaign to the entire school, you must test its validity and impact.

Reflect deeply on your work: How do you know the data is 100% true? How will the message be delivered? And most importantly, explain why this message is vital to your own and your friends' health, safety, and personal wellbeing.
