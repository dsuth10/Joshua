# Let's All Be Active — 7-Week Lesson Sequence

**Unit:** Let's all be active (Year 6 Health, C2C Unit 2)  
**Year level:** 6  
**Duration:** 7 weeks (1 × ~60 min Health lesson per week)  
**Summative assessment:** Let's all be active (Assignment/Project)

---

## Unit at a Glance

| Element | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Unit focus** | How physical activity connects people, supports wellbeing, and celebrates diversity |
| **Key AC codes** | ACPPS054, ACPPS059, ACPPS060, ACPMP064 |
| **Assessment data begins** | Week 1 (`My physical activity`) |
| **Assessment submitted** | Week 7 |

**Achievement standard focus:** Students describe the significance of physical activity to health and wellbeing, describe their own and others' contributions to safety and wellbeing, and examine how physical activity, celebrating diversity and connecting to the environment support community wellbeing and cultural understanding.

---

## Week-by-Week Sequence

### Week 1 — Launch: Why we move and who moves

**C2C topic:** Topic 1 — Valuing diversity influences community wellbeing  
**Lesson title:** *My activity, our community*

**Learning intentions**
- Define physical activity and reflect on personal choices
- Explain reasons for participating (or not) in physical activity
- Recognise that different groups participate in different activities, including First Nations and Asian cultural activities

**Key activities**
1. Unit launch — connect to Term 3 theme **Be active**
2. Complete **My physical activity** sheet (assessment evidence begins)
3. **Physical activity alphabet** — brainstorm the range of activities in community settings
4. Introduce cultural activities: Dabi, Edor, Keentan, Wana Wana, Tai chi
5. Exit ticket: one way different groups work together through physical activity

**Resources:** `PPS_Y06_U2_SH_MyPhysicalActivity`, `PPS_Y06_U2_SH_PAAlphabet`, SLR Indigenous games + Tai chi, Yulunga website

**Formative check:** Can students identify their motivation for participation? Can they give an example of different groups participating together?

---

### Week 2 — How active are we? Gathering the data

**C2C topic:** Topic 2 (Part 1) — Participation in physical activity  
**Lesson title:** *Active Australians — what the data tells us*

**Learning intentions**
- Interpret participation data for young Australians (5–17 years)
- Understand methods for collecting information about physical activity
- Conduct a structured peer interview on activity choices

**Key activities**
1. Discuss **Is sport enough?** participation rates
2. Estimate then compare with actual statistics — **Physical activity and Year 6: a picture**
3. Teach interview protocol; pairs complete **Interview** sheet
4. Begin collating class data

**Resources:** `PPS_Y06_U2_SH_PAY6Picture`, `PPS_Y06_U2_SH_Interview`, SLR Participation rates, SLR Interview collation

**Formative check:** Can students collect and record information about physical activity?

**Assessment link:** `Physical activity and Year 6: a picture` feeds into the final task.

---

### Week 3 — Active spaces and fuel for movement

**C2C topic:** Topic 2 (Parts 2 & 3) — Participation in physical activity  
**Lesson title:** *Mapping movement + energy for action*

**Learning intentions**
- Map physical activity locations in the school setting
- Explain how food choices support sustained energy for physical activity
- Consolidate school-community data for later assessment responses

**Key activities**
1. **Physical activity map** — annotate school grounds
2. Gallery walk of maps; discuss connections to natural and built environments
3. **Energy foods** activity — sort/classify foods that provide sustained energy
4. Finish collating interview data from Week 2 if incomplete

**Resources:** `PPS_Y06_U2_SH_PAMap`, `PPS_Y06_U2_SH_EnergyFoods`, SLR Map making, SLR Energy foods

**Formative check:** Can students annotate a map of PA locations? Can they identify energy-supporting food choices?

**Assessment link:** `Physical activity map` is a required reference sheet for the summative task.

---

### Week 4 — What movement does for our bodies and minds

**C2C topic:** Topic 3 (Part 1) — Physical activity supports community health and wellbeing  
**Lesson title:** *Body systems and dimensions of health*

**Learning intentions**
- Describe benefits of physical activity on body systems
- Match benefits to physical, social, emotional, and environmental dimensions of health

**Key activities**
1. **Physical activity and body systems**
2. Introduce **Dimensions of health** diagram
3. **Benefits of physical activity** — match benefits to health dimensions
4. Short fitness activity; debrief impact on body systems

**Resources:** `PPS_Y06_U2_SH_PABodySystems`, `PPS_Y06_U2_SH_BenefitsPA`, SLR PA body systems, SLR Dimensions of health

**Formative check:** Can students match PA benefits to dimensions of health?

**Assessment link:** `Benefits of physical activity` is required for the summative task.

---

### Week 5 — Activity, community, and country

**C2C topic:** Topic 3 (Part 2) — Physical activity supports community health and wellbeing  
**Lesson title:** *Healthy, active communities*

**Learning intentions**
- Explain how outdoor activity connects people to natural environments
- Apply a case study to the school context
- Examine how PA supports community wellbeing and cultural understanding

**Key activities**
1. Slideshow: **Healthy, active communities**
2. **Have you ever?** — personal connections to outdoor/community activity
3. Nature Play QLD discussion
4. Apply case study: how could our school community be more active together?

**Resources:** `PPS_Y06_U2_SS_HealthyActiveCommunities`, `PPS_Y06_U2_SH_HaveYouEver`, SLR Natural environment supports wellbeing

**Formative check:** Can students relate case study information to their school context?

---

### Week 6 — Design, safety, and technology

**C2C topic:** Topic 4 (Part 1) — Health, safety and wellbeing through strategies  
**Lesson title:** *Creating an inclusive activity*

**Learning intentions**
- Review collected unit work
- Apply assessment guidelines to draft a physical activity for six Year 6 students
- Consider how technology can support physical activity and wellbeing

**Key activities**
1. Introduce **Let's all be active** assessment task and GTMJ
2. Review assessment guidelines
3. Draft activity: name, location, equipment, instructions, safety rationale
4. **Technology and wellbeing**
5. Teacher/PE specialist safety check of draft activities

**Resources:** `PPS_Y06_U2_AT_LetBeActive`, `PPS_Y06_U2_TN_LetBeActive`, `PPS_Y06_U2_SH_TechWellbeing`, SLR Tech and wellbeing

**Formative check:** Can students create a PA matching the guidelines? Can they comment on technology supporting PA?

---

### Week 7 — Assessment completion and celebration

**C2C topics:** Topic 4 (Part 2) + Assessment  
**Lesson title:** *Let's all be active — submit and play*

**Learning intentions**
- Complete all written assessment components
- Explain how their activity accounts for diversity and supports community wellbeing
- Participate in peer-designed activities (optional celebration)

**Key activities**
1. Finalise assessment using completed reference sheets
2. Complete dimensions-of-health table and diversity explanation
3. Submit assessment
4. Peer activity festival (if time/space permits)
5. Unit reflection

**Resources:** All completed student sheets, model response, sun safety / risk management procedures

**Summative assessment:** Full **Let's all be active** task submitted and marked against GTMJ.
