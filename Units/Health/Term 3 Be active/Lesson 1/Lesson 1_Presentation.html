Australian Sports Commission | Yulunga

Four activities, four specific contexts

Mabuiag Island, Torres Strait

Dabi

Continuous control and passing with a soft ball and hockey-style sticks.

Aurukun, north Queensland

Edor

A large-group, goal-orientated chasing-and-tagging game.

North-west central Queensland

Keentan

A running, passing and catching keep-away game with jumping catches.

Noongar, south-west WA

Wana Wana

A circle practice activity using controlled lobbing, hitting, catching and rotation.

Source: Australian Sports Commission - Yulunga
