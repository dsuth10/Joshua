What Counts as Physical Activity?

Definition

Any movement that uses energy, raises your heart rate, and helps keep your body healthy.

Examples

Walking, sport, dance, swimming, gardening, riding, cultural games, martial arts, playing at lunch.

Where?

School, home, parks, beaches, clubs, community centres, natural environments.

Why?

Fun, fitness, friendship, competition, culture, relaxation, challenge, belonging.

Activities From Many Cultures

Physical activity is part of culture around the world. We learn about these activities with respect — understanding their history and significance.

Dabi

Traditional throwing and catching game from Aboriginal cultures. Develops hand-eye coordination and teamwork.

INDIGENOUS

Edor

Team chasing game from Ghana, played in many African communities. Builds agility and cooperation.

AFRICAN

Keentan

Traditional Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander game similar to keep-away. Encourages speed and strategy.

INDIGENOUS

Wana Wana

Traditional Aboriginal game involving running and tagging. Played for fitness and community connection.

INDIGENOUS

Tai Chi

Chinese martial art practised for health, balance, and calm movement. Supports physical and emotional wellbeing.

Diversity in Action

When people from different backgrounds, ages, and abilities participate together, physical activity:

  • Builds respect and understanding
  • Creates friendships across groups
  • Strengthens community wellbeing
  • Celebrates cultural identity

School Examples

Discuss as a class:

  • Inter-school sport carnivals
  • Mixed-age lunch games
  • Inclusive PE activities
  • Community fun runs or walks
  • Cultural celebration days with movement
