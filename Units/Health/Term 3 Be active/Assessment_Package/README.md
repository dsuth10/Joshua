## The 2-Session Structure

### Session 1: Design Studio & Health Dimension Analysis (60 min)
1. **Launch & Non-Negotiables:** Deconstruct the 3 rules (100% Inclusion, Zero Elimination, Safety by Design).
2. **Game Blueprint & Rules:** Name, safe location, minimal equipment, spatial court diagram, and 5 numbered rules.
3. **The 4 Dimensions of Health:** Detailed written analysis of **Physical**, **Social**, **Emotional**, and **Environmental** benefits.
4. **Diversity & Adaptations:** Explicit modifications for differing mobility (wheelchair users), skills, and respectful cultural game origins (*Dabi*, *Edor*, *Keentan*, *Wana Wana*).
5. **Pair Safety Audit:** 2-minute pitch and safety check.

### Session 2: Peer Activity Festival & Final Submission (60 min)
1. **Safety Briefing & Referee Protocol:** 3 Golden Rules for Game Leaders.
2. **The Festival (Live Playtesting):** 2 × 12-minute outdoor playtest rounds with groups of 6 peers.
3. **Peer Feedback:** "Two Stars and a Safety Wish" evaluation.
4. **Iterative Redesign:** Documenting an explicit design refinement made after testing.
5. **GTMJ Self-Assessment & Portfolio Hand-In:** Guide to Making Judgments A–E self-rating and unit celebration.

## How to Use the Interactive Presentation (`Assessment_Presentation.html`)

* Open `Assessment_Presentation.html` in any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox). Works **100% offline**.
* **Navigation:**
  * Use **Left / Right Arrow** keys or **Spacebar** to advance slides.
  * Click **Session 1** or **Session 2** buttons in the toolbar to jump between sessions.
* **Presenter Notes Drawer (`N` key):**
  * Press `N` or click the **Notes** button on the bottom toolbar to open the off-canvas presenter notes drawer containing explicit `[DO]`, `[WORK]`, `[RECORD]`, `[FINISH]`, and `[CHECK]` instructions.
* **Fullscreen (`F` key):**
  * Press `F` or click the **Fullscreen** button to project in true full-viewport view.
* **Interactive Elements:**
  * Click on constraint cards (Slide 2) to reveal non-negotiables.
  * Click on the 4 Dimensions tabs (Slide 6) to reveal physiological/social explanations and sentence stems.
  * Use the embedded **Design Sprint Timers** (Slides 5, 8, 12, 14) with custom presets (5m, 10m, 15m).
