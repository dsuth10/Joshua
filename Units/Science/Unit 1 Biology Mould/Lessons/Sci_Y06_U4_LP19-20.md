# # # Life on Earth                                                                                                                                                                                                                Year 6                                     Unit 4

# # # Lessons 19--20                                                                                                          Extreme beings --- Extreme living

## ## ## Lesson concepts                                                                                                                                                                   Example learning sequence

Review requirements of living things

  - Review the requirements for living things to survive.

  - Identify environments on Earth where these requirements might not be met.

Introduce the concept of extremophiles

  - View the slideshow *Extreme living* about organisms that live in extreme environments.

  - Define the term 'extremophile' as a living thing that lives in extreme environmental conditions that would be harmful to
most other life on Earth.

Research extremophiles

  - Conduct research into the living conditions of a number of different extremophiles using supporting resources:

  - Identify the factors that make the environment extreme.

  - Describe the adaptations that allow the extremophile to survive in that environment.

  - Compare the requirements of extremophiles to those of other living things.

## Lesson objectives

Students will:

Recognise that living things grow and survive in extreme environments.

Understand that the requirements of extremophiles are different to other living things.

## Evidence of learning

Can the student:

Describe the extreme elements of an environment that a living thing grows and survives in?

Compare the requirements of extremophiles to those of other living things?

## Ideas for monitoring

Monitor students' ability to:

Describe the extreme elements of an environment that a living thing grows and survives in.

Compare requirements of extremophiles and other living things.

## Learning alerts

Be aware of:

Students thinking that living things can only exist under the same environmental conditions as they do.

## Suggested next steps for learning

Ensure that students understand that living things can exist in a very wide range of environments. Some organisms can live where most other life would die.

## Ideas for differentiation

Support

Allow students to work in small groups to research extremophiles and to present their findings to the class.

Extension

Ask students to research other extremophiles and the environmental conditions they live in.
