# # # Lessons 7--9                                                                                                                                                                                                Environmental effects on plants --- Investigating plant growth

## ## ## Lesson concepts                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       **Note:** This lesson uses plants that were germinated in a previous lesson.

## ## ## Example learning sequence

Plan an investigation on the effects of light on plant growth

  - Review environmental conditions that affect plant growth.

  - Identify variables that need to be controlled and measured during the light investigation. (The variable to be changed
is light.)

  - Develop a question that can be investigated scientifically.

  - Identify risks that are associated with the investigation and plan steps to remove or minimise the risk.

  - Predict the outcome of changing the amount of light each plant receives that demonstrates a simple cause-and-effect
relationship.

  - Plan a method for determining the effect of light on plant growth.

  - Record observations during the investigation.

**Note:** This investigation should have been left for at least one week before recording the 'after' results.

Plan an investigation on the effects of salinity on plant growth

  - Identify variables that need to be controlled and measured during the salinity investigation. (The variable to be
changed is the salt content in the water.)

  - Develop a question that can be investigated scientifically.

  - Identify risks that are associated with the investigation and plan steps to remove or minimise the risk.

  - Predict the outcome of using salt water instead of tank water that demonstrates a simple cause-and-effect
relationship.

  - Plan a method for determining the effect of salt water on plant growth.

  - Record observations during the investigation.

**Note:** This investigation should be left at least one week before recording the 'after' results.

Record and present investigation results

  - Record data from the plant growth from each experiment, testing the effect of light and salinity on plant growth.

  - Construct graphs showing data in a relevant way.

  - Complete investigation questions using recorded data.

  - Discuss the effects of changing environmental conditions on plant growth.

  - Explain how scientific knowledge about growth of plants can be used to solve problems and inform personal and
community decisions.

Learning area specific language:

fair test, variable

## Lesson objectives

Students will:

Understand variables need to be changed, measured and controlled in a fair test.

Plan and conduct an investigation into environmental effects on plant growth.

Represent data using graphs.

Relate experimental data on plant growth to environmental conditions.

Understand how scientific knowledge is used to solve problems and inform decisions.

## Evidence of learning

Can the student:

Identify variables to be changed, measured and controlled?

Plan and conduct an investigation?

Draw graphs to represent data?

Interpret experimental data?

Explain how scientific knowledge is used to solve problems and inform decisions?

## Ideas for monitoring

Monitor students' ability to:

Identify variables to be measured and controlled.

Plan and conduct an investigation.

Represent data using graphs.

Interpret data.

Explain how scientific knowledge is used to solve problems, and inform decisions.

## Learning alerts

Be aware of:

Students thinking that plants need full sunlight all day in order to grow.

## Suggested next steps for learning

Inform students that different plants respond differently to their environment. Some plants are suited to low light conditions, while others require high exposure to sunlight.

## Ideas for differentiation

Support

Provide students with examples of what observations they should (or could) be making to determine the effect of light on plant growth.

Extension

Provide opportunities for students to observe different levels of sunlight (not just full sun or no sun).
