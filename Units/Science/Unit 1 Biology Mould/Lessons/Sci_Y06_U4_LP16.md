## Lesson concepts

## Example learning sequence

Review requirements for living things
- Review the environmental conditions that different living things need to live and grow.
Introduce the concept of introduced species
- Define the terms:
- introduced species --- any living thing that is present in an area where it is not native
- feral --- any living thing that has changed from being domesticated to being wild
- pest --- a living thing that is destructive or considered a nuisance.
- List some of the introduced species common to the local area in particular and Australia in general.
Explore the environments favoured by introduced species
- Explore environments (e.g. use videos) where different introduced species of animal thrive.
- Use a fact sheet to explore the environment where an introduced plant species thrives.
- Review environments where different introduced species thrive.
- Explain why introduced animals and plants cause habitat problems for native species.

## Lesson objectives

Students will:
Analyse information about introduced species and their impact on native plants and animals.

---

## Evidence of learning

Can the student:
Identify environmental conditions that allow introduced species to grow and survive?
Describe the impact of introduced species on native species?

---

## Ideas for monitoring

Monitor students' ability to:
Identify environmental conditions that allow introduced species to grow and survive.
Describe the impact of introduced species.

---

## Learning alerts

Be aware of:
Students thinking that all plants and animals in the wild are native to Australia.

---

## Suggested next steps for learning

Inform students that some plants and animals have been introduced to Australia from other countries; this often results in damage to native plant and animal habitats and reduction in native species.

---

## Ideas for differentiation

## Support

Encourage students to work in small groups to research the different introduced species.

---

## Extension

Ask students to investigate other species that have been introduced to other countries, resulting in a negative impact to the environment.
