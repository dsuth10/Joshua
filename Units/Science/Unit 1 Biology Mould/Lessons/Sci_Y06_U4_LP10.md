## Lesson concepts

## Example learning sequence

Investigate environments where mould can grow
- Review the environmental conditions required for living things.
- Explore environments where mould can be found using the learning object *Mould detective* about mould in the home.
- Describe environments where mould can be found.
Identify that mould has different varieties
- Explore mould growing in different locations in the home.
- Explain that there is a wide variety of different moulds.
Identify the functions of mould in the environment
- Explore the video *Mould exposure*, which highlights the negatives of mould in the environment.
- View the video *Not all mold is bad: About penicillin*, which demonstrates some positives of mould in the environment.
- Categorise mould as either helpful or harmful to other living things.
Compare plant and mould growth environmental factors
- Identify environmental conditions required for different types of mould growth, e.g. wet, moist, warm, warm air, dark.
- Compare the similarities and differences between environmental factors necessary for plant and mould growth.

## Lesson objectives

Students will:
Understand the environments in which mould grows and survives.
Understand the positive and negative effects of mould.

---

## Evidence of learning

Can the student:
Identify environmental factors that affect the survival and growth of mould?
Identify functions of mould in the environment?

---

## Ideas for monitoring

Monitor students' ability to:
Identify environmental factors that affect the survival and growth of mould.
Identify functions of mould in the environment.

---

## Learning alerts

Be aware of:
Students thinking that all mould is bad for humans.

---

## Suggested next steps for learning

Inform students that while some forms of mould are toxic to humans, other varieties such as penicillin are beneficial.
