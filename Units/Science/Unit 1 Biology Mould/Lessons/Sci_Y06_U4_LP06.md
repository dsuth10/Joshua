## Lesson concepts

## Example learning sequence

Review the requirements of living things
- Describe the environmental requirements for living things to survive.
Review research into living things across Asia
- Review research into two different animals and their environments across Asia.
- Explore the predictions and methods used by scientists to conduct their research and gather data.
- Explain how new techniques or technology was able to assist scientists in their research.
Combine research
- Combine information gathered about the study of animals across Asia.
- Explore the similarities in the methods used and the problems faced by researchers in those areas studied.
- Describe how these new techniques or instruments could be used in other areas.

## Lesson objectives

Students will:
Understand how data collection by scientists in the field helps generate a greater understanding of living things and their environment.

---

## Evidence of learning

Can the student:
Identify different methods scientists use for data collection in field work?
Identify reasons for data collection?

---

## Ideas for monitoring

Monitor students' ability to:
Identify different methods scientists use for data collection in field studies.
Identify reasons for data collection.

---

## Learning alerts

Be aware of:
Students thinking that we know everything about common species of animals and how they interact with their environment.

---

## Suggested next steps for learning

Explain to students that new discoveries are being made all the time about animals' interactions with the environment, even very well studied animals.

---

## Support
