## Lesson concepts

## Example learning sequence

Identify environmental factors affecting plant growth
- Discuss the different environmental factors that may affect plant growth, including:
- salinity (amount of salt)
- temperature
- water availability
- sunlight.
- Predict whether these environmental factors could have a positive or negative impact on plant growth.
Conduct a simulation to investigate germination of plants
- Discuss (e.g. view a slideshow) planning and conducting investigations using fair testing.
- Review the concept of a fair test as an investigation where one variable is changed, one is measured and everything else is kept the same.
- Using the learning object *Fair test* and follow instructions to investigate the effects of environmental changes on when a plant germinates or grows.
- Identify variables to be measured, changed and controlled.
- Pose an investigable question to be answered by the investigation.
- Predict the outcome of the investigation that demonstrates a simple cause-and-effect relationship.
- Jointly plan the method for this investigation. (This may vary depending on the variables changed and measured.)
- Conduct the investigation using the online simulation.
- Record the results of this investigation.
Graph relevant data using online tools
- Construct a graph of the recorded data using online graph makers.
- Identify all the important features of a graph.
- Use the constructed graphs to write a conclusion to the investigation.
Conduct a fair test independently
- Record (e.g. use a learning object or Science journal) the steps needed to conduct a fair test:
- Identify variables to be measured, changed and controlled.
- Pose a scientific question to be answered by the investigation.
- Predict the outcome of the investigation that demonstrates a simple cause-and-effect relationship.
- Plan the steps to be taken in the investigation.
- Conduct the investigation.
- Record and present the data gained through the investigation.
- Present the findings of the investigation to the class.
Identify environmental factors affecting growth
- Identify the environmental factors considered, e.g. nutrients (liquid solution), type of light, light intensity, light duration, temperature, amount of water.
- Describe the effect that different environmental factors had on the growth of the plants in the simulation.
- Categorise these effects as either positive or negative.
- Discuss how scientific knowledge about growth of plants can be used to solve problems and inform personal and community decisions.
- Explore how understanding factors that affect plant growth can be used by people in their occupations and daily lives.

## Lesson objectives

Students will:
Plan and conduct investigations using a fair test.
Predict and analyse the results of a fair test.

---

## Evidence of learning

Can the student:
Plan and conduct an investigation using a fair test?
Predict and analyse results of a fair test?

---

## Ideas for monitoring

Monitor students' ability to:
Plan and conduct investigations using a fair test.
Predict and analyse the results of a fair test.

---

## Learning alerts

Be aware of:
Students thinking that changing a plant's environment can only have a negative impact on its growth and survival.

---

## Suggested next steps for learning

Explain to students that some environmental factors can have a positive influence on a plant's growth, some may have either a positive or negative effect, and some may indeed have a negative effect.

---

## Ideas for differentiation

## Support

Allow students to work in small groups to plan and conduct their simulated investigation.

---

## Extension

Provide students with the opportunity to test some of the more complicated environmental factors and their effect on plant growth, e.g. soil acidity.
