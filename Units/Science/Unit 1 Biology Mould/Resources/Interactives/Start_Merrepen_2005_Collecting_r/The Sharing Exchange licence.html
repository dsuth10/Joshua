Introduction

The Sharing Exchange is one deliverable of the Sharing Exchange Service Project for The Le@rning Federation Phase Three plan. The primary goal of the Sharing Exchange is to provide a national level infrastructure to share and distribute quality-assured digital learning content where further payments are not required under statutory licences.

The Sharing Exchange service project will provide a platform for educators to explore collaborative opportunities and to share ideas during the digital content development process. This in turn will facilitate national level engagement in development of appropriate content for sharing at National level. Thus, the repository will be a significant ‘one-stop shop’ of rich and diverse digital content pool, difficult to source and access in any other way.
