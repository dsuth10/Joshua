**How**** ****to**** ****make a simple**** ****switch****:**** T****eacher**** ****note**

You can make a simple and inexpensive switch using some materials found in the classroom. Below are the instructions for two different switches and a picture of each one.

**SWITCH**** ****ONE**

**Materials:**

- 8 cm x 4 cm thick cardboard (corrugated card)

- metal paper clip

- two split pins

**Procedure:**

- Place the thick cardboard down lengthways (landscape).

- Place the paper clip lengthways on the middle of the cardboard.

- Mark each end of the paper clip.

- Place one split pin through the cardboard at one end of the split pin.

- Push the other split pin through one end of the paper clip and attach to the cardboard.

- Check underneath the cardboard that the arms of the split pins are not touching.

- The wires of your circuit can be attached to the ‘arms’ of the split pins.

- Swing the paper clip so that it can connect with the split pin.

**SWITCH**** ****TWO**

**Materials:**

- 8 cm x 4 cm thick cardboard (corrugated card)

- aluminium foil

- 2 x drawing pins

**Procedure:**

- Place the thick cardboard down lengthways (landscape).

- Fold a strip of aluminium foil lengthways to form a strip.

- Push one drawing pin through one end of the aluminium strip.

- Wrap the circuit wire around the shaft of the pin and push into the cardboard.

- Wrap a second circuit wire around the shaft of the second pin and push into the cardboard near the top of the aluminium strip.

- The aluminium strip can then be rotated in and out of contact with the second drawing pin to open and close the switch.
