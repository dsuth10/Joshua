For an electrical circuit to work properly, electricity must be able to flow around the circuit freely. This is called a complete** **(or closed) circuit. When electricity isn’t flowing around a circuit, it is called an incomplete (or open) circuit.

Adding a switch to a complete circuit allows the flow of electricity to be turned on or off. When the switch is open, electricity will not flow (incomplete circuit). When the switch is closed, the electricity will flow (complete circuit).
