**Requirements for a complete circuit**

An electrical circuit will only work if it is **complete**.

A simple circuit can consist of two wires, a cell (the power source) and a bulb (the load).

When connected correctly these four parts form a complete circuit.

This complete circuit will cause the light bulb to produce light.

You will be provided with the four parts (2 x wires, 1 x light bulb, 1 x cell) so that you can create a complete circuit.
