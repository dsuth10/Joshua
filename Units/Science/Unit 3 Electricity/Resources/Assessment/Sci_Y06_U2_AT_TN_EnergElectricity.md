**Prior to Part ****A****: Circuits**

**Teacher:**

- Access and become familiar with the assessment resources.

- Collect the required materials and equipment to prepare components (refer to the *Materials and equipment list* for this unit).

- In Part A, students will be required to test four **individual components** of a Steady Hand Game that is malfunctioning at the local show. The Steady Hand Game **does not need to be constructed**.

- Three out of the four components require **no assembly**, as shown below:

| **Metal course** | **Buzzer** | **Light bulb** |
| --- | --- | --- |

- It is advised that teachers test the buzzers and light bulbs **prior to the assessment lessons** to ensure they are in working order.

- One component does require assembly — the wire loop, handle and connecting wire. **N****ote:** This part of the Steady Hand Game includes a **deliberate fault** (i.e. shown in Step 2) that students must identify as part of the assessment task. Use the casing of a pen or a piece of wooden dowel for the handle. Use electrical tape or similar to secure the loop and connecting wire to the handle and visibly disguise the fault.
