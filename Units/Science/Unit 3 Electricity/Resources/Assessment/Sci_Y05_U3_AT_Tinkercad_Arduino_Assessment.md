### Task Purpose
You will construct and analyse a series of electrical circuits using **Tinkercad Circuits** and an **Arduino Uno power setup**, demonstrating your understanding of complete circuits, switches, and energy transformations. You will also use scientific knowledge to evaluate energy sources and map the transformations required to generate electricity for specific communities.

---

### Instructions
This assessment task consists of two parts:

- **Part A: Practical Circuit Construction & Analysis (Tinkercad Circuits)**  
  Build, simulate, diagram, and analyse a progressive series of three simple circuits in Tinkercad Circuits:
  1. *Circuit 1:* Simple LED Light Circuit (Complete electrical pathway & single output)
  2. *Circuit 2:* Switched Circuit (Controlling current flow with a switch)
  3. *Circuit 3:* Dual-Output Alarm Circuit (Simultaneous light and sound outputs)
- **Part B: Energy Sources & Electricity Generation**  
  Select, diagram energy transformations for, and evaluate suitable energy sources to supply electricity to two remote Queensland locations (an Island and a Desert community).

## Assessment Rubric & Achievement Standards Guide

**Purpose of Assessment:** To analyse requirements for the transfer of electrical energy in circuits and systems; describe how energy transforms from one form to another to generate electricity; and explain how scientific knowledge is used to evaluate energy sources for specific purposes.

| Standard | Physical Sciences (Science Understanding) | Use and Influence of Science (SHE) | Communicating & Questioning (Inquiry Skills) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A** | • Comprehensively constructs and explains the 3-circuit sequence in Tinkercad (simple, switched, and dual-output).<br>• Thoroughly explains complete vs open circuits, switch mechanisms, and multiple simultaneous energy transformations with accurate scientific terminology.<br>• Accurately maps and explains multi-step energy transformation chains for electricity generation. | • In-depth, insightful explanation of how scientific and technological knowledge of energy generation informs community and operational decisions. | • Constructs sophisticated, accurate schematic and breadboard layout representations using standard symbols.<br>• Communicates findings using precise scientific vocabulary and multi-modal diagrams. |
| **B** | • Clearly constructs the 3-circuit sequence in Tinkercad.<br>• Accurately explains requirements for complete circuits, switch functions, and energy transformations (electrical to light and sound).<br>• Accurately describes energy transformations from source to electricity generation. | • Explains how knowledge of electricity generation helps solve practical problems and evaluates advantages and disadvantages of energy sources effectively. | • Constructs clear, correctly labelled circuit diagrams and Tinkercad layouts.<br>• Communicates ideas clearly using appropriate scientific language. |
| **C** | • Builds basic working circuits in Tinkercad with teacher guidance.<br>• Identifies simple circuit requirements (closed path) and describes basic energy transformations (electrical to light/sound).<br>• Identifies basic advantages and disadvantages of selected energy sources. | • Describes how scientific knowledge helps solve practical problems. | • Constructs basic circuit representations and records observations in provided tables.<br>• Communicates ideas using everyday and basic scientific language. |
| **D** | • Constructs a simple circuit with assistance.<br>• Identifies a single component or form of energy.<br>• Lists a simple advantage or disadvantage. | • Mentions a practical use of electricity in daily life. | • Incomplete circuit representations or tables.<br>• Uses fragmented, everyday language. |
| **E** | • Partial recognition of electrical components or energy forms.<br>• Incomplete responses with significant misconceptions. | • Minimal or unattempted response regarding science influence. | • Fragmented communication without scientific representation. |
