# TC04: Challenge 3 — Take Control

## Purpose
To design, simulate, and build a switched circuit to understand how a switch controls the flow of electricity, and to compare maintained (slide/rocker) and momentary (push-button) switches.

---

## Equipment

### Tinkercad Circuits Phase
- iPad/Chromebook/computer with access to [Tinkercad Circuits](https://www.tinkercad.com/circuits).

### Physical Build Phase
- 1x Battery Holder (with 2x AA cells)
- 1x Light Bulb (in lamp holder base)
- 1x Slide Switch or Rocker Switch
- 1x Push-Button Switch
- Connecting wires with alligator clips

---

## Safety Check
1. **Always Disconnect Power:** Turn off or unplug the battery pack *before* making any changes to the wires.
2. **Prevent Short Circuits:** Do not connect a wire directly from the red (positive) battery terminal to the black (negative) terminal without a bulb or load in the middle. This makes the batteries get very hot!
3. **Handle Bulbs Gently:** Light bulbs are made of thin glass and can break. Handle them carefully.
4. **🛑 Teacher Checkpoint:** You must show the teacher your physical circuit before turning the battery pack switch ON.

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Predict
Open your journal **JN04**. Under Section 2, record your prediction of what the switch will do to the path when it is toggled.

### Step 2: Tinkercad Prototyping
1. Log in to Tinkercad and create a new **Circuit** design.
2. Drag a **9V Battery** (or **1.5V Battery pack**), a **Slide Switch**, and a **Light Bulb** onto your workspace.
3. Wire them in a single, closed loop:
   - Connect the battery positive terminal to the slide switch's **common (middle) terminal (Terminal 2)**.
   - Connect one of the outer switch terminals (**Terminal 1**) to one terminal of the light bulb.
   - Connect the other light bulb terminal back to the battery negative terminal.
4. Click **Start Simulation**. Toggle the slide switch. 
5. Verify the bulb turns on and off. Take a screenshot of the simulation when the bulb is ON and paste it into Section 4 of **JN04**.

### Step 3: Physical Build (maintained switch)
1. Ensure your physical battery pack is switched **OFF** or disconnected.
2. Using alligator clip leads, replicate your Tinkercad circuit on your desk:
   - Connect battery positive to the middle contact of your slide/rocker switch.
   - Connect an outer contact of the switch to one terminal of the lamp holder.
   - Connect the other terminal of the lamp holder back to the battery negative terminal.
3. **🛑 STOP:** Call the teacher to check your connections.
4. With teacher approval, switch the battery pack **ON** and test the switch. What happens when you flip it? Does it stay in position?

### Step 4: Compare a Push-Button (momentary switch)
1. Turn the battery pack **OFF**.
2. Replace the slide switch with the **Push-Button Switch** in the same position in the circuit.
3. Turn the battery pack **ON**. Observe the bulb.
4. Press and hold the button. What happens? Release your finger. What happens?
5. Write your comparison observations in the table in Section 4 of **JN04**.

---

## Success Test
Your circuit is successful if:
- [ ] The slide switch turns the light bulb on and holds it on without you touching it.
- [ ] The push-button switch turns the bulb on *only* when your finger is holding the button down.
- [ ] All connections are firm and do not fall apart.

---

## Troubleshooting
- **Bulb won't light up (Slide Switch):** Check if you connected wires to the middle pin (Common) and an outer pin of the switch. If you connect wires to the two outer pins, the switch will not operate correctly.
- **Loose Contacts:** Ensure the alligator clips are gripping the metal terminals tightly, not the plastic insulation.
- **Dead Battery/Bulb:** If your circuit matches the diagram perfectly but won't light up, ask the teacher to test the battery and bulb.

---

## Pack-up Instructions
1. Switch the battery pack **OFF**.
2. Disconnect all alligator clip leads carefully. Do not yank the wires.
3. Sort the components back into your team's organizer box:
   - Wind the leads neatly.
   - Separate the switches.
   - Ensure bulbs are safely secured in their boxes.
4. Return the organizer box to the equipment shelf.
