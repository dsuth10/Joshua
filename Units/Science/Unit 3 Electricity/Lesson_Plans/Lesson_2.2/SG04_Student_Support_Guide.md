# SG04: Student Support Guide — Circuit Symbols Cheat Sheet

This guide shows you how to map physical circuit parts to the symbols used in circuit diagrams.

---

## 1. Symbol Mapping Key

| Physical Part | Standard Symbol | What to remember |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Wire** | `──────────` | Draw as a straight line. Do not draw curved lines. |
| **Cell / Battery** | `───│ ┃───` | Draw one long thin line (positive) and one short thick line (negative). |
| **Light Bulb** | `───⨂───` | Draw a circle with an 'X' inside. Do not draw the bulb base. |
| **Open Switch (OFF)** | `───○ ┌  ○───` | The slanted line points UP. This shows the gap in the wire. |
| **Closed Switch (ON)** | `───○───○───` | The flat line touches both circles. This shows a complete path. |

---

## 2. Drawing Self-Check Checklist

When drawing your circuit diagram, ask yourself these three questions:

- [ ] **Are my lines straight?** Use a ruler or grid lines. Do not draw circular loops or squiggles.
- [ ] **Are my corners sharp?** Make sure wires turn at neat 90-degree corners (like a square box).
- [ ] **Did I use symbols?** Never draw pictures of the actual battery holder, bulb threads, or switch body. Only use the standard symbols shown above.

---

## 3. Switch State Reminder

- **Open Switch = OFF:** The bridge is **UP**. Electricity cannot cross the air gap. The load turns OFF.
- **Closed Switch = ON:** The bridge is **DOWN**. The path is completed. Electricity flows and the load turns ON.
