# TA01: Teacher Guide - Energy all around us

## Expected Answers

**Source-Form Sorter (TP01 / WS01)**
- *Sources:* Coal, The Sun, Battery, Wind
- *Forms:* Light, Heat, Electrical, Sound

**Classroom Energy Hunt (TC01)**
- *Smartboard:* Source = Mains Electricity (Grid); Forms = Light, Sound, Heat.
- *Laptop:* Source = Battery; Forms = Light, Sound, Heat.
- *Ceiling Fan:* Source = Mains Electricity; Forms = Kinetic (Movement), Sound.

**Energy Chain Exit Check**
- Example: Battery -> Electrical -> Light + Heat (for a torch).
- Example: Mains Electricity -> Electrical -> Kinetic + Sound (for a fan).

## Misconception Alerts
1. **"Electricity is a source."** 
   - *Reality:* Electricity is an energy *carrier* or *form*. We often casually say "it gets its energy from electricity", but scientifically, the source is the mains grid (which gets it from coal/solar/hydro) or a battery. If students say electricity is a source, prompt them: "Where did the electricity come from?"
2. **"Energy gets used up."**
   - *Reality:* Energy transforms. A battery doesn't run out of energy; its chemical potential energy has just fully transformed into electrical energy, and eventually heat/light. 

## Safety Notes
- Emphasise the "Look, don't touch" rule during the Energy Hunt. Year 6 students must not interact with mains power points or frayed cables.

## Conferencing Prompts (During Practical)
- "You wrote 'Heat' for this laptop. Why do you think a laptop produces heat? Is that its main job?" (Leads to understanding that some energy transformations are "wasted" energy).
- "I see you chose the ceiling fan. What form of energy makes the blades spin?" (Kinetic/Movement).
