# TC01: Classroom Energy Hunt

## Purpose
To find evidence of energy transformations in the real world by identifying objects, their energy sources, and the forms of energy they produce.

## Safety Check
- **Look, don't touch!** Do not unplug any cords or touch power points. 
- If you find an object with a frayed wire, tell the teacher immediately. Do not investigate it yourself.

## Equipment
- Your OneNote Journal (JN01) on your device.
- A camera/tablet to take photos.

## Steps
1. Walk around the classroom safely with your partner.
2. Find **three** different objects that are currently using energy (or can use energy).
3. Discuss with your partner: 
   - *What is the Energy Source? (e.g. Battery, Mains Electricity, The Sun)*
   - *What Energy Forms does it make? (e.g. Light, Sound, Heat, Kinetic/Movement)*
4. Take a clear photograph of your favourite object.
5. Return to your desk and fill in the **Classroom Energy Hunt** table in your journal.
6. Insert the photograph into your journal.

## Success Test
Check your table. Did you make sure that "Electricity" is listed as a source (mains) or did you write "Battery"? Are your forms things you can see, hear, or feel?
