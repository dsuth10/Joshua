# VC01: Transcript and Cue Sheet (Energy Transformation)

**Focus Question (Before viewing):** 
Watch carefully to see what happens to the energy from the sun after it hits the solar panel.

## Storyboard & Transcript

| Timestamp | Visual | Audio Transcript |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 - 0:15 | Animation: The Sun shining brightly in space, zooming down to a house with solar panels on the roof. | **Narrator**: Energy is all around us. But it doesn't just stay in one place. It moves and changes. It starts at an energy source, like the Sun. |
| 0:15 - 0:30 | Close up: Sunbeams (light energy) hitting the blue silicon cells of the solar panel. Sparkles indicate transformation. | **Narrator**: The solar panels on this roof take the light energy from the sun and transform it—or change it—into electrical energy. |
| 0:30 - 0:45 | Animation: A glowing wire representing electrical energy travelling down from the roof, into the house, and to a desk lamp. | **Narrator**: That electrical energy travels through the wires in the house, acting like a bridge. |
| 0:45 - 1:00 | Close up: The desk lamp turns on, emitting light and a little bit of heat (shown as wavy red lines). | **Narrator**: Finally, the lamp transforms the electrical energy back into light energy so you can see, and a little bit of heat energy too! Energy never disappears; it just changes form. |
