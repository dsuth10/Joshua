# SG03: Student Support Guide — Circuit Troubleshooting Cheat Sheet

This guide helps you trace your circuit loops and find common errors that stop electricity from flowing.

---

## 1. Tracing the Closed Path (The Loop)

Before calling the teacher to look at a circuit that isn't working, try the **Finger Trace Test**:

```text
  [+] Battery Terminal ────(Trace along the wire)────> [Bulb]
                                                          │
  [-] Battery Terminal <───(Trace along the wire)─────────┘
```

- **Step 1:** Put your index finger on the **positive (+) terminal** of the battery holder.
- **Step 2:** Slide your finger along the wire. Ensure it connects to the metal contact screw of the bulb holder.
- **Step 3:** Trace the path *through* the bulb filament to the other bulb holder terminal contact.
- **Step 4:** Slide your finger along the second wire back to the **negative (-) terminal** of the battery holder.
- **Did your finger cross a gap?** If yes, you have an **incomplete path** (open circuit). Find a way to bridge the gap!

## 2. Three Common Circuit Faults (How to Spot and Fix)

| What it looks like | What the problem is | How to fix it |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Trapped Insulation** | The alligator clip jaw is biting the plastic colored sleeve of the wire, not the metal copper core inside. | Unclip the lead, pull the sleeve back slightly, and clip onto the **bare metal** wires or screw. |
| **Floating Wire** | The wire tip is resting inside the holder or nearby, but it is not physically touching the metal contact plate. | Push the wire tip fully under the terminal screw and **tighten** the screw to pinch the metal. |
| **Open Switch** | The switch lever or rocker is in the OFF position, creating an air gap inside. | Flip the switch to **ON** (closed) to bring the internal contacts together. |

---

## 3. Insulator vs Conductor Quick Test

If you are not sure if a material is stopping your path, check its class:
- **Conductors (Allows flow):** Copper wire, aluminum foil, steel paperclips, brass keys, water (mostly).
- **Insulators (Blocks flow):** Plastic wire sleeves, dry paper, cardboard, rubber bands, air (gaps).

*Rule:* If you have an insulator in the middle of your loop, the circuit will remain incomplete and the bulb will not light.
