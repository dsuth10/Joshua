# TC03: Challenge 2 — Complete the Path

## Purpose
To develop systematic troubleshooting skills by identifying why a circuit fails to light a bulb, repair the break physically and digitally, and explain the difference between a complete and incomplete circuit.

---

## Equipment

### Tinkercad Circuits Phase
- iPad/Chromebook/computer with access to [Tinkercad Circuits](https://www.tinkercad.com/circuits).

### Physical Build Phase
- 1x Battery Holder (with 2x AA cells)
- 1x Light Bulb (in lamp holder base)
- Connecting wires with alligator clips
- 1x Switch (rocker or slide)
- Scrap paper or plastic card (to act as a temporary insulator)

---

## Safety Check
1. **Always Disconnect Power:** Turn off or unplug the battery pack *before* making any changes to the wires.
2. **Short Circuit Alert:** Do not connect a wire directly from one battery terminal to the other without a bulb or load in the loop. This causes the wires to heat up very quickly and can drain or damage the batteries.
3. **Handle Clips Carefully:** Grip the plastic sleeves of alligator clips, not the metal jaws.
4. **🛑 Teacher Checkpoint:** You must show the teacher your physical circuit before turning the battery pack switch ON.

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Predict
Open your journal **JN03**. Under Section 2, look at the launch diagram. Record your prediction of whether the circuit will operate and why.

### Step 2: Tinkercad Troubleshooting Phase
1. Log in to Tinkercad and create a new **Circuit** design.
2. Open or construct the **Four Fault Cases** (based on the layouts provided in the presentation):
   - **Case A: The Unconnected Battery** — One battery wire is floating.
   - **Case B: The Trapped Clip** — The connection clip is touching the plastic insulation instead of the metal wire.
   - **Case C: The Floating Wire** — Wires are nearby but not actually touching the bulb holder contacts.
   - **Case D: The Open Switch** — The switch in the path is turned OFF (open).
3. Click **Start Simulation** for each case. Observe the bulb.
4. Diagnose the fault. Modify the connections in Tinkercad to repair each circuit.
5. In Section 4 of **JN03**, record whether the bulb lit, the fault you found, and how you repaired it.
6. Take a screenshot of one successfully repaired Tinkercad circuit (with the bulb ON) and paste it into **JN03**.

### Step 3: Physical Build & Partner Challenge
1. Ensure your battery pack is switched **OFF**.
2. Construct a simple working circuit (Battery -> Bulb -> Battery) using alligator clips. Verify it works, then switch it **OFF**.
3. **The Hidden Fault Challenge:** Working with your partner, one person turns around while the other introduces a deliberate fault. 
   - *Example faults:* Grip the insulation sleeve instead of the metal screw terminal; insert a tiny piece of paper (insulator) between a clip and a terminal contact; leave a wire slightly loose.
4. Turn back around. Switch the battery **ON**. The bulb should remain dark.
5. **Diagnose and Repair:** Trace the path systematically from the positive terminal to the negative terminal. Locate the break, explain the fault to your partner, and fix it.
6. Swap roles and repeat the challenge!

---

## Success Test
Your circuit is successful if:
- [ ] You can trace a continuous, closed path of metal connectors from the battery, through the lamp, and back.
- [ ] You successfully identify and repair the fault introduced by your partner.
- [ ] All wire clips grip metal contacts firmly, with no plastic insulation trapped in the jaws.

---

## Troubleshooting
- **Bulb won't light up but wires look connected:**
  - Check if the alligator jaws are clamping the metal part of the bulb holder or the plastic/insulated part.
  - Verify that the batteries are inserted in the correct direction (+ and - aligned).
  - Ensure the bulb is screwed firmly into its socket.
- **Circuit gets warm:** Switch off the battery immediately! This means there is a short circuit (electricity is flowing directly from + to - without passing through the bulb). Re-trace your wires.

---

## Pack-up Instructions
1. Switch the battery pack **OFF**.
2. Disconnect all alligator clips carefully. Do not pull on the insulated wires; squeeze the clips to release them.
3. Wind the leads neatly.
4. Sort and place all components back into your team's organizer box.
5. Return the box to the storage shelf.
