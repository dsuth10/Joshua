📜 Scientific Symbol Reference

A summary of standard symbols used in Year 6 Science. Use this reference when drawing your diagrams on grid paper.

Component Standard Symbol Definition & Rule
Wire A straight path of metal conducting material. Draw with straight lines.
Cell (1.5V Battery) Long thin line represents the positive (+) terminal, short thick line represents negative (-).
Light Bulb A device that slows flow and transforms electrical energy to light and heat.
Open Switch A controlled break (air gap) that stops the electric current. State = OFF.
Closed Switch Metal contacts touch, completing the conducting path. State = ON.
