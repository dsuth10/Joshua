# Unit 2 (Year 6): Energy and Electricity — Unit Summary & Lesson Objectives

This document provides a comprehensive overview of the Year 6 Science unit **Energy and electricity**, detailing the objectives for each lesson and summarising the unit structure into two core parts.

---

## Part 1: Electrical Circuits, Conductivity, and Safety
This part of the unit focuses on the physics of electrical circuits, constructing and diagramming circuits, investigating the properties of conductors and insulators, and applying safety measures at home and school.

*   **Reviewing Energy Forms:** Students begin by reviewing the different forms of energy in daily life (heat, light, movement, sound, electrical) to understand that energy can be generated from multiple sources.
*   **Circuit Construction and Diagramming:** Students learn the practical requirements of circuit building (forming complete loops with cells, wires, sockets, bulbs, and buzzers), the functioning of switches, and the standardised schematic representations used to draw circuit diagrams.
*   **Conductivity Properties:** Students investigate materials to distinguish between electrical conductors (metals, graphite, salt water) and insulators (plastics, rubber, card), describing their real-world purposes.
*   **Safety Campaign & Professional Practice:** Students explore how household circuits function similarly to simple circuits, study the personal protective equipment (PPE) worn by electricians, and design a multimodal school campaign to promote electrical safety.

---

## Part 2: Electricity Generation, Energy Sources, and Sustainability
This part of the unit focuses on the processes and energy transformations involved in generating electricity, comparing fossil fuels and renewable energy sources, and exploring alternative bioenergy solutions.

*   **Fossil Fuels vs. Renewable Sources:** Students explore how fossil fuels (coal, gas, oil) are formed and the energy transformations required to generate electricity from them. They compare this with renewable sources (solar, wind, hydro, geothermal) used in Australia.
*   **Sustainability and Drawbacks:** Students analyse the environmental impact, availability, and sustainability of different energy sources, distinguishing between "renewable" (regenerable in a short time) and "sustainable" (long-term availability).
*   **Alternative Bioenergy Solutions:** Students investigate biowaste (food scraps, organic waste) and sugarcane bagasse as sustainable alternative fuels to generate electricity.
*   **Assessment of Concepts:** Students demonstrate their cumulative understanding through supervised assessments on circuit analysis and energy transformations, alongside monitored practical activities on conductors and energy sustainability.
