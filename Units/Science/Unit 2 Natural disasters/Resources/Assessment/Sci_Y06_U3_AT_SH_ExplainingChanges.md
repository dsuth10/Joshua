  - Column 1: **6**

  - Column 3: 40–50km/h
  - Column 4: 22–27

- **Row 11:**
  - Column 1: **7**
  - Column 2: Near gale
  - Column 3: 51–62km/h
  - Column 4: 28–33
  - Column 5: Sea heaps up, white foam streaks off breakers
  - Column 6: Whole trees moving, resistance felt walking against wind
- **Row 12:**
  - Column 1: **8**
  - Column 2: Gale
  - Column 3: 83–75km/h
  - Column 4: 34–40

  - Column 6: Twigs breaking off trees, generally impedes progress
- **Row 13:**
  - Column 1: **9**
  - Column 2: Strong gale
  - Column 3: 76–87km/h
  - Column 4: 41–47
  - Column 5: High waves, sea begins to roll, dense streaks of foam, spray may reduce visibility
  - Column 6: Slight structural damage occurs, slate blows off roofs
- **Row 14:**
  - Column 1: **10**
  - Column 2: Storm
  - Column 3: 88–102km/h
  - Column 4: 48–55
  - Column 5: Very high waves with overhanging crests, sea white with densely blown foam, heavy rolling, and lowered visibility. The sea becomes ‘white’ in appearance.
  - Column 6: Seldom experienced on land, trees broken or uprooted, ‘considerable structural damage’
- **Row 15:**
  - Column 1: **11**
  - Column 2: Violent storm
  - Column 3: 103–117km/h
  - Column 4: 56–63
  - Column 5: Exceptionally high waves, foam patches cover sea, visibility more reduced
  - Column 6: Very rare — extensive damage
- **Row 16:**
  - Column 1: **12**
  - Column 2: Hurricane
  - Column 3: 118 km/h or more
  - Column 4: 64+
  - Column 5: Air filled with foam, huge waves, sea completely white with driving spray, visibility greatly reduced
  - Column 6: Very rare — extensive damage
