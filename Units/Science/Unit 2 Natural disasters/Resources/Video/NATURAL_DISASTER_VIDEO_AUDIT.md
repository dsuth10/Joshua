# Natural Disaster Video Audit

This document provides a summary of the natural disaster video segments found in the `C:\Users\dsuth\Videos` directory. Most of these segments are from the Australian educational program **Behind The News (BTN)**.

## Bushfires
- **Black Summer Aftermath**: Reflects on the 2019-20 bushfires, focusing on the scale of destruction, recovery efforts, and the emotional resilience of affected communities.
- **Bushfire Escape**: An educational segment teaching practical survival skills, such as wearing protective clothing and using heavy-duty wool blankets for sheltering.
- **Bushfire Heroes**: Highlights personal stories of bravery from children and volunteers during bushfire crises, emphasizing community spirit.
- **Bushfire Plan**: Focuses on the importance of creating and annually updating a Bushfire Survival Plan, including establishing "trigger points" for evacuation.
- **Bushfire Season**: Explains the scientific "fire triangle" (fuel, oxygen, heat) and explores the role of Indigenous cultural burning in land management.
- **Don't Panic**: A safety-focused segment designed to help young people understand the science of bushfires to reduce fear and promote calm preparedness.
- **Fire Rating Update**: Explains the updated Australian Fire Danger Rating System (Moderate, High, Extreme, Catastrophic) and the specific actions required for each level.

## Cyclones
- **Cyclone**: A general educational introduction to tropical cyclones, their formation, and how they impact coastal communities.
- **Cyclone Alfred**: Reports on the early 2025 event in Southeast Queensland, covering its downgrade to a tropical low and the resulting record-breaking power outages.
- **Cyclone Debbie**: Covers the devastating impact of the 2017 Category 4 storm in North Queensland and the subsequent community resilience and recovery.
- **Cyclones Explained**: A scientific breakdown of how cyclones form over warm ocean waters and how they are categorized based on wind speed.

## Drought
- **Drought Kids**: Explores the personal and financial impact of drought on children living on farms, highlighting their increased responsibilities and community resilience.
- **Northern Hemisphere Drought**: Covers the 2022 global drought event that affected major rivers like the Rhine and Yangtze, disrupting energy and food security.
- **Queensland Drought**: Details the severe dry spells in Queensland (notably 2017-18), explaining the economic consequences for farmers and consumers.

## Earthquakes & Tsunamis
- **Afghanistan Earthquake**: Reports on the 2022 earthquake in remote provinces, focusing on the challenges of rescue efforts in difficult terrain.
- **Aussie Earthquakes**: Explains the science of "intraplate" earthquakes following the 2023 Melbourne tremor and provides safety tips.
- **Japan Kids**: Focuses on the resilience of Japanese children following the 2011 Tōhoku earthquake and tsunami as they returned to school.
- **Lombok Earthquake**: Covers the 2018 Indonesian quakes, including the dramatic rescue of hikers stranded on Mount Rinjani.
- **Morocco Earthquake**: Reports on the 2023 High Atlas Mountains quake, focusing on the destruction of remote villages and historic sites.
- **Nepal Earthquake**: Details the 2015 disaster in Kathmandu, highlighting the global effort to rebuild schools and support affected children.
- **NZ Earthquake**: Covers the major seismic events in Christchurch (2011) and Kaikoura (2016), focusing on the long-term rebuilding of these communities.
- **The Science of Earthquakes**: A comprehensive scientific explanation of tectonic plates, fault lines, hypocenters, and the measurement of seismic waves.
- **Tsunami**: Explains how undersea disturbances create massive waves and emphasizes the importance of recognizing natural warning signs.
- **Türkiye and Syria Earthquakes**: Reports on the 2023 magnitude 7.8/7.5 disasters, providing geological context on the Anatolian Fault System.
- **Why was the Türkiye and Syria Earthquake So Deadly**: An analytical follow-up examining how timing, shallow depth, and building quality contributed to the high fatality rate.

## Floods
- **Climate Disaster Impacts**: Based on a UNICEF report, this segment explores how extreme weather events like floods are affecting the health and rights of millions of children.
- **Flood Cleanup**: Details the massive 2025 cleanup efforts in New South Wales, the costs of infrastructure repair, and the work of SES volunteers.
- **Flooded School**: Follows the recovery of Mypolonga Primary School after the Murray River floods, featuring perspectives from students and staff.
- **Floods**: A general overview of the causes of flooding and the safety measures communities take to prepare for rising waters.
- **Floods Update**: Provides an update on recent flooding events, focusing on community response and ongoing emergency warnings.
- **Pakistan Floods**: Covers the 2022 "climate catastrophe" that displaced millions of people and devastated Pakistan's agricultural sector.
- **Queensland Floods**: Reviews the repeated flood cycles in Queensland and highlights educational programs like "The Pillowcase Project" for disaster readiness.
- **Spain Floods**: Reports on the 2026 Storm Leonardo, discussing the link between warming ocean temperatures and more frequent extreme rainfall events.

## Volcanoes
- **Ring of Fire**: Explains the geology of the Pacific's horseshoe-shaped belt where most of the world's volcanic and seismic activity occurs.
- **Tonga Volcano**: Covers the massive 2022 Hunga Tonga eruption, detailing its atmospheric impact, the resulting tsunami, and the recovery challenges.
- **Tourist Volcanoes**: Explores the trend of volcano tourism, balancing the thrill of seeing Earth's power with the inherent dangers and safety management.
