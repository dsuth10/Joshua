Preparation for weather events --- Identifying preparation for extreme weather events

## Lesson concepts

#### Note: Teachers should be aware that students may have experienced some of the geological or extreme weather events learned about in this unit. Consider the needs of these students when discussing relevant events.

---

## Example learning sequence

Track the path of a cyclone
- View a weather map to identify the path taken by a cyclone off the coast of Queensland.
- View a cyclone tracking map that can be used to measure the distance of a cyclone from a local area.
- Discuss the benefits for people in the local area of tracking a cyclone.
- Explain how forecasting and predicting the path of a cyclone can minimise the impact of the extreme weather event.
Identify safety measures during extreme weather events
- Recognise the Standard Emergency Warning Signal.
- Explore safety measures that can be taken in preparation and during an extreme weather event, using the cyclone tracking map resource.
- Describe how these safety preparation measures can help reduce the impact of an extreme weather event.
Identify items useful in an emergency kit
- Identify items that would be useful during an extreme weather event.
- Explain why items would be necessary in a safety kit that will be used during an emergency.
- Create an emergency plan that details the actions you should take and the items you would require in the event of a cyclone.

## Lesson objectives

Students will:
Understand the benefits of mapping the progress of a tropical cyclone.
Explore safety preparation measures needed for an extreme weather event.

---

## Evidence of learning

Can the student:
Explain why scientists record the progress of a tropical cyclone on a tracking map?
Describe precautions that should be taken to prepare for an extreme weather event?

---

## Ideas for monitoring

Monitor students' ability to:
Describe reasons for recording the progress of a tropical cyclone on a tracking map?
Describe the steps that can be taken to prepare for an extreme weather event?

---

## Learning alerts

Be aware of:
Students thinking that in an extreme weather event there is little that can be done to prevent disaster.

---

## Suggested next steps for learning

Explain to students that preparations, if given enough time, can minimise the impact of many extreme weather events.
