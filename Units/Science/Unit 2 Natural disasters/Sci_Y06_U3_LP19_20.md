Forecasting and warnings --- Researching technologies for weather forecasting and warning

---

## Lesson concepts

#### Note: Teachers should be aware that students may have experienced some of the geological or extreme weather events learned about in this unit. Consider the needs of these students when discussing relevant events.

---

## Example learning sequence

Explore global alerts
- Explore disaster alerts from sources from around the world using a website that alerts global disasters.
- Identify how alerts alter the behaviour of people in an area to reduce the impact of the natural disaster.
Research modern modes of communication for alerts
- Explore the current option for the distribution of extreme weather advice for a severe thunderstorm.
- Identify new and innovative ways for technology to be used to enhance this distribution.
Explore the conditions that justify an alert
- Identify the requirements for a disaster alert to be issued.
- Construct a sample warning with extreme weather advice using a severe thunderstorm warning as a guide.

## Ideas for monitoring

Monitor students' ability to:

---

## Learning alerts

Be aware of:
Students thinking that the issuing of a disaster alert guarantees that the disaster will occur.

---

## Suggested next steps for learning

Explain to students that a disaster alert is issued as a precaution and that an alert does not always mean a disaster is inevitable.
