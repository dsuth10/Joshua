Geological events --- Exploring how people prepare for geological disaster

## Lesson concepts

#### Note: Teachers should be aware that students may have experienced some of the geological or extreme weather events learned about in this unit. Consider the needs of these students when discussing relevant events.

---

## Example learning sequence

Review effects of damage from natural disasters
- Review the damage that can be caused by natural geological events such as earthquakes and volcanoes.
- Explain that individual geological events can affect Earth's surface in different ways, and are only regarded as natural disasters if they affect humans.
Explore how people prepare for natural disasters
- Discuss how people around the world prepare for a disaster.
- Complete an activity about disaster preparation.
- Identify procedures and techniques that could have been used to minimise the loss of life and property in past events.
- Explore methods of preparing for a geological disaster, which could be used in the local area.

## Learning alerts

Be aware of:
Students thinking that nothing can be done to minimise the impact of a natural disaster.

---

## Suggested next steps for learning

Explain to students that, although little can be done to prevent a natural disaster, steps can be taken to reduce the impact it has on human life and property.
