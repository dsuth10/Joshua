## Lesson concepts

## Example learning sequence

Identify the requirements for a weather pattern to be considered a drought
- Review droughts and how they affect Earth's surface and people.
- Explore the requirements for an area to be considered in drought and define the two levels of drought:
- serious drought --- when a region experiences monthly rainfall that is between 5--10 per cent of the average for three or more months
- severe drought --- when a region experiences monthly rainfall that is below 5 per cent of the average for three or more months.
- Understand how rainfall data is collected and used when determining if an area is in drought.
Explain requirements for a region to be free of drought
- Discuss other methods for identifying if an area is experiencing a drought.
- Identify problems associated with predicting the 'end' of a period of drought.
- Explore methods used by the Bureau of Meteorology to determine when the end of a drought occurs.
Investigate rainfall data
- Interpret rainfall history sources to compare monthly rainfall experienced by different regions.

---

## Ideas for monitoring

Monitor students' ability to:
Describe periods when a region is in drought by using collected data.

---

## Learning alerts

Be aware of:
Students thinking that a drought can be lifted after a few short periods of rainfall.

---

## Suggested next steps for learning

Explain to students that the end point of a drought can only be determined after a long period of average rainfall has occurred.
