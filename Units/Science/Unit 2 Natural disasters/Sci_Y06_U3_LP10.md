## Lesson concepts

#### Note: Teachers should be aware that students may have experienced some of the geological or extreme weather events learned about in this unit. Consider the needs of these students when discussing relevant events.

---

## Example learning sequence

Review natural disasters
- Review the requirements for a weather event to be considered a natural disaster.
Explore how drought affects people
- Understand that significant weather events can be classed as either severe or extreme depending on the impact on Earth's surface and people.
- View a video to identify the concept of drought and its effect on the surface of Earth and the people living there.
- Explain the effects that drought can have on the surface of Earth and the people living there.
- Identify whether droughts can be considered natural disasters.
Explore drought mitigation techniques used in other cultures
- Identify techniques used by other cultures to reduce the impact of drought on their communities.
- Explore how these techniques could be used to reduce the effects of drought in Australia.

## Lesson objectives

Students will:
Understand how drought can affect Earth's surface.
Understand that methods for reducing the impact of drought can be found in a range of cultures.

---

## Evidence of learning

Can the student:
Describe the effects of drought on Earth's surface?
Describe how different techniques for drought mitigation from around the world can be used in Australia?

---

## Ideas for monitoring

Monitor students' ability to:
Describe the effects of drought on the surface of Earth.
Explain how different techniques for drought mitigation from around the world can be used in Australia.

---

## Learning alerts

Be aware of:
Students thinking that only the interior of Australia suffers drought conditions.

---

## Suggested next steps for learning

Explain that many areas of Earth experience below average rainfall and that they can also experience drought.

## Support
