Preparation for weather events --- Researching significant weather events in Australia

## Lesson concepts

#### Note: Teachers should be aware that students may have experienced some of the geological or extreme weather events learned about in this unit. Consider the needs of these students when discussing relevant events.

---

## Example learning sequence

Explore the behaviour of a recorded cyclone
- Explore information about a severe tropical cyclone in the Australia region.
- Track the path of a severe tropical cyclone.
- Record information about changes in wind speed as the cyclone moves from sea over land.
- Explain the rapid changes that would occur to the surface of Earth as a result of a severe tropical cyclone.
Research significant weather events which have affected Australia in the last decade
- Explore significant weather events, identifying changes to the conditions experienced on Earth's surface.
- Compare and contrast different significant weather events in relation to the impact they've had on people's lives in the area affected.

Students will:

---

## Evidence of learning

Can the student:
Identify key data about extreme weather events?
Describe the effects of extreme weather events?

---

## Ideas for monitoring

Monitor students' ability to:
Collect information about significant weather events.
Compare key data about significant weather events.

---

## Learning alerts

Be aware of:
Students thinking that only recent significant weather events are recorded and tracked.

---

## Suggested next steps for learning

Explain to students that significant weather events, including tropical cyclones, have been tracked for many years. The data gathered from these past events can be used for predicting future events.
