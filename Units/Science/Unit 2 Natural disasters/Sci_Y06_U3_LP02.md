## Lesson concepts

**Note:** Teachers should be aware that students may have experienced some of the geological or extreme weather events learned about in this unit. Consider the needs of these students when discussing relevant events.

---

## Example learning sequence

Introduce earthquakes
- Revise the definition of natural disaster.
- View a video about earthquakes.
- Explain why earthquakes can be classified as natural disasters.
- Define an earthquake as the sudden shaking or movement of the ground caused by movements in Earth's surface or crust.
- Recognise that the epicentre is the source of the earthquake.
- List evidence that may indicate an earthquake has taken place.
- Discuss the use of a seismograph as a device to collect data about an earthquake.
- Explain how knowledge of earthquake activity can be used by communities.
List earthquakes in Australia in the recent past
- Identify noteworthy earthquakes in Australia.
- Describe changes to Earth's surface that were caused by these earthquakes.
Explore the effects of earthquakes and landslides on Earth's surface
- View video footage of an earthquake in the Asia-Pacific region.
- Identify the effects of earthquakes in the video.
- Discuss tsunamis and landslides.
- Explain changes to Earth's surface due to earthquakes.
- Recognise that not all earthquakes are natural disasters.

## Learning alerts

Be aware of:
Students thinking that landslides are only caused by earthquakes.

## Suggested next steps for learning

Explain that landslides can be caused by other factors (e.g. heavy rainfall).
