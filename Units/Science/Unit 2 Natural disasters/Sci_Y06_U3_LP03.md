## Lesson concepts

Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander histories and cultures in this lesson
Australia's First Peoples' knowledge about sudden geological events (earthquakes) possibly thousands of years ago is retained.
Australia's First Peoples' knowledge of earthquakes in their local area allows scientists to understand that sudden geological events (earthquakes) have occurred before western methods were used to record the events and may indicate antiquity of the events. This information can assist scientists in their research into these geological events.

## Example learning sequence

Explore the Newcastle earthquake and its effects
- Review noteworthy earthquakes in Australia.
- Use a video to explore the effects of the historical 1989 Newcastle earthquake on Earth's surface, people and the economy.
- Discuss the small earthquakes or aftershocks that follow a large earthquake.
- Identify the types of information that could be gathered during and following an earthquake.
- Explain how this information could be used to benefit communities.
- Understand that science involves gathering data to understand how geological events occur and how their effects can be minimised to communities.
Discuss how Aboriginal Dreaming stories can be used to explain geological events in a region
- Understand that Aboriginal peoples have been recording significant historical geological events for thousands of years in their Dreaming stories.
- Discuss how traditional knowledge of earthquakes (in Dreaming stories) can be used to assist scientists in gathering evidence of past geological events.
- Explain how people moving or living in these areas can use the information in these stories.

## Lesson objectives

Students will:
Understand evidence can be collected about an earthquake from a range of sources.
Understand that Australia's First Peoples' knowledge of natural disasters has been used to assist scientific research.

---

## Evidence of learning

Can the student:
Identify evidence gathered from the earthquakes around the Newcastle area?
Describe how Australia's First People's knowledge of natural disasters assists in scientific research?

---

## Ideas for monitoring

Monitor students' ability to:
Describe the types of evidence collected about earthquakes in the Newcastle area.
Relate Australia's First Peoples' knowledge of natural disasters to scientific research.

---

## Learning alerts

Be aware of:
Students thinking that data from an earthquake can only be gathered during the actual event.

---

## Suggested next steps for learning

Explain to students that data is collected during and after an earthquake. Information about earthquakes that have occurred in the past can be used to predict the future effects of earthquakes.

---

## Ideas for differentiation

Allow students to work in pairs to complete the questions associated with the earthquake video.

---

## Extension

Ask students to identify other regions of Australia that have dreaming stories associated with geological events.
