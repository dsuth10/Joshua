Protractor Studio Quick Guide

Measuring Angles with Protractor

  • Move: Click and drag anywhere inside the transparent protractor body or the center origin dot.
  • Rotate: Drag the top circular rotation handle or scroll your mouse wheel while hovering over the protractor.
  • Resize: Drag the bottom corner handles ↙ ↘ or pinch with two fingers on touch screens.
  • Flip & Reset: Use the Flip button to turn the baseline upside down or Reset to restore position.
