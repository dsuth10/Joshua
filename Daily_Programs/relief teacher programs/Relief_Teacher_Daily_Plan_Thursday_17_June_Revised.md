## Middle Session: English (Information Reports)
Students will write a short information report on a topic of their choice. They should complete this on the dedicated page in the **English section of their OneNote** (pre-created for them).

### Suggested Flow & Checklist
1. **Intro & Plan (10 mins):** Remind them of report features (facts, subheadings). Students choose a topic and plan.
2. **Writing (40 mins):** Quiet, independent writing in OneNote.
3. **Editing (10 mins):** Students check their work against the list below before finishing.

* **Must-Haves:** [ ] Clear title [ ] Intro paragraph [ ] Grouped facts with subheadings [ ] Topic-specific words [ ] Conclusion [ ] Checked spelling & capitals.
* **Optional Extras:** Labelled diagrams, "fun facts" box, glossary, timeline, or map.
