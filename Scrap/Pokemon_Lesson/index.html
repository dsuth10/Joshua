EXPANDED NOUN GROUPS

Writers expand noun groups to express precise details about a creature's appearance or traits without having to write multiple short, repetitive sentences.

ADJECTIVES: Placed BEFORE the noun to detail colour, size, or temperament.

QUALIFIERS: Prepositional phrases starting with with, on, from, or in placed AFTER the noun.
