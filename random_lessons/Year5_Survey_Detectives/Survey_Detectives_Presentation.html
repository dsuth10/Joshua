MODEL THE FINAL ANSWER

A strong conclusion has four jobs

CLAIM

Our generated data does not support the idea that sport-enjoyers tend to dislike Maths.

EVIDENCE

Of the 12 who enjoyed sport, 8 (67%) also enjoyed Maths and 4 (33%) did not.

INTERPRET

In this sample, enjoying both was more common than enjoying sport but not Maths.

LIMIT

This was a synthetic sample of only 19 responses. It cannot prove what all Year 5 students think—or that one preference causes the other.
