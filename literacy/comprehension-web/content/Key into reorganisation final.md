With these questions you are looking for KEY words and phrases that tell you -

the items in a group or the steps of a sequence. It may involve a referent.

A mathematical operation is then used.

 

MODEL

 

PARAGRAPH:

Early on Tuesday morning the robot was sent down onto the ocean floor. Its task was to take a photo of the reef every hour for our magazine. We returned late the following Thursday to bring it back up.

 

QUESTION:

How many days was the robot on the ocean floor?

 

ANSWER:

Three.

 

KEY WORDS:

Early / Tuesday / robot / sent down/ late / following Thursday / back up

 

CALCULATION:

1 (Tuesday)+ 1 (Wednesday)+ 1 (Thursday)= 3 days

NOTE: The type of operation (addition, subtraction etc.) is determined by the way the question is worded (e.g. How much? How long?)

MODEL

 

PARAGRAPH:

\"Do you know where my book is?\" asked John.

\"On the table,\" Sue replied.

\"Thank you.\"

 

QUESTION:

Who said thank you?

 

ANSWER:

John.

 

KEY WORDS:

asked John (line one) Sue replied (line two) Thank you (line 3 - John)

(There are only two speakers therefore the speaker after Sue is John.)

 

NOTE:

The common way to show two or more people speaking is by using:

1\. speech marks

2\. a name (noun or pronoun) at least once - to show who the speaker is.

3\. a new line each time the speaker changes

\"Did you see the walker with two legs sitting on a bicycle beside the old factory?\"

DRAGSTERS

Dragsters are made for fast racing. In fact they are the fastest racing cars in the world. They can cover 400 metres down a special track called a drag strip in only five seconds. And that is about as long as a drag race lasts!

ITEM 8

RATS

There are two main groups of rats - black rats and brown rats. They belong to the family of animals called rodents. Other animals in this family are mice, beavers, porcupines and squirrels.

 

The black rat can grow to a length of about 40 centimetres, and about half that length is its tail. A rat\'s tail has no hair or fur on it. Brown rats are bigger and have shorter ears and tails. They have always been very good at finding their way onto ships. Because of this they can be found all over the world.

 

Like the other members of their family, rats have two sharp pairs of front teeth used for gnawing. Rats will gnaw anything -they have been known to gnaw through lead piping.

 

Rats are pests. They damage food supplies, often in poorer countries where food is hard to get. They can also damage buildings by gnawing through wiring, walls and ceilings. They carry diseases that can be spread to humans. Perhaps the best known of these is plague. Millions of people died of it before anyone knew what was causing it.

 

Even so, they have become a popular pet, with owners of all ages who are totally devoted to them.

b\. About what length can a black rat\'s tail grow to? GC

ITEM 9

KNOTS

Knots are used by different people for many reasons. Sailors and fishermen use them in their daily activities. When climbers use knots, they must be properly tied for safety.

 

A good knot should be strong when it is in use. It also should be easy to untie and not get tangled. An exploding knot is one that can be untied with one pull of the rope with no tangles. Knots have some interesting names.

 

The Butterfly Dropper, Blood Knot and King Sling are all knots used by fishermen. They use them when attaching hooks and tying on bait. They are also important to them when repairing broken fishing lines.

 

Sailors use a Sheet Bend to tie two ropes together. This knot gets its name from the rope attached to the corner of the sail. The rope is called the sheet. A Sheep Shank is used for shortening a rope. It might just be too long or it might be worn in places.

 

A Reef Knot is a common knot that is used for tying two ends together. It is also the one we use to tie our shoelaces. A good way to remember it is to say \'right over left - left over right\' as you are holding the ends of the laces in your hands. If you get it wrong, it is called a \'Granny Knot\' and won\'t stay done up.

 

a\. What sort of people use knots? G

b\. What makes a good knot? GR

c\. What do fishermen use knots for? GR

d\. Why would a rope need to be shortened? JR

e\. Which knot gone wrong is called a \'Granny Knot\'? JR

JUPITER

 

Our solar system has eight planets. The largest of these is Jupiter, named by the ancient Romans. They gave it the same name as their King of the Gods. It is large and very bright. This makes it quite easy to see in the night sky.

 

The first astronomers wrote about it. They thought it must be hot like the sun and other stars. They thought it was shining by its own light. We now know that its light comes from the sun and it is being reflected from the cloudy surface of Jupiter. The cloud layer that surrounds Jupiter is very thick and made up of poisonous gases. It is known as a cold planet. The rocky centre of the planet sends out heat, but the clouds are freezing cold.

 

Jupiter spins very quickly. One day on this planet lasts only 9 hours and 55 minutes.

 

Jupiter has many moons. We still don\'t know exactly how many, as more are being discovered all the time. At the moment we know of thirty-nine moons circling Jupiter. Many of them are small. The smallest is only two kilometres across. The ones closest to Jupiter are rocky, the ones further away are icy.

 

Even though we have learned a lot about Jupiter from information sent back from spacecraft, there is still much that is unknown.

Walton District Health wishes to announce that for the next four months they are offering at risk citizens free immunisation against influenza.

Several cases of people being hospitalised in the districts of Glink and Bailey have been reported by the National Department of Health.
