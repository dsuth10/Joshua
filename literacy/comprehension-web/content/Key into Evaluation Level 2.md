Popcorn
Why does popcorn pop?  How does a little, hard kernel of corn become a soft, dry, fluffy snack.
Each kernel of corn contains a small amount of water stored inside a circle of soft starch.  The soft starch is surrounded by the kernel’s hard outer shell.  When the kernel is heated, the water turns to steam and builds up pressure, pushing against the hard shell.
Eventually, the shell gives way, and explodes.  When this happens, the soft starch inflates to up to 40 times its original size and bursts, turning the kernel inside out and releasing the steam.

Caving
Many English words have Greek origins.  The old Greek word spelaion means caves.  A spelean spider is a cave dwelling spider.  People who are interested in exploring and studying caves are known as speleologists  (spee-lee-ol-o-jists).

They wear warm clothing and their boots must be strong with a good tread.

Planet Names
Apart from Earth, there are seven known planets in our solar system.  They are named after ancient Roman or Greek gods, because it was believed that the bright objects in the night sky were gods.  Until recently, Pluto was the furthest known planet.  It was named after the ancient Roman god of the underworld.  In 1914 it was predicted to exist but it wasn’t proven for another sixteen years.  In 2006 Pluto was downgraded from being a true planet to a dwarf planet, or plutoid as dwarf planets are now called.
Neptune is named after the Roman sea god.  Although its existence was proved in 1846, it was another year before it was actually seen through a telescope.
Uranus is named after the Greek sky god.  It was originally called Georgium Sidus (meaning Star of George).  This was in honour of King George the Third of England (1738-1820).
Saturn was named after the Roman god of farming.  Jupiter was named after the Roman king of the gods, Mars after their god of war and Venus after their goddess of love and beauty.  Closest to the sun is Mercury, named after the Roman god Mercury, a winged messenger.
a.   Was Neptune named by the ancient Romans?  Why do you say that?
b.  Which planet do you think was last to be named?  Why do you say that?
