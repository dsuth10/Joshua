The flight to India was about to leave. Paul and Nicola were finally off on their overseas holiday. They thought they had everything they needed to get on the plane. They had their passports. They had the boarding passes. They waved their last good-byes to the family and disappeared through the doors. The family were turning to go when to their surprise, the couple reappeared. They were laughing, but looking a bit sheepish. They confessed that they couldn't get on the plane because they hadn't paid the departure tax. They had to come back to do it at the counter beside the bank where they had earlier changed some of their money into Indian currency. Once they had the tax sticker on their boarding passes, they could get on the plane.

Five minutes later, they waved good-bye to the family and finally disappeared through the doors.

"Who's volunteering?" Leo asked.

Samantha pushed the door open for him and he put his backpack inside, then went back outside. The others crowded in, relieved to be in the shelter. They took off wet boots and socks, stretching ankles and toes as they did so.

When the fire was crackling in the grate, they hung the socks on a line that was stretched across the width of the fireplace. They wouldn't take long to dry there.

Backpacks were emptied and it wasn't long before Noel had a large pot of stew cooking, suspended above the fire by a steel hook.

Later, relaxing and warming themselves in front of the fire they planned tomorrow's stage of the walk.
