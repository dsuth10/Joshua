# Lesson Plan: 24-Hour Time Problems & Timetables

**Year Level:** Year 5  
**Subject:** Mathematics  
**Strand:** Measurement  
**Topic:** 24-Hour Time & Reading Timetables  
**Curriculum Alignment:** Australian Curriculum v9 (AC9M5M03 - *"Solve problems involving time duration, including using 24-hour time and timetables"*)  
**Duration:** 60 Minutes  

---

## 🧠 Pedagogical Contemplation

### 1. Cognitive Goal
The core cognitive skill students are practicing is **analytical interpretation and multi-step deduction**. Reading a timetable is not just visual retrieval; it requires students to map a service chronologically, coordinate arrival and departure points, calculate differences across different units of time (hours and minutes), and synthesise these offsets when external factors (like delays) are introduced.

### 2. Interactive Alignment
We utilise a **Matching Pairs (12-hour to 24-hour)** interaction. This is the optimal fit for the warm-up/review phase because it forces students to actively convert and connect equivalent representations of time. Instead of passive listening, students manipulate the cards, reinforcing the mathematical rule that for p.m. times, we add 12 to the hours, while a.m. times remain identical (except for midnight). 

### 3. Surfacing Student Thinking
By using the **Physical-Digital Whiteboard Protocol** for the guided practice questions, the teacher can instantly surface the thinking of the entire class. Having students show their calculations on mini-whiteboards *before* clicking "Show Correct Answer" on the interactive screen ensures that errors in time subtraction (a common misconception being treating time subtraction as base-10 subtraction, e.g., $13:25 - 12:50 = 75$ minutes) are caught and addressed immediately.

### 4. Pedagogical vs. Engagement Goal
*   **Pedagogical Goal:** Accurately read a column-based train timetable and calculate elapsed time intervals.
*   **Engagement Goal:** Tapping colourful matching cards, watching them spin, and interacting with high-contrast, premium dark/light themed slides to keep cognitive energy high.

---

## 🎯 Learning Intentions & Success Criteria

### Learning Intention
We are learning to interpret 24-hour timetables and solve real-world duration problems so that we can confidently plan and manage travel schedules.

### Success Criteria
*   I can identify and convert between 12-hour (a.m./p.m.) and 24-hour time notations.
*   I can explain how midnight (`00:00`) and noon (`12:00`) are represented in 24-hour time.
*   I can read a train or bus timetable by tracking down columns for services and across rows for stops.
*   I can calculate travel durations by counting forward to the next hour and adding minutes.
*   I can calculate new arrival times when services are delayed.

## ⏱️ 60-Minute Lesson Sequence

### 1. Introduction & Warm-up (10 Minutes)
*   **The Hook:** Show **Slide 1** and **Slide 2**. Introduce the concept of a "Time Traveler". Discuss why critical systems (airports, trains, hospitals, defense forces) use the 24-hour clock. (Avoids confusion between 8:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m.!).
*   **Interactive Review (Slide 3):** Play the **Matching Pairs** game on the smartboard. Call on students to pair the 12-hour and 24-hour departure times from our Saturday Train Timetable. 
    *   *Examples:* `12:50 p.m.` ↔ `12:50`, `1:20 p.m.` ↔ `13:20`, `2:20 p.m.` ↔ `14:20`, `2:56 p.m.` ↔ `14:56`.

### 2. Guided Practice: Reconstructing the Train Timetable (20 Minutes)
*   **The Timetable Structure (Slide 4):** Review the Saturday Train Timetable from South Bank to the Domestic Airport. Explain that columns represent individual train runs (services 1, 2, 3, 4) and rows represent stations (South Bank, Central, International Airport, Domestic Airport).
*   **Modelling Calculations (Slide 5 - "I Do"):** Model how to calculate the duration of Service 1 (South Bank 12:50 to Domestic Airport 13:25).
    *   *Step 1:* Jump to the next hour: `12:50` to `13:00` = **10 minutes**.
    *   *Step 2:* Add the remaining minutes: `13:00` to `13:25` = **25 minutes**.
    *   *Step 3:* Add them together: $10 + 25 = 35$ minutes.
*   **Co-construction (Slide 6 - "We Do"):** Have students work in pairs on their mini-whiteboards to calculate the durations of Service 2, 3, and 4.
    *   *Service 2 (13:20 to 13:56):* $13:20 \rightarrow 13:56 = 36$ minutes.
    *   *Service 3 (13:50 to 14:25):* $13:50 \rightarrow 14:00$ (10 mins) $+ 14:00 \rightarrow 14:25$ (25 mins) = **35 minutes**.
    *   *Service 4 (14:20 to 14:56):* $14:20 \rightarrow 14:56 = 36$ minutes.
*   Conduct a whiteboard reveal to check accuracy before writing these values in the handout.

### 3. Deep Dive Timetable Problems (15 Minutes)
*   **Timetable Interpretation (Slide 7):** Work through problems **b**, **c**, and **d** from the textbook:
    *   *Question b:* How long does the 12:50 train take to travel from Central to Domestic Airport? (Central depart 13:01, Domestic arrive 13:25. $13:01 \rightarrow 13:25 = 24$ minutes).
    *   *Question c:* How much longer does the 13:20 train take from South Bank to Domestic Airport (36 mins) than from South Bank to International Airport (South Bank 13:20 to International 13:53 = 33 mins)? (Calculation: $36 - 33 = 3$ minutes).
    *   *Question d:* When is the latest train you could catch from South Bank to get to the International Airport before 2 p.m. (`14:00`)? 
        *   Review Service 2: South Bank 13:20, arrives International Airport at 13:53. (Arrives before 2 p.m. - YES).
        *   Review Service 3: South Bank 13:50, arrives International Airport at 14:22. (Arrives after 2 p.m. - NO).
        *   *Conclusion:* The latest train is the **13:20 service**.
*   **Delays & Service Frequency (Slide 8):** Solve textbook problems **e** and **f**:
    *   *Question e:* The 14:20 train from South Bank was delayed at Central for 12 minutes. At what time is the train likely to arrive at Domestic Airport? 
        *   Standard arrival time: `14:56`.
        *   Add delay: $14:56 + 12\text{ minutes} = 15:08$.
    *   *Question f:* How often does a train travel from South Bank to Domestic Airport?
        *   Compare departure times: `12:50`, `13:20`, `13:50`, `14:20`.
        *   *Interval:* Every **30 minutes**.

### 4. Independent Application: The Monday Bus Timetable (10 Minutes)
*   **Transition to Bus Timetable (Slide 9):** Present the Monday Bus Timetable from Flinders St to Camberwell. Note that the bus leaves Flinders St every 10 minutes.
*   **Student Worksheet Work:** Students work independently on Part 2 of their handout:
    *   *Question a:* Travel time from Flinders St (12:52) to Camberwell (13:06)? (Calculation: $12:52 \rightarrow 13:06 = 14$ minutes).
    *   *Question b:* Levi catches the bus from Flinders St at 12:52. At what time would he reach Glenferrie? (Timetable shows Glenferrie arrival is **13:02**).
    *   *Question c:* Joshua misses the 12:52 bus and takes the next one instead. At what time would he arrive at the Auburn bus stop?
        *   Next bus leaves Flinders St 10 minutes later: $12:52 + 10 = 13:02$.
        *   Travel time from Flinders St to Auburn: $12:52 \rightarrow 13:04 = 12$ minutes.
        *   Arrival time: $13:02 + 12\text{ minutes} = 13:14$.

### 5. Consolidation & Exit Ticket (5 Minutes)
*   **Exit Ticket (Slide 10):** Students solve textbook problem **2d** on a slip of paper (or mini-whiteboard) to exit:
    *   *Question d:* At what time would the 13:12 bus expect to arrive at Camberwell bus stop?
        *   Flinders St base departure: `12:52` (arrives Camberwell `13:06` - duration is 14 minutes).
        *   Our bus departure: `13:12` (leaves 20 minutes after 12:52).
        *   *Calculation:* $13:12 + 14\text{ minutes} = 13:26$.
        *   *Alternative Calculation:* $13:06 + 20\text{ minutes} = 13:26$.

## 🪜 Differentiation Strategies

### Support (Scaffolded Pathway)
*   Provide a printed **"24-Hour Helper" number track** showing `12:00` aligned to `12:00 p.m.`, `13:00` to `1:00 p.m.`, etc.
*   Focus on calculating time durations that do not cross the hour mark (e.g., `12:52` to `12:58` is 6 minutes) before introducing hour-crossing calculations.

### Extension (Lucas Pathway Challenge)
*   **The Multi-Leg Queensland Commuter:** Plan a journey from Flinders St bus stop to the Domestic Airport. 
    *   *Scenario:* Catch a bus from Flinders St to connect with the train at Central. If the bus journey takes 15 minutes and you need a 6-minute transfer buffer, which bus must you catch to board the `13:20` train from Central? Calculate the total travel and waiting duration.
