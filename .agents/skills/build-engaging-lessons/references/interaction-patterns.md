# Interaction patterns

Choose the smallest interaction that exposes the intended thinking.

## Retrieve and reveal

- Require a location, observation, or explanation before revealing.
- Include a plausible decoy when it exposes a useful misconception.
- Do not use reveal cards only to pace teacher exposition.

## Sort and classify

- Make at least one category boundary genuinely difficult.
- Ask students to state the classification rule.
- Give retry feedback that names the distinction without placing every answer.
- Support click/touch placement as well as dragging.

## Compare and choose

- Define audience, purpose, or constraints.
- Publish two to four criteria.
- Require “X is more effective because…” or equivalent reasoning.
- Allow multiple choices when justification is legitimate.

## Sequence and causality

- Ask students to predict before revealing.
- Use because, therefore, and which means.
- Distinguish chronological order from cause-and-effect.

## Source credibility check

Evaluate named author/organisation, evidence and method, currency, relevance to the exact claim, purpose/perspective, and corroboration. Do not teach “official-looking” as authority.

## Annotated model

- Show or perform the whole model first.
- Reveal one decision at a time.
- Ask students to name its job before showing the label.
- Connect every annotation to the independent task.

## Build studio

- Chunk the product into meaningful decisions.
- Offer optional scaffolds rather than mandatory formulaic text.
- Preserve work locally when technically safe.
- Display a useful constraint such as time, length, required evidence, or success checks.

## Rehearsal and feedback

- Give speaker/creator and listener/reviewer roles.
- Provide two precise feedback stems.
- Require immediate revision of one element.
- Make timers easy to start and reset.

## Exit evidence

Sample the central learning, not participation or enjoyment. Good forms include one worked example plus reasoning, claim plus evidence, revision plus explanation, or confidence plus a specific help need.
