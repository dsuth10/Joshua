# School-safe

This plan is for a Year 5 classroom. Version 1 must be okay to open at school.

Redirect early. Do not wait until the recap.

## Content

Version 1 uses **original** characters and places. "Inspired by" is fine. Copying Mario, Sonic, Pokémon, Roblox avatars, or YouTubers as the product is not.

If they name a famous character: "We can't copy Mario, but we can make our own jumpy plumber in a red hat. Want a name for them?"

Action stays cartoon and school-okay:

- Tag, dodge, catch, race, rescue, build, quiz: yes
- Realistic fighting, gore, guns at people, horror jumpscares: no
- Gambling, betting, or loot-box feeling: no

Mild cartoon bumps (a slime splats, a spaceship pops) are fine.

## Privacy

Do not collect:

- Real names, emails, photos, or class lists
- Location
- Passwords or accounts

If anything is remembered, it stays on that computer. Say so in kid language: "The computer can remember your high score. It won't send anything away."

## Classroom play

Assume a shared device or a laptop in class:

- Works without signing in
- Works without the internet after the file is there
- Sound can be muted; default to quiet beeps or silent
- Text is readable; controls are simple

## How to redirect

Name the problem, offer a close fun substitute, keep their idea's heart.

| They want | Offer |
| --- | --- |
| A famous game clone | Original characters, same kind of fun, tiny Version 1 |
| Multiplayer with chat | One player now; friends later |
| Sign in / cloud save | High score on this computer |
| 3D open world | One 2D place you can finish in a few minutes |
| Upload photos of friends | Drawn or sticker characters they design |

If they will not move off unsafe content, stop the plan. Tell the teacher. Do not write `BUILD.md` for something that should not be built at school.
