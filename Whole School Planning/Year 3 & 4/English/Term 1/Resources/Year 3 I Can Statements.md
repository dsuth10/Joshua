# Year 3 I Can Statements – Assessment Task

## READING AND VIEWING – I CAN…

•  explain the purpose of a text (why it was written).  

•  identify the audience (who the text is written for).

  
•  find literal meaning in a text (information directly stated). 

 
•  explain inferred meaning (ideas suggested but not directly stated).  

•  describe how a story develops through characters, settings and events.  

•  explain how characters or events help move the story forward.  

•  read aloud fluently using phonic, morphemic and grammatical knowledge.  
•  read multisyllabic words with more complex letter patterns.  

•  self-correct when something doesn’t sound right.  

## SPEAKING AND LISTENING – I CAN…

•  interact respectfully with others when sharing ideas.  

•  listen carefully during discussions.  

• n create and share a spoken or multimodal story.  

•  describe characters, settings or events clearly when speaking.  

## WRITING AND CREATING – I CAN…

•  create a story for a clear purpose and audience.  

•  relate ideas from a learnt topic, a topic of interest or a studied text.  

•  organise my ideas into paragraphs.  

•  sequence ideas so that my writing flows logically.  

•  use compound sentences to extend meaning.  

•  use topic-specific vocabulary in my writing.  

•  include literary devices to make my writing engaging.  

•  include visual features when creating a multimodal text. 

 
•  read my writing aloud to check it makes sense.  

•  edit to improve spelling, punctuation and sentence structure.
