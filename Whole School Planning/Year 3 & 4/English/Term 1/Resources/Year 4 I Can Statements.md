# Year 4 I Can Statements – Assessment Task 1.1 Responding to Literature

## READING AND VIEWING – I CAN…

•  explain the purpose of an imaginative text (to inform, influence or engage).  

•  identify how the author engages the audience, using evidence from the text.  

•  describe how the author shows the context (time, place, historical or cultural details).  

•  explain how characters, settings and events develop the main ideas.  

•  describe how authors build plot tension to interest the audience.  

•  read aloud fluently and accurately.  

•  use strategies like re-reading, pausing, questioning and self-correcting when I get stuck.  

•  answer questions that show what I understand from the text (literal and inferred meaning).  

SPEAKING AND LISTENING – I CAN…

•  take turns and listen respectfully to others.  

•  clearly share my adaptation ideas during discussions.  

•  respond to others’ ideas and build on what they say.  

•  tell a story that adapts a character, setting or event from a familiar narrative.  

• describe the sequence of events clearly when telling my story.  

• speak clearly using expression and vocabulary that suits my story.  

## WRITING AND CREATING – I CAN…

• plan an imaginative adaptation by choosing a character, setting or event to adapt.  

• identify exactly where my adaptation fits in the original narrative.  

•  keep the context of the original story the same (e.g., no modern items in historical settings).  

•  write for a clear purpose and audience.  

• organise my ideas using paragraphs that link together.  

•  use complex sentences to make my writing more interesting.  

•  use precise vocabulary and topic-specific words from the original text.  

•  use direct and indirect speech with correct punctuation.  

•  use literary devices (such as similes or personification) to interest the reader.  

•  build tension in my writing to make the story engaging.  

•  revise and edit my writing to improve meaning, vocabulary and punctuation.  

•  publish my work and include illustrations if I choose.
