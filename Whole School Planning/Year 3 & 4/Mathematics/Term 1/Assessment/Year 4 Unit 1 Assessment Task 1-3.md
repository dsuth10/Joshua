## Implementation advice

Adapt this assessment task to suit the diverse needs of the students, school and local context.

When adapting the assessment, maintain alignment to the targeted achievement standard aspects and aligned curriculum elements and consider ways to make the assessment meaningful and accessible for students. 

Each part of the summative assessment may be conducted as a series of smaller tasks to be used to gather evidence of student learning against the marking guide.

Consider your cohort and change the topic of investigation if needed, for example: planning for a school fundraiser. This statistical investigation can be changed to generate discrete numerical data, for example: the number of badges to order for a fundraiser.

In order to meet the targeted achievement standard aspect, ‘Use surveys and digital tools to generate categorical or discrete numerical data in statistical investigations and communicate their findings in context’, identify opportunities for students to use digital tools when they generate data. For example:

the survey can be conducted using email, polling tools or an online survey creator

the data can be recorded using a spreadsheet, online survey creator or word-processing program.

At the start of the assessment, have a class discussion on the sandwich fillings that could be included as categories in the survey question. Have each student choose four sandwich fillings to use as categories and record in Part A Question 1.

**Categorical data** involves values or observations that can be sorted into categories, for example: eye colour, choices of sandwich fillings.

## Part A: Conduct a statistical investigation for categorical data

## UNDERSTAND

There is going to be a morning tea for a celebration day. Your teacher wants to know which sandwich fillings to put on the sandwiches.

Collect data to find out the three most popular sandwich fillings.

## PLAN

Choose four fillings to create a survey question.

Plan how you will use digital tools to conduct the survey with at least 20 people and collect the data. 

## DO

Conduct the survey using your question and digital tools.

Make a many-to-one picture graph to represent the survey data on **Sheet — Part A graphs**. Remember to include a title and labels and give your graph a **key**.

Make a column graph to represent the survey data on **Sheet — Part A graphs**. Remember to include a **title** and **labels**.

Look at your many-to-one picture graph.

	What was the most popular sandwich filling?

	What was the least popular sandwich filling?

	List the sandwich fillings in order from most popular to least popular.

	When you made your many-to-one picture graph, what did you need to include?

	How is a picture graph useful for answering questions about the sandwich filling data?

Look at your column graph.

- 	How many people chose the most popular sandwich filling?

- 	How many people chose the least popular sandwich filling?

- 	How many more people chose the **most popular** sandwich filling compared to the **least popular** sandwich filling?

- 	When you made your column graph, what did you need to include?

- 	How is a column graph useful for answering questions about the sandwich filling data?

## CONSIDER

Look at your many-to-one picture graph and column graph.

- 	Which graph is best for showing the most popular sandwich filling? Why do you think this?

- If you could only show your teacher one graph to help them decide on the three most popular sandwich fillings, which graph is more effective to show the data? Why do you think this?

## COMMUNICATE

The three most popular sandwich fillings are _________, _________ and ________.

My data supports this because …

## Part B: Conduct a statistical investigation for discrete numerical data

## UNDERSTAND

You have collected data about the sandwich fillings to make. Now collect data about how many sandwich triangles to make.

## PLAN

You are going to survey at least 20 people to answer the question: ‘How many sandwich triangles should be made for each person: 3, 4, 5 or 6?’.

Plan how you will use digital tools to conduct the survey of at least 20 people and collect the data.

## DO

- Conduct a survey using the question and digital tools.

Make a column graph to represent the survey data on **Sheet ****—**** Column graph**. Remember to include a **title** and **labels**.

Look at your column graph.

- 	What was the most popular number of sandwich triangles?

- 	What was the least popular number of sandwich triangles?

- What does the data tell you? (Hint: Think about the shape of distribution and variation in data.)

## CONSIDER and COMMUNICATE

My advice is that ____ sandwich triangles should made for each person.

My data supports this because …

Answers will vary, for example: I would show the teacher the column graph because it quickly shows that cheese, ham and tuna are the three most popular sandwich fillings out of 25 students. The column graph shows the number of responses for each category quickly. 9 students chose cheese and 8 chose ham, so the teacher should make similar quantities of sandwiches with these fillings. The teacher should make less tuna sandwiches because only five people chose this filling.
