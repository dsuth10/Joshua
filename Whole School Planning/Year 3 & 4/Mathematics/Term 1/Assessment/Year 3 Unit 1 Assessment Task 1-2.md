## Part A: Interpret a map

Look at Sheet — Friendly Zoo map.

Circle where you would go to visit the monkeys.

Circle the toilet buildings. How many toilet buildings are there?

Can you visit giraffes at this zoo? How do you know?

Can you visit elephants at this zoo? How do you know? 

What building is located between the rubbish bin and the monkeys?

What animal is located beside the first aid building and next to the crocodiles?

## Part B: Create a map

You will create a map of a room from a top view perspective. On your map you may include: furniture, windows, doors, rugs, beanbags and other objects.

- Draw the room and use shapes to represent the objects in the room.

Label the objects on your map.

Use stars to show three different places on your map, for example: bookshelf, art cupboard, desks.

Create directions that someone could follow to get to those three different places in the room if they start at the doorway.

## Part A: Interpret a map

- Look at Sheet — Friendly Zoo map.

- Circle where you would go to visit the monkeys.

See map.

Circle the toilet buildings. How many toilet buildings are there?

See map. Three toilet buildings.

Can you visit giraffes at this zoo? How do you know? 

Yes, because there is a giraffe icon. The giraffes are located opposite the koalas.

Can you visit elephants at this zoo? How do you know?

No, because there is no elephant icon on the map.

What building is located **between** the rubbish bin and the monkeys?

The toilet building.

What animal is located beside the first aid building and next to the crocodiles?

The snakes.

## Part B: Create a map

You will create a map of a room from a top view perspective. On your map you may include: furniture, windows, doors, rugs, beanbags and other objects.

- Draw the room and use shapes to represent the objects in the room.

Label the objects on your map.

Use stars to show three different places on your map, for example: bookshelf, art cupboard, desks.

Create directions that someone could follow to get to those three different places in the room if they start at the doorway.

Answers will vary, for example:

Start at the doorway and walk forward across the rug until you reach the bookshelf.

Go past the beanbags, around the art table and turn right until you reach the art cupboard.

Walk towards the whiteboard, turn left and walk forward to the desks.
