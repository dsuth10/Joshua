Solve the following problems and show your working.

	Jack practises piano every week for 2 hours 30 minutes on Tuesday and 1 hour 45 minutes on Thursday. How long does Jack practise piano for every week? For example: Jack practises piano every week for 4 hours and 15 minutes.

	A bus leaves the bus stop at 11.25 am and arrives at the shopping centre at 2.45 pm. How long does the trip take? For example: The trip takes 3 hours and 20 minutes.

	Akot swam 200 metres in 5 minutes and 39 seconds. Peter swam 200 metres in 420 seconds. Who was the fastest? For example:

5 minutes × 60 = 300 + 39 = 339 seconds

Akot swam the fastest.

Gabby leaves her house at 9.20 am and drives to the beach. She arrives at the beach 65 minutes later. On the clock, draw the time Gabby arrived at the beach. For example:

9:20 am + 60 min = 10:20 am + 5 minutes = 10:25 am.
