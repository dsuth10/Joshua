## Implementation advice

Adapt this assessment task to suit the diverse needs of the students, school and local context.

When adapting the assessment, maintain alignment to the targeted achievement standard aspects and aligned curriculum elements and consider ways to make the assessment meaningful and accessible for students.

Answer spaces have not been included to provide flexibility in how and where students will respond.

Each part of the summative assessment may be conducted as a series of smaller tasks to be used to gather evidence of student learning against the marking guide.

In Question 4, an example activity is given, making an origami whale. If choosing an alternative activity, ensure the activity takes longer than a minute so that it can be measured by reading the start and finish times to the nearest minute on a clock.

In Question 4, students measure the length of the activity by recording the start time and finish time on the clock faces in the table to the nearest minute and then calculate the duration of the activity. Students need access to one type of clock (analog or digital) when completing this question. They then apply their knowledge of representing the times they recorded on the other type of clock.
