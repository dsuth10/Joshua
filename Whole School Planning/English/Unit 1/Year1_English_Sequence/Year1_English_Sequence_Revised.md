# Year 1 English Teaching Sequence: Assessment-Led (10 Weeks) - Revised Book Order

**Focus:** Writing simple sentences to retell a story, including who/what, action, and circumstances.
**Differentiation:** Standard (Support / Core / Extend)

## Curriculum Alignment (Australian Curriculum v9)

- **AC9E1LA06**: understand that a simple sentence consists of a single independent clause representing a single event or idea
- **AC9E1LA07**: understand that words can represent people, places and things (nouns), happenings (verbs), and details such as when, where and how (circumstances)
- **AC9E1LY06**: create and re-read to edit short written texts to recount a real or imagined event, using grammatically correct simple sentences, some topic-specific vocabulary, and sentence boundary punctuation
- **AC9E1LE03**: discuss plot, character and setting, which are features of stories
- **AC9E1LE05**: orally retell or adapt a familiar story using plot and characters...
- **AC9E1LA10**: understand that written language uses punctuation such as full stops... and capital letters
