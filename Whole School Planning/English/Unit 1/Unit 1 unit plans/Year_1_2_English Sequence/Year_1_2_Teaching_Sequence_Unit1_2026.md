# Year 1 / Year 2 English — Unit 1: Examining and Creating Imaginative Literary Texts

**Term 1, 2026 | 10 Weeks | 40 Lessons**
**Assessment Task 1.1 — Responding to Literature**

---

## Unit Overview

Students in Year 1 and Year 2 engage with a rich collection of picture books and literary texts — including a First Nations oral narrative — to explore how authors and illustrators create characters, settings and events. Students move from reading and responding to literature, through to adapting and creating their own imaginative texts.

**Year 1 Assessment Purpose:** To retell and adapt a familiar story using oral language and create a written and/or multimodal recount.
**Year 2 Assessment Purpose:** To recount and share ideas using oral language and adapt an idea in a written and/or multimodal story.
