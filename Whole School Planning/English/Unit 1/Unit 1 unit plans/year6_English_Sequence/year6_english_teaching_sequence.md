# Year 6 English – Teaching Sequence
## Unit 1: Examining and Creating Imaginative Literary Texts
### Core Text: *The Burnt Stick* — Anthony Hill (illustrated by Mark Sofilas)
**Year Level:** Year 6 | **Duration:** Term 1, Weeks 1–10 (40 lessons) | **School:** Tarampa SS Cluster

---

## Unit Overview

Students engage with *The Burnt Stick*, a powerful novella exploring the history of the Stolen Generations in Australia. Through close reading and aesthetic response, students examine how Anthony Hill uses character, setting, narrative perspective, and language to reflect the social, cultural, and historical contexts of colonial Australia. Students compare the novella with a second narrative text, then adapt the original text to produce a cohesive narrative of their own, maintaining the author's original style, purpose, and audience.

**Assessment Task 1.1 (Summative):** Students read, view, comprehend, and compare imaginative narrative texts. They create a written and/or multimodal imaginative narrative adaptation.

## Year 6 Achievement Standard — Targeted Aspects

**Reading and Viewing (Assessed):** Students read, view and comprehend different texts created to inform, influence and/or engage audiences. They identify similarities and differences in how ideas are presented and developed including through characters, settings and/or events, and how texts reflect contexts.

**Writing and Creating (Assessed):** Students create written and/or multimodal texts, including literary texts, for particular purposes and audiences, developing, explaining and elaborating on relevant ideas from topics or texts. They use text structures and vary paragraphs to organise, develop and link ideas. They use and vary language features including sentence structures, topic-specific vocabulary and literary devices, and/or multimodal features.
