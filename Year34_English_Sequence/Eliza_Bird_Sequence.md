# English Teaching Sequence: Eliza Bird: Child Convict (Year 3/4)

**Unit Title:** Examining and creating imaginative literary texts  
**Core Text:** *Eliza Bird: Child Convict* by Kerri Lane, Illustrated by Rachel Tonkin  
**Duration:** 10 Weeks (40 Lessons)  
**Curriculum Focus:** Australian Curriculum v9 (Years 3 & 4)  
**Differentiation:** Standard (Support, Core, Extend)

## 📅 10-Week Teaching Sequence

| Week | Sequence | Lesson | Learning Intention | Teaching and Learning Sequence | Reading | Differentiation | Resources |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **1** | **1** | Understand the historical context of 19th-century Australia. | **Introduction:** Discuss "convicts" and "transportation". Use a map to show the journey from Britain to Australia. <br>**Explore:** View the cover of *Eliza Bird: Child Convict*. Predict the setting and Eliza's feelings. <br>**Connect:** Class brainstorm on what life might have been like in 1788 for a child. | Cover & Blurb | **Support:** Word bank of 18th-century terms. <br>**Core:** Contribute to map activity. <br>**Extend:** Research why children were transported. | Novel, World Map |
| **1** | **1** | **2** | Identify the orientation of a narrative and its key components. | **Introduction:** Define 'Orientation'. <br>**Explore:** Read the first section. Identify characters (Eliza) and setting (Sydney Cove). <br>**Connect:** Students draw Eliza based on the author's description and labels. | Pages 1-4 | **Support:** Drawing template with labels. <br>**Core:** Label 3 character features. <br>**Extend:** Identify how illustrations establish mood. | Novel, Sketchbooks |
| **1** | **1** | **3** | Explore how nouns and adjectives create vivid settings. | **Introduction:** Review noun groups. <br>**Explore:** Re-read descriptions of the convict ship and the new settlement. <br>**Connect:** List noun groups from the text (e.g., 'rusty chains', 'rugged coastline'). | Pages 5-8 | **Support:** Highlight noun groups in text. <br>**Core:** List 5 noun groups. <br>**Extend:** Write a snapshot using original noun groups. | Novel, Highlighters |
| **1** | **1** | **4** | Recognise the purpose and audience of historical fiction. | **Introduction:** Explain 'Historical Fiction' vs 'Non-fiction'. <br>**Explore:** Discuss why Kerri Lane wrote this story. Who is it for? <br>**Connect:** Write a short 'Reason for Reading' - what they hope to learn about the past through Eliza. | Pages 9-12 | **Support:** Visual guide to history/fiction. <br>**Core:** Identify 2 historical facts. <br>**Extend:** Evaluate the effectiveness of fiction. | Novel |
| **2** | **1** | **5** | Identify the initial complication in a narrative. | **Introduction:** Define 'Complication'. <br>**Explore:** Read about Eliza's first night or major struggle. <br>**Connect:** Plot Eliza's emotions on a 'Tension Graph'. | Pages 13-16 | **Support:** Emoji bank for tension graph. <br>**Core:** Plot 3 key emotional points. <br>**Extend:** Predict how the complication might escalate. | Novel, Tension Graph Template |
| **2** | **1** | **6** | Understand how verbs represent different processes (doing, feeling). | **Introduction:** Review verb types (Action vs Sensation). <br>**Explore:** Find examples of what Eliza *does* vs what she *feels*. <br>**Connect:** Sort verbs from pages 17-20 into categories. | Pages 17-20 | **Support:** Cloze activity with verbs. <br>**Core:** Categorise 'doing' and 'feeling' verbs. <br>**Extend:** Write a diary entry using 'thinking' verbs. | Novel, Sorting Sheet |
| **2** | **1** | **7** | Explain how image and text work together to convey meaning. | **Introduction:** Discuss Rachel Tonkin's illustrations. <br>**Explore:** Look at a specific full-page illustration. How does it add to the text? <br>**Connect:** Identify details in the art not mentioned in the words. | Pages 21-24 | **Support:** Point-and-name visual items. <br>**Core:** Find 2 details not in the text. <br>**Extend:** Analyze salience and framing in the images. | Novel |
| **2** | **1** | **8** | Discuss connections between personal experiences and characters. | **Introduction:** Use [AC9E3LE02] to connect. <br>**Explore:** Discuss Eliza's separation from family. Have students ever felt lonely or out of place? <br>**Connect:** Venn Diagram - Eliza vs Me. | Pages 25-28 | **Support:** Prompted scenario cards. <br>**Core:** Complete Venn Diagram. <br>**Extend:** Write a letter of advice to Eliza. | Novel, Venn Diagram |
| **3** | **2** | **9** | Identify and use subjective language to express opinions. | **Introduction:** Define 'Subjective' (opinions/feelings) vs 'Objective'. <br>**Explore:** Find Eliza's opinions of the guards vs the factual description. <br>**Connect:** Rewrite a factual sentence to make it subjective. | Pages 29-32 | **Support:** Match feelings to characters. <br>**Core:** Match subjective words to characters. <br>**Extend:** Debate if Eliza's view is 'fair'. | Novel, Whiteboards |
| **3** | **2** | **10** | Understand how tense anchors a story in time. | **Introduction:** Review Past, Present, Future tense. <br>**Explore:** Confirm most historical fiction is written in Past Tense. <br>**Connect:** Practice converting 'Eliza is hungry' to 'Eliza was hungry'. | Pages 33-36 | **Support:** Simple action verb practice. <br>**Core:** Convert 5 present-tense sentences. <br>**Extend:** Discuss why an author might switch tenses. | Novel, Verb Tense Sheet |
| **3** | **2** | **11** | Describe how plot tensions are developed through events. | **Introduction:** Revisit the tension graph. <br>**Explore:** Read the 'climax' of a mini-conflict within the story. <br>**Connect:** Write a 'Cliffhanger' ending for one of the chapters. | Pages 37-40 | **Support:** Sentence starter for cliffhanger. <br>**Core:** Write a cliffhanger ending. <br>**Extend:** Identify words that create 'Pace' in action. | Novel |
| **3** | **2** | **12** | Use interaction skills to discuss character motivations. | **Introduction:** Group Discussion Rules. <br>**Explore:** Why does Eliza make 'that' choice? (e.g., sharing food, keeping a secret). <br>**Connect:** 'Think-Pair-Share' on Eliza's bravery. | Pages 41-44 | **Support:** Discussion sentence stems. <br>**Core:** Contribute one idea to discussion. <br>**Extend:** Link to another student's opinion. | Novel |
| **4** | **2** | **13** | Investigate how quoted (direct) speech is used. | **Introduction:** Rules for 'Speaking Marks' [AC9E4LA12]. <br>**Explore:** Locate dialogue between Eliza and another character. <br>**Connect:** Write a 3-line dialogue using correct punctuation. | Pages 45-48 | **Support:** Speech bubble templates. <br>**Core:** Punctuate 3 lines of dialogue. <br>**Extend:** Use 'reported' speech to summarise. | Novel, Dialogue Templates |
| **4** | **2** | **14** | Identify character stage: The 'Turning Point'. | **Introduction:** Discuss narrative structure (Rising Action). <br>**Explore:** Identify a moment where Eliza's situation changes for better/worse. <br>**Connect:** Draft a 'Turning Point' paragraph for their own story. | Pages 49-52 | **Support:** Guided paragraph draft. <br>**Core:** Draft a turning point paragraph. <br>**Extend:** Use complex sentences for causality. | Novel |
| **4** | **2** | **15** | Expand vocabulary with synonyms for emotions. | **Introduction:** 'Sad' is boring. What else? <br>**Explore:** Find words for sadness/fear in the text (e.g., desolate, quaking). <br>**Connect:** Create a 'Shades of Meaning' emotive word wall. | Pages 53-56 | **Support:** Focus on 3 key synonyms. <br>**Core:** Add 5 synonyms to the word wall. <br>**Extend:** Explain nuances between synonyms. | Novel, Word Wall |
| **4** | **2** | **16** | Summarise key events while maintaining the context. | **Introduction:** Define 'Summary'. <br>**Explore:** Summarise the story so far in 3 sentences. <br>**Connect:** Peer check - did they include modern things by mistake? | Pages 57-60 | **Support:** Scaffolded summary steps. <br>**Core:** Summarise using text connectives. <br>**Extend:** Write a 'Blurb' to encourage readers. | Novel |
| **5** | **3** | **17** | Identify and explain similes in the text. | **Introduction:** Like a... As a... <br>**Explore:** Find similes used to describe the Australian landscape. <br>**Connect:** Complete the simile: "The heat was like..." | Pages 61-64 | **Support:** List of similes from the text. <br>**Core:** Identify 3 similes and explain them. <br>**Extend:** Explain why the author used that comparison. | Novel |
| **5** | **3** | **18** | Understand how adverb groups provide circumstantial detail. | **Introduction:** Where? When? How? <br>**Explore:** Look at how Eliza moves (e.g., 'With a heavy heart', 'Under the moonlight'). <br>**Connect:** Add an adverb group to a basic sentence. | Pages 65-68 | **Support:** Multiple choice for adverb groups. <br>**Core:** Identify 'How' in a sentence. <br>**Extend:** Use 'When' and 'Where' in one sentence. | Novel |
| **5** | **3** | **19** | Compare depictions of events in historical vs modern texts. | **Introduction:** [AC9LY01]. Compare Eliza's ship to a modern cruise. <br>**Explore:** Read a non-fiction snippet about convict ships. <br>**Connect:** T-Chart - Fact vs Fiction. | Snippet & Novel | **Support:** Sorting facts on a T-Chart. <br>**Core:** Find 1 similarity and 1 difference. <br>**Extend:** Discuss how fiction 'humanises' history. | Novel, Non-fiction Snippet |
| **5** | **3** | **20** | Use paragraphs to group related information. | **Introduction:** Paragraph = One main idea. <br>**Explore:** Look at Page 69. Why did the paragraph change? <br>**Connect:** Sort a jumbled 3-paragraph text. | Pages 69-72 | **Support:** Sort 2 paragraphs. <br>**Core:** Identify paragraph main ideas. <br>**Extend:** Write a vivid 'colony' paragraph. | Novel, Jumbled Text |
| **6** | **3** | **21** | Identify metaphors and their impact on meaning. | **Introduction:** Metaphor = A is B. <br>**Explore:** Discern if "The sun was a golden coin" is present in the text style. <br>**Connect:** Describe the Australian bush using a metaphor. | Pages 73-76 | **Support:** Metaphor matching task. <br>**Core:** Differentiate simile vs metaphor. <br>**Extend:** Analyze mood created by metaphors. | Novel |
| **6** | **3** | **22** | Investigate reported (indirect) speech. | **Introduction:** Summary of talk without " ". <br>**Explore:** Find an instance where an adult summarises Eliza's crimes. <br>**Connect:** Change direct speech from C1 into indirect speech. | Pages 77-80 | **Support:** Scaffolded conversion sheet. <br>**Core:** Convert direct to indirect speech. <br>**Extend:** Discuss irony in reported speech. | Novel |
| **6** | **3** | **23** | Explore prepositional phrases for setting detail. | **Introduction:** Position words (In, on, at, through). <br>**Explore:** Map Eliza's hut based on prepositional descriptions. <br>**Connect:** Write directions for Eliza to find the freshwater spring. | Pages 81-84 | **Support:** Word bank of prepositions. <br>**Core:** Draw hut from text details. <br>**Extend:** Use complex sentences with prepositions. | Novel, Map Template |
| **6** | **3** | **24** | Review: Character change and growth. | **Introduction:** How has Eliza changed since Page 1? <br>**Explore:** Re-read the Orientation vs the current chapter. <br>**Connect:** Draw 'Eliza then' vs 'Eliza now' with character trait labels. | Orientation vs Current | **Support:** Select traits from a list. <br>**Core:** Identify 2 inner growth changes. <br>**Extend:** Justify changes using evidence. | Novel |
| **7** | **4** | **25** | Identify the Resolution of the core narrative. | **Introduction:** How do stories end? <br>**Explore:** Read the final chapters of *Eliza Bird*. <br>**Connect:** Discuss - was it a 'happy' ending?
