# Iron Man: A Hero in Armour

**Reading Level:** Green  
**Word Count:** 134 words  

---

Tony Stark is a famous inventor (a person who makes new machines). He is very clever with metal and electricity. His company made weapons (tools made for fighting). Tony was rich, but he was not always careful.

Then Tony was captured (taken and held against his will). He was hurt in the chest. Another prisoner helped him stay alive. Together they built a metal suit. The suit had a small reactor (a tiny machine that makes power). Tony used the armour to escape. The first suit was heavy, but it worked.

Tony went home and changed his life. He stopped making weapons for war. He built a better suit of armour. He became Iron Man, a hero who helps people. He uses his inventions to protect others. Now he fights to keep people safe.

---

## Key Vocabulary

- **Inventor**: A person who makes new machines
- **Weapons**: Tools made for fighting
- **Captured**: Taken and held against his will
- **Reactor**: A tiny machine that makes power
