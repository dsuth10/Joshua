# Tony Stark: From Inventor to Iron Man

**Reading Level:** Blue  
**Word Count:** 219 words  

---

Tony Stark is a brilliant inventor (a person who designs new machines and tools). He ran a huge company that made weapons for armies. Tony was famous, wealthy, and proud of his clever inventions, and for a long time he did not think about the harm those weapons could cause.

Then Tony was captured (taken and held against his will) while travelling overseas. He was badly hurt in the chest. Another prisoner, a kind doctor, helped keep him alive. The captors wanted Tony to build a powerful weapon. Instead, Tony and the doctor secretly built a metal suit of armour. The suit used a small reactor (a compact machine that makes electric power). The first armour was heavy and clumsy, but it was strong enough, and Tony escaped in the suit. Yet his friend did not survive.

Tony returned home and changed his purpose. He stopped selling weapons to armies. He built a stronger Iron Man suit to protect people, not to start wars. The glowing reactor in his chest kept him alive and powered the armour. Tony tested each new suit with great care, and he became Iron Man, a hero who uses science and engineering to defend others. His greatest invention was no longer a weapon for sale. It was a suit built to keep people safe.

---

## Key Vocabulary

- **Inventor**: A person who designs new machines and tools
- **Captured**: Taken and held against his will
- **Reactor**: A compact machine that makes electric power
