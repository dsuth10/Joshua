# The Origin of Iron Man: Tony Stark's Choice

**Reading Level:** Red  
**Word Count:** 257 words  

---

Tony Stark is a brilliant inventor (a person who designs new machines and tools). He ran Stark Industries, a company famous for making advanced weapons. Tony was known for his genius, his wealth, and his bold public shows. For years he gave little thought to the harm those weapons caused far from home.

Then Tony was captured (taken and held against his will) during a journey overseas. An explosion left metal close to his heart. Another prisoner, a skilled doctor, helped keep him alive. Their captors demanded that Tony build a terrible weapon. Instead, the two men secretly built a heavy suit of armour. A small reactor (a compact machine that produces electricity) kept Tony alive and powered the suit. The first armour was crude, but it was strong enough to break him free. Tony escaped, but his friend did not survive.

Tony returned home determined to change. He stopped his company from making weapons. He rebuilt the armour into the Iron Man suit. The chest reactor kept him alive and also powered every flight and shield. Tony studied each failure and improved the next design. The finished armour could fly, lift heavy loads, and fire beams of energy. He painted the suit red and gold so people could recognise the hero. Cities called for help when machines or villains caused danger. Iron Man answered those calls himself. Tony Stark became Iron Man, a hero who uses engineering to protect people. He would no longer profit from war. He would use his inventions to defend others instead.

## Key Vocabulary

- **Inventor**: A person who designs new machines and tools
- **Captured**: Taken and held against his will
- **Reactor**: A compact machine that produces electricity
