# The Origin of Storm: Ororo Munroe's Choice

**Reading Level:** Red  
**Word Count:** 253 words  

---

Storm is one of Marvel's most famous mutant heroes (people born with unusual powers). Her real name is Ororo Munroe. She was born in New York to a Kenyan princess and an American photojournalist. The family later lived in Cairo (the capital city of Egypt). When a disaster destroyed their home, Ororo's parents died, and she was trapped in the rubble. That fear of tight spaces stayed with her for life.

Ororo survived on the streets of Cairo as a child thief. Later she walked south across Africa. In the open grasslands her mutant weather power grew immense. She could summon wind, rain, thunder, and lightning. Local people honoured her as a rain goddess (a worshipped figure believed to control the sky). For a time she accepted that role and brought storms to help the land. She learned how dangerous uncontrolled weather could be, so she practised until her control became precise.

Professor Charles Xavier then invited Ororo to join the X-Men, a team of mutants who protect both mutants and ordinary people. She chose the name Storm and left goddess worship behind. She became a leader who uses the weather to defend others. She has striking white hair. Lightning often answers her call. Friends trust her to stay calm when the sky turns wild. Storm's origin is a story of loss, survival, and the choice to serve people rather than be worshipped. In later years she also fought beside other Marvel heroes when the world needed her. Storm still protects people today.
