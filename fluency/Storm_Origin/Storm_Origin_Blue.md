# Ororo Munroe: From Cairo to Storm

**Reading Level:** Blue  
**Word Count:** 205 words  

---

Storm is a Marvel hero who can command the weather. Her real name is Ororo Munroe. She was born in New York to a Kenyan mother and an American father. As a child she lived in Cairo (a large city in Egypt), and her early years were full of travel and change.

Then her parents died, and young Ororo was left alone. She survived on the city streets by using speed, skill, and courage. Later she travelled into the wide lands of Africa. There her mutant power (a special ability she was born with) grew much stronger. She could summon wind, rain, and lightning. Village people honoured her as a goddess (a powerful being from old stories) because she could bring rain to dry land. Storm learned to guide the sky with care, and she used that gift to help farmers and travellers.

Professor Xavier asked Ororo to join his team of mutant heroes, the X-Men. She chose the name Storm. She left her old life behind and used her weather power to protect people, not to be worshipped. Storm became a leader who fights for those who cannot protect themselves. She has striking white hair. Her storms made her easy to recognise in battle.

## Key Vocabulary

- **Cairo**: A large city in Egypt
- **Mutant power**: A special ability she was born with
- **Goddess**: A powerful being from old stories
