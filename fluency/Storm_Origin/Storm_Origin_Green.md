# Storm: A Hero of Weather

**Reading Level:** Green  
**Word Count:** 120 words  

---

Storm is a hero who can control the weather. Her real name is Ororo Munroe. She was born in New York. As a child she lived in Cairo (a big city in Egypt).

Then her parents died. Ororo was left alone. She had to look after herself on the streets. Later she travelled across Africa. Her mutant power (a special power she was born with) grew strong. She could call wind, rain, and lightning. Some people thought she was a goddess (a powerful being from old stories). She brought rain to dry land.

A teacher named Professor Xavier asked her to join a team. Ororo chose a new name. She became Storm. Now she uses the weather to protect people.

---

## Key Vocabulary

- **Cairo**: A big city in Egypt
- **Mutant power**: A special power she was born with
- **Goddess**: A powerful being from old stories
