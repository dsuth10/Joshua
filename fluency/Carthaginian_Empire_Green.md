# Carthage: A City by the Sea

**Reading Level:** Green  
**Word Count:** 134 words  

---

The Carthaginian Empire was a strong city by the sea. Carthage sat in North Africa (the land across the sea from Europe). The people of Carthage were expert sailors, and they built many ships. The Carthaginian navy (a group of warships) protected the traders.

Carthage grew rich from trade (the buying and selling of goods). Ships carried metal, grain, and cloth across the Mediterranean Sea. The city had a busy harbour (a safe place for ships to rest). Carthage made purple cloth that many people wanted.

Rome was a rival (a strong city that wanted the same power). A general named Hannibal led a huge army over the mountains. He took war elephants on that dangerous journey. Rome and Carthage fought long wars. In the end, Rome won, and the city of Carthage fell.

---

## Key Vocabulary

- **North Africa**: The land across the sea from Europe
- **Navy**: A group of warships
- **Trade**: The buying and selling of goods
- **Harbour**: A safe place for ships to rest
- **Rival**: A strong city that wanted the same power
