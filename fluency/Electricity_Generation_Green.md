# Electricity: Sun, Wind, and Coal

**Reading Level:** Green  
**Word Count:** 131 words  

---

We use power in our homes and classrooms every day. Power gives us light and heat. We make power in three ways.

First is sun power. It is called solar power (energy made from sunlight). Dark panels sit on roofs. They catch the warm sun. Solar power is clean and makes no smoke. But panels cannot make power at night.

Next is wind power from moving air. Big wind turbines (tall towers with spinning blades) turn fast in the breeze. Wind power is clean and renewable. However, the turbines stop when the air is still.

Last is coal, which comes from deep underground. Coal (a black rock dug from the earth) is burned in stations. Coal makes steady power day and night. But burning coal makes black smoke and air pollution.

---

## Key Vocabulary

- **Solar power**: Energy made from sunlight
- **Wind turbines**: Tall towers with spinning blades
- **Coal**: A black rock dug from the earth
